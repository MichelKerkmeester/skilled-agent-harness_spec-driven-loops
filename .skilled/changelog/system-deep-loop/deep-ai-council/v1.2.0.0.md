# v1.2.0.0, AI Council Gets Its Own Identity

v1.2 gives the AI Council an identity. That identity reflects its planning role, not its link to autonomous-loop work. The skill `deep-ai-council` changes families. Its agent becomes `@ai-council`.

## Why This Release

The Council is a planning sentinel. It sits closer to `sk-prompt` than to `deep-research` and `deep-review`. Those two iterate to convergence.

## What's New at a Glance

- **The skill `deep-ai-council` changes families.** Its prior family was `deep-loop`.
- **The agent is now `@ai-council`.** Its prior name was `@deep-ai-council`.
- **Council behavior is unchanged.** Its trigger phrases and MCP wiring are unchanged.
- **Historical records preserve the prior identity.** Earlier changelogs and the originating packet retain their original state.

---

## Council Identity

#### A Planning Sentinel's Place

Its graph metadata updates `skill_id`, entities and key_files. It moves its family from `deep-loop` to `sk-util`. Its category changes from `autonomous-loop` to `utility`. The regenerated compiled skill graph holds 22 skills in 6 families. `deep-ai-council` now sits in the `sk-util` family. It sits alongside `sk-prompt-small-model`, `sk-doc`, `sk-git`, `sk-prompt` and `deep-agent-improvement`. The `deep-loop` family is now strictly `deep-research` and `deep-review`.

&nbsp;

#### The Agent's Own Name

The agent drops the `deep-` prefix. That is because its identity is AI Council, not Deep Anything. Agent names omit the `sk-` family prefix. Examples include `@code`, `@debug`, `@review`, `@context`, `@markdown` and `@orchestrate`. The agent was renamed across all three runtime mirrors. Three `orchestrate.md`/`.toml` agent files serve as delegation targets. Those files and `.codex/config.toml` were updated to the new agent name.

---

## Unchanged Behavior

#### The Council Keeps Its Logic

- The council graph projection is unchanged.
- The deliberation logic is unchanged.

---

## Historical Provenance

#### Prior Names Stay Documented

The `v1.0.0.0.md` and `v1.1.0.0.md` changelogs preserve historical versions under the original name. The originating packet remains unchanged. Historical references in past packets, evidence captures and frozen benchmark fixtures preserve the prior-name state.

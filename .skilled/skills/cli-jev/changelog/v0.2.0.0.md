# v0.2.0.0, cli-jev Joins the Compiled Serving Closure

The `cli-jev` hub now serves its compiled route when `SPECKIT_COMPILED_ROUTING` is unset.

## What's New at a Glance

- **A compiled-routing shadow child covers `cli-jev`.** `009-parent-hub-rollout/008-cli-jev` compiles `SKILL.md`, `hub-router.json` and `mode-registry.json` into a policy. It includes its own registry compiler, a router that replays a request against the compiled contract, a policy card and a `fixtures/canary-cases.v1.json` corpus of seven cases: four judgment requests that must route and three that must not.
- **Generation 1 activates compiled serving.** `013-live-activation/activation/cli-jev/manifest.json` pins the compiled policy hash of this hub's routing inputs and declares `servingAuthority: "compiled"`. `fence-state.json` records the first activation flip (epoch 1), and the authored mirror under the router-unification program carries the pre-activation manifest, the rollback copy and both records.
- **The hub joins the default-on cohort.** It is registered in the engine's dispatch map and in all cohort copies, so `compiled-route.cjs --hub cli-jev --prompt "<task>"` returns a compiled decision rather than the legacy sentinel.
- **The routing files use version `0.2.0.0`.** The version applies across `SKILL.md`, `ROUTER.md`, `hub-router.json`, `mode-registry.json` and `description.json` because the hub's serving state changed.
- **The `SKILL.md` directive now states live behavior.** The removed scaffold paragraph said the directive was inert while the hub had no manifest and named the then-five serving hubs. The directive above it now states the live behavior found in every other serving hub's `SKILL.md`.
- **The hub-level routing corpus resolves under the compiled router.** `CJ-001` asks with the hub-vocabulary phrase `jev judgment`, since its old wording deferred. `CJ-003`'s gold names the no-route label instead of a null mode, since a null gold label is unparseable to the admission check.
- **The transport contract and judgment surface are unchanged.** The transport contract has one `packetKind: "transport"` mode, `mutatesWorkspace: false` and `routingClass: metadata`. `Write`, `Edit` and `Task` are forbidden, and the dispatch guards enforce eight hard rules. The judgment surface, JEV scenario IDs and recorded verdicts inside `cli-usage/` did not change.

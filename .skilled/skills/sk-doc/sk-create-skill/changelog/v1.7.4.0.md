# v1.7.4.0, Long Prose Lines Are Short Sentences

The seven prose lines in the packet `SKILL.md` that ran past 400 characters are now short numbered steps with short sentences under them. The advisor-discovery steps, the metadata contract steps and the `--check` limits read one idea at a time.

The facts did not move. Every command, every path, every metadata key and every limit still appears, and each step keeps its number. No route changed and no behavior changed.

> Spec folder: `specs/sk-doc/072-skill-routing-and-clarity/010-prose-clarity-pass` (Level 2)

&nbsp;

## What's New at a Glance

- **The long lines are split.** The packet `SKILL.md` carries no prose line over 400 characters.
- **The steps read one idea at a time.** Each long step is a lead sentence and the short sentences that follow it, indented so the number still holds them.
- **The templates pass the gate they feed.** `assets/skill/skill-md-template.md`, `assets/parent-skill/parent-skill-hub-template.md` and the three scaffolds under `assets/` carried semicolons and em dashes that the human-voice scan counts as hard blockers, so a new skill could fail the create flow's gate on its first run. Each now scans 0 hard blockers. The compiled-routing directive reads the same in the hub template and the hub scaffold.
- **Nothing else moved.** Every command, path, metadata key and limit reads as it did.

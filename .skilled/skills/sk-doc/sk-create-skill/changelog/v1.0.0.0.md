# v1.0.0.0, The create-skill Workflow Debuts

The initial release gives OpenCode skill authors a dedicated creation workflow in the `sk-doc` parent hub. The `create-skill` packet is one of the hub's ten workflow-only packets and scaffolds, authors and validates OpenCode skill packages under `.opencode/skills/`.

## What's New at a Glance

- **`SKILL.md` is the primary executable workflow contract and selects between two authoring modes.** It includes rules and success criteria. `create-skill` guides standalone skills with their own advisor identity, while `create-skill-parent` guides parent hubs that dispatch to nested workflow or surface packets.
- **`create-skill` scaffolds standalone packages.** It plans resource placement and uses `init_skill.py` to scaffold. It authors `SKILL.md`, `README.md`, `references/` and `assets/` then validates.
- **`create-skill-parent` prepares parent-hub files.** Its workflow covers hub root files and nested-packet shape. It sets rules for `mode-registry.json` and `hub-router.json`.
- **References guide standalone, shared and parent-hub authoring.** `references/` includes standalone workflow guidance in `skill/creation-workflow.md` and examples and maintenance guidance in `skill/examples-and-maintenance.md`. Shared guidance is in `shared/overview.md`, `shared/common-pitfalls.md` and `shared/validation-and-packaging.md`, while parent-hub guidance is in `parent-skill/parent-skills-nested-packets.md` and `parent-skill/parent-hub-router-schema.md`.
- **Copy-from assets provide templates for both modes.** Standalone templates are `skill/skill-md-template.md`, `skill/skill-readme-template.md`, `skill/skill-reference-template.md`, `skill/skill-asset-template.md` and `skill/skill-smart-router.md`. Parent-hub templates are `parent-skill/parent-skill-hub-template.md`, `parent-skill/parent-skill-registry-template.json`, `parent-skill/parent-skill-hub-router-template.json`, `parent-skill/parent-skill-description-template.json` and `parent-skill/parent-skill-graph-metadata-template.json`.
- **The `scripts/` directory covers scaffolding and the validation and packaging gate.** `init_skill.py` scaffolds a new standalone skill folder. `package_skill.py` is the validation and packaging gate: `--check` checks structure and frontmatter, while a plain run creates a zip for distribution.
- **The packet has no `graph-metadata.json` of its own.** The single advisor identity and `mode-registry.json` entries for both modes live at the `sk-doc` hub root.

## Upgrade

No migration required.

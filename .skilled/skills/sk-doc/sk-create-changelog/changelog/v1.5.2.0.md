# v1.5.2.0, Release Notes Have No Item Ceilings

The shape check failed an expanded entry past 32 H4 items or 14 glance bullets. The v4.0.2.1 entry sat at both, so a new section meant folding another away. Both ceilings are gone.

&nbsp;

## What's New at a Glance

- No `h4-items` or `glance-bullets` rule.
  - `check_changelog_shape.py` no longer counts either.
- The template keeps its usual counts.
  - 12 to 22 H4 items and 5 to 9 glance bullets stay the target, not a gate.
- The workflow line names only the sentence gates.
  - Both `/create:changelog` files drop the two counts.
- The other gates stay.
  - Glance order, the spec-folder line, How It Was Checked and both sentence rules.

&nbsp;

## Upgrade

No migration required.

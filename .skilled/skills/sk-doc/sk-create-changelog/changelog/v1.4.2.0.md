# v1.4.2.0, Short Sentences Over Sentence Counts

The template capped prose by sentence count. A summary had three sentences at most, and an item had three. Writers met the cap by chaining long sentences, which read badly. Prose limits are now word budgets, and short sentences are the default.

&nbsp;

## What's New at a Glance

- **Prose limits count words.** A summary or an item runs about 60 words, in as many short sentences as it needs.
- **Short sentences are the rule.** The voice list asks for about 10-15 words a sentence, split rather than cut.
- **An item can take two paragraphs.** A second paragraph no longer needs a breaking change as its reason.
- **Budgets guide, they do not gate.** The shape check still enforces only file length, item count, glance count and glance order.

&nbsp;

## Upgrade

No migration required. An entry written to the old counts still passes.

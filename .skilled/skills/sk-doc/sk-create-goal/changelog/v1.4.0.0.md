The goal-criteria lint can now be measured against a model. `score-goal-lint.cjs` gains two opt-in arms that re-judge every labeled criterion and print a keep-or-kill verdict per rule, and the label sample that makes the measurement real is now filled.

## 2026-10-01

> Spec folder: `specs/cli-jev/003-cli-jev-workflow-integration/043-label-finding-fixes` (Level 2)

## What's New at a Glance

- **Two opt-in model arms.** `--jev` and `--deem` each need `--out <dir>` and ask the model two questions per labeled criterion, three reruns each for Jev and one pass for Deem. Every call lands in `calls.jsonl` and the columns land in `report.json` under `--out`.
- **A strict keep rule.** A rule keeps its model column only when the arm beats the lint's F1 by at least 0.2, holds precision of at least 0.8 and, for Jev, flips its answer on at most a tenth of the questions. Anything less kills the column, and a rule with fewer than nine in ten rows measured stops on coverage instead of getting a verdict.
- **The labels are in.** `goal-criteria-labels.jsonl` now carries 98 labeled rows under rubric `mimo-02-strict-v1`, marked by an operator-delegated arbiter, and 2 stale rows stay null.
- **The first real score.** The lint reads F1 0.83 on rule 4 but catches only one of the 75 criteria the arbiter marked as rule-5 violations. That gap is what the model arm exists to close.
- **Nothing changes by default.** Without `--jev` or `--deem` the scorer prints the same lines as before and writes nothing.

## Upgrade

No migration required. Run `node .skilled/skills/sk-doc/sk-create-goal/scripts/score-goal-lint.cjs --labels .skilled/skills/sk-doc/sk-create-goal/scripts/goal-criteria-labels.jsonl --jev --out <dir>` to measure the Jev arm.

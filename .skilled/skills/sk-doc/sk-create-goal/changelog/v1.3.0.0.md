Rules 4 and 5 of this mode ask for completion criteria that are self-contained and checkable without opening another file. Until now nothing checked them, so a criterion that points at "the report" or "every row in the spec" could reach the objective and leave completion open. This release adds an advisory lint that flags those lines.

> Spec folder: `specs/cli-jev/003-cli-jev-workflow-integration/006-goal-criteria-lint` (Level 1)

## What's New at a Glance

- **An advisory lint for criteria.** `lint-goal-criteria.cjs` reads every active goal, or one packet, and flags two kinds of line: a reference such as "the report" that the line itself never names, and wording such as "as described in" whose check needs another document. It makes no model call and always exits 0.
- **Fixture goals stay out of the count.** Goals under a `scratch` folder are fixtures, not authored criteria, so the lint skips them and prints how many it skipped. Archived goals stay out as before.
- **The checker is unchanged.** `check-goal.cjs` keeps its five checks, its output and its exit codes. The lint is a separate script and blocks nothing.

## Upgrade

No migration required. Run `node .skilled/skills/sk-doc/sk-create-goal/scripts/lint-goal-criteria.cjs <packet>` to see the flagged lines for one goal.

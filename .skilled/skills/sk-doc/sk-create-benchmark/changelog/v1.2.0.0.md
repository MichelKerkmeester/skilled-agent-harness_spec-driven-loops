# v1.2.0.0, Two New Benchmark Families and Normalized SKILL.md Sections

`create-benchmark` now authors four benchmark families. That is up from two. The MCP-promotion family (`mcp-server/benchmarks/benchmark-<YYYY-MM-DD>/`) and the `behavior_benchmark` family are unchanged in contract. `skill-benchmark` and `model-benchmark` are the two additions. The release normalizes the `SKILL.md` section structure to the canonical sk-doc shape.

The new Lane C and Lane B materials define the authored families. They point to the lane-local contracts for scoring and evaluation. The `SKILL.md` summaries now cover the two added families. Its routing table covers all six families. That includes the two code-owned non-goals.

## Why This Release

Before this release, the packet failed `package_skill.py --check`. It was missing its `SMART ROUTING` and `REFERENCES` sections.

---

## What's New at a Glance

- **This packet owns skill-benchmark storage and its run-label index.** The hub's `benchmark/` tree and `benchmark/README.md` index now have a guide and template.
- **This packet owns model-benchmark fixtures and run profiles.** The model under test answers the fixtures. The profiles drive a run.
- **Lane A appears in Smart Routing only for disambiguation.** Its artifacts are code-owned. They run entirely in-lane.
- **`SKILL.md` sections 10–12 cover both additions.** The family summaries and references point to guides and lane-owned contracts.
- **Smart Routing now separates `WHEN TO USE` from `SMART ROUTING`.** Its family table distinguishes ownership from routing. It covers all six families.
- **The section numbers and cross-file references follow the new structure.** MCP-promotion moves to sections 3–8. `behavior_benchmark` moves to section 9.

---

## Benchmark Authoring

#### Skill-Benchmark Separates Runs and Reports

This packet owns the storage-and-naming standard for a hub's `benchmark/` tree. It also owns the template for its `benchmark/README.md` run-label index. `references/skill-benchmark/skill-benchmark-storage-guide.md` defines sibling run-label folders. It defines the frozen `baseline/` anchor and what lands in each run-label directory. `assets/skill-benchmark/skill-benchmark-readme-template.md` is a fillable scaffold. It covers the index of runs only.

The per-run `skill-benchmark-report.md` is a `build-report.cjs` anti-drift render. It is never hand-authored or templated. The D1-D5 scoring contract stays lane-local in `deep-improvement`. It is cross-linked, never restated.

&nbsp;

#### Model-Benchmark Fixtures Define Their Inputs

This packet owns model-benchmark input fixtures the model under test answers. They are a code-task oracle, a pattern/capability evidence contract and a reviewer-prompt. `references/model-benchmark/model-benchmark-fixture-guide.md` covers three fixture families and their shape markers. It covers the profile shape and sweep keys. It covers the reviewer-fixture shape at a glance and the lane boundary for scoring.

&nbsp;

#### Templates Define Fixture Forms

`assets/model-benchmark/model-benchmark-code-task-fixture-template.md` scaffolds one tiered code-task oracle fixture. It carries `fn_name`, `tests[]` and `hidden_tests[]`. Oracle values come from a verified reference implementation. `assets/model-benchmark/model-benchmark-pattern-fixture-template.md` scaffolds the two non-code-task shapes. They are the pattern evidence-contract fixture and the reviewer-prompt capability fixture.

&nbsp;

#### Profiles Keep Evaluation Lane-Local

This packet owns the run profiles that drive a run. `assets/model-benchmark/model-benchmark-profile-template.md` scaffolds a Lane B run profile. It carries fixtures, models, frameworks, scoring, sampling and gate. It is authored against the lane's profile validator. The evaluator, scorers and reviewer-verdict contract stay lane-local in `deep-improvement`. They are cross-linked.

&nbsp;

#### Artifacts Stay in Their Owning Lane

Skill-benchmark and model-benchmark artifacts are authored into their owning lane, `system-deep-loop`. They are not authored into this packet. This packet ships only the templates and guides for those two families. Lane A (`agent-improvement`) is named in Smart Routing only for disambiguation. Its artifacts are code-owned and run entirely in-lane. So this packet ships no template, guide or index for Lane A.

## SKILL.md Structure and Routing

#### Family Guidance Lives in SKILL.md

`SKILL.md` sections 10 and 11 give in-contract summaries of the two new families. Each mirrors section 9. They cover what it is, package and artifact shape, template map and authoring workflow. They also cover ALWAYS/NEVER, including renderer-owned and lane-local boundaries and success criteria. Section 12 adds a REFERENCES H2. It routes to the packet's family guides and lane-owned normative contracts. Those are the behavior framework, Lane C scoring contract and renderer. They also include the Lane B evaluator and reviewer schema.

&nbsp;

#### Smart Routing Distinguishes Ownership

The merged `## 1. WHEN TO USE + SMART_ROUTING` header became `## 1. WHEN TO USE` and `## 2. SMART ROUTING`. The Benchmark Families table and the routing and decision content moved under SMART ROUTING. Its new OWNS-vs-ROUTES column covers all six families. Those are four authored families and two code-owned non-goals.

&nbsp;

#### Section Numbers Follow the New Order

All H2s were renumbered contiguously. MCP-promotion sections 2 through 7 became sections 3 through 8. The `behavior_benchmark` section 8 became section 9. Intra-file `section N` cross-references were updated to match. The packet now passes `package_skill.py --check`.

&nbsp;

#### Cross-File References Match Sections

Two cross-file references were updated after the renumbering. `references/behavior-benchmark/behavior-benchmark-guide.md` now points to MCP-promotion sections 3 through 8. It pointed to sections 2 through 7 before. `assets/shared/benchmark-report-template.md` identifies the report contract as Section 5. It said Section 4 before.

&nbsp;

#### Version Metadata Covers Four Families

The `version` changed from 1.1.0.0 to 1.2.0.0. The description and Keywords comment were both updated to cover all four authored families.

&nbsp;

#### Advisor Identity Lives at the Hub

This packet has no packet-local `graph-metadata.json`. It has no bound `/create:` slash command. The single advisor identity lives at the `sk-doc` hub root.

## Known Pre-Existing Issues (not changed here)

#### Packaging Warnings Are Advisory

`package_skill.py --check` still emits advisory warnings (non-blocking). The packet has no recommended `INTEGRATION POINTS` or `RELATED RESOURCES` sections. `SKILL.md` exceeds the recommended 3000-word soft cap. It is under the 5000-word hard cap. `SMART ROUTING` does not carry the `discover_markdown_resources`, `_guard_in_skill` or `UNKNOWN_FALLBACK` pseudocode markers. This is expected. The prose-routed workflow packet has no machine router. So canonical router pseudocode does not apply.

&nbsp;

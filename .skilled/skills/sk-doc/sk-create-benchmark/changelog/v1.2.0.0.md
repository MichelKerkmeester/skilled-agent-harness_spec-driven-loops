# v1.2.0.0, Two New Benchmark Families and Normalized SKILL.md Sections

`create-benchmark` now authors four benchmark families.

- That is up from two.
- `skill-benchmark` and `model-benchmark` are the two additions.
- MCP-promotion (`mcp-server/benchmarks/benchmark-<YYYY-MM-DD>/`) and `behavior_benchmark` are unchanged in contract.

The release normalizes the `SKILL.md` section structure to the canonical sk-doc shape.

- The `SKILL.md` summaries now cover the two added families.
- Its routing table covers all six families.
- That includes the two code-owned non-goals.

New materials define the authored families.

- The new Lane C and Lane B materials define the authored families.
- They point to the lane-local contracts for scoring and evaluation.

&nbsp;

## Why This Release

The release closes a packaging failure.

- Before this release, the packet failed `package_skill.py --check`.
- It was missing its `SMART ROUTING` and `REFERENCES` sections.

## Benchmark Authoring

This packet ships only templates and guides for the two added families.

#### Skill-Benchmark Separates Runs and Reports <a id="c-skillbenchmark-separates-runs-and-reports"></a>

- This packet owns the storage-and-naming standard for a hub's `benchmark/` tree.
- It also owns the template for its `benchmark/README.md` run-label index.
- `references/skill-benchmark/skill-benchmark-storage-guide.md` defines sibling run-label folders.
- It defines the frozen `baseline/` anchor and what lands in each run-label directory.
- `assets/skill-benchmark/skill-benchmark-readme-template.md` is a fillable scaffold.
- It covers the index of runs only.
- The per-run `skill-benchmark-report.md` is a `build-report.cjs` anti-drift render.
- It is never hand-authored or templated.
- The D1-D5 scoring contract stays lane-local in `deep-improvement`.
- It is cross-linked, never restated.

#### Model-Benchmark Fixtures Define Their Inputs <a id="c-modelbenchmark-fixtures-define-their-inputs"></a>

- This packet owns model-benchmark input fixtures the model under test answers.
- They are a code-task oracle, a pattern/capability evidence contract and a reviewer-prompt.
- `references/model-benchmark/model-benchmark-fixture-guide.md` covers three fixture families and their shape markers.
- It covers the profile shape and sweep keys.
- It covers the reviewer-fixture shape at a glance and the lane boundary for scoring.

#### Templates Define Fixture Forms <a id="c-templates-define-fixture-forms"></a>

- `assets/model-benchmark/model-benchmark-code-task-fixture-template.md` scaffolds one tiered code-task oracle fixture.
- It carries `fn_name`, `tests[]` and `hidden_tests[]`.
- Oracle values come from a verified reference implementation.
- `assets/model-benchmark/model-benchmark-pattern-fixture-template.md` scaffolds the two non-code-task shapes.
- They are the pattern evidence-contract fixture and the reviewer-prompt capability fixture.

#### Profiles Keep Evaluation Lane-Local <a id="c-profiles-keep-evaluation-lanelocal"></a>

- This packet owns the run profiles that drive a run.
- `assets/model-benchmark/model-benchmark-profile-template.md` scaffolds a Lane B run profile.
- It carries fixtures, models, frameworks, scoring, sampling and gate.
- It is authored against the lane's profile validator.
- The evaluator, scorers and reviewer-verdict contract stay lane-local in `deep-improvement`.
- They are cross-linked.

#### Artifacts Stay in Their Owning Lane <a id="c-artifacts-stay-in-their-owning-lane"></a>

- Skill-benchmark and model-benchmark artifacts are authored into their owning lane, `system-deep-loop`.
- They are not authored into this packet.
- This packet ships only the templates and guides for those two families.
- Lane A (`agent-improvement`) is named in Smart Routing only for disambiguation.
- Its artifacts are code-owned and run entirely in-lane.
- So this packet ships no template, guide or index for Lane A.

## SKILL.md Structure and Routing

The `SKILL.md` section structure now follows the canonical sk-doc shape.

#### Family Guidance Lives in SKILL.md <a id="c-family-guidance-lives-in-skillmd"></a>

- `SKILL.md` sections 10 and 11 give in-contract summaries of the two new families.
- Each mirrors section 9.
- They cover what it is, package and artifact shape, template map and authoring workflow.
- They also cover ALWAYS/NEVER, including renderer-owned and lane-local boundaries and success criteria.
- Section 12 adds a REFERENCES H2.
- It routes to the packet's family guides and lane-owned normative contracts.
- Those are the behavior framework, Lane C scoring contract and renderer.
- They also include the Lane B evaluator and reviewer schema.

#### Smart Routing Distinguishes Ownership <a id="c-smart-routing-distinguishes-ownership"></a>

- The merged `## 1. WHEN TO USE + SMART_ROUTING` header became `## 1. WHEN TO USE` and `## 2. SMART ROUTING`.
- The Benchmark Families table and the routing and decision content moved under SMART ROUTING.
- Its new OWNS-vs-ROUTES column covers all six families.
- Those are four authored families and two code-owned non-goals.

#### Section Numbers Follow the New Order <a id="c-section-numbers-follow-the-new-order"></a>

- All H2s were renumbered contiguously.
- MCP-promotion sections 2 through 7 became sections 3 through 8.
- The `behavior_benchmark` section 8 became section 9.
- Intra-file `section N` cross-references were updated to match.

#### Cross-File References Match Sections <a id="c-crossfile-references-match-sections"></a>

- Two cross-file references were updated after the renumbering.
- `references/behavior-benchmark/behavior-benchmark-guide.md` now points to MCP-promotion sections 3 through 8.
- It pointed to sections 2 through 7 before.
- `assets/shared/benchmark-report-template.md` identifies the report contract as Section 5.
- It said Section 4 before.

#### Version Metadata Covers Four Families <a id="c-version-metadata-covers-four-families"></a>

- The `version` changed from 1.1.0.0 to 1.2.0.0.
- The description and Keywords comment were both updated to cover all four authored families.

#### Advisor Identity Lives at the Hub <a id="c-advisor-identity-lives-at-the-hub"></a>

- This packet has no packet-local `graph-metadata.json`.
- It has no bound `/create:` slash command.
- The single advisor identity lives at the `sk-doc` hub root.

The Human Voice Rules standard moved into the packet that applies it. The packet's supporting material now follows the shapes `sk-create-skill` publishes.

## Why This Release

The shared tier is for files more than one packet needs. One packet applies the Human Voice Rules. `sk-create-quality-control` reaches them through a declared alias. So the shared placement bought a hop and no reuse. This release reverses a decision recorded in `v1.0.0.0`. The `v1.0.0.0` entry said the standard would not move. It cited a headline count of 641 referencing files. But 614 of those are frozen spec documents under `specs/`. They keep their historical path, exactly as every other rename does. That leaves roughly ten live consumers rather than hundreds. The entry stays as written because a changelog records what was decided at the time.

## What's New at a Glance

- **The standard moved to `references/hvr-rules.md`.** It left the hub's shared tier for the packet that applies it. Its body is byte-identical, except for the relative link the move broke.
- **`scripts/hvr_scan.py` resolves the standard one directory up rather than three.** `DEFAULT_RULES_PATH` now walks `parents[1] / "references" / "hvr-rules.md"`. Its fail-closed floors are unchanged. If it cannot parse the standard, it exits 2 rather than reporting a clean scan.
- **Roughly thirty live consumers were repointed.** They include the hub `ROUTER.md` resource map. They include the `leaf-aliases.json` projection that lets `sk-create-quality-control` reach the file. The generated `leaf-manifest.json` and three hub playbook scenarios are on the list. So are `sk-communication`, `/create:with-human-voice` and `/rewrite:*`. The list also names `repo-rules/communication.md`. It names the `sk-create-readme` and `sk-create-skill` templates. Ten spec-kit templates are included too. They emit an HVR reference line into generated spec docs.
- **The references and assets now follow `sk-create-skill`'s published shapes.** Both references and the report asset open on `## 1. OVERVIEW`. `contextType` changed from `reference` to the enum published by the standard. That is because the frontmatter contract does not define `reference`.
- **Frozen surfaces kept their historical paths.** These were not repointed. They are the 614 spec documents under `specs/`. They are the compiled-routing benchmark reports under `sk-doc/benchmark/` and `system-deep-loop/benchmark/`. They are the `v1.0.0.0` entry too.
- **The spec-kit golden snapshot preserved unrelated entries.** It was updated by substituting the one changed line. It was not updated by a `vitest -u` rerun. So no unrelated or obsolete snapshot entry moved with it.

No migration required.

The standard moved into the packet that applies it, and the packet's references and assets
were brought onto the shapes `sk-create-skill` publishes.

> Spec folder: `specs/sk-doc/046-human-voice-standard-ownership` (Level 2)

---

## What Changed

#### Changed

- **The standard now lives at `references/hvr-rules.md`.** It moved out of the hub's
  shared tier, whose meaning is that more than one packet needs a file. One packet applies
  the Human Voice Rules and one more reaches them through a declared alias, so the shared
  placement bought a hop and no reuse. The file's body is byte-identical apart from one
  relative link the move broke.
- **`scripts/hvr_scan.py` resolves the standard one directory up rather than three.**
  `DEFAULT_RULES_PATH` now walks `parents[1] / "references" / "hvr-rules.md"`. Its
  fail-closed floors are unchanged, so a standard it cannot parse still exits 2 rather than
  reporting a clean scan.
- **Roughly thirty live consumers were repointed**, among them the hub `ROUTER.md`
  resource map, the `leaf-aliases.json` projection that lets `sk-create-quality-control`
  reach the file, the generated `leaf-manifest.json`, three hub playbook scenarios, the
  `sk-create-readme` and `sk-create-skill` templates, `sk-communication`, the
  `/create:with-human-voice` and `/rewrite:*` command surfaces, `repo-rules/communication.md`,
  and the ten spec-kit templates that emit an HVR reference line into generated spec docs.
- **The references and assets were conformed to the packet templates.** Both references and
  the report asset now open on `## 1. OVERVIEW`, and `contextType` moved off the value
  `reference`, which the frontmatter contract does not define, onto the enum this standard
  publishes.

#### Notes

- **This reverses a decision recorded in v1.0.0.0.** That entry said the standard would not
  move, on a headline count of 641 referencing files. Measured, 614 of those are frozen
  spec documents that keep their historical path exactly as every other rename does, which
  left roughly ten live consumers rather than hundreds. The v1.0.0.0 entry stays as
  written, because a changelog records what was decided at the time.
- **Frozen surfaces kept the old path**: the 614 spec documents under `specs/`, the
  compiled-routing benchmark reports under `sk-doc/benchmark/` and `system-deep-loop/benchmark/`,
  and the v1.0.0.0 entry above.
- **The spec-kit golden snapshot was updated by substituting the one changed line**, not by
  a `vitest -u` rerun, so no unrelated or obsolete snapshot entry moved with it.

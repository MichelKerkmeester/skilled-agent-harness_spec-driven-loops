The Human Voice Rules standard moved into the packet that applies it, and the packet's supporting material now follows the shapes `sk-create-skill` publishes.

> Spec folder: `specs/sk-doc/046-human-voice-standard-ownership` (Level 2)

## Why This Release

The shared tier is for files more than one packet needs. One packet applies the Human Voice Rules, while `sk-create-quality-control` reaches them through a declared alias, so the shared placement bought a hop and no reuse. This release reverses a decision recorded in `v1.0.0.0`. The `v1.0.0.0` entry said the standard would not move based on a headline count of 641 referencing files, but 614 of those are frozen spec documents under `specs/` that keep their historical path exactly as every other rename does, leaving roughly ten live consumers rather than hundreds. The entry stays as written because a changelog records what was decided at the time.

## What's New at a Glance

- **The standard moved to `references/hvr-rules.md`.** It left the hub's shared tier for the packet that applies it. Its body is byte-identical except for the relative link the move broke.
- **`scripts/hvr_scan.py` resolves the standard one directory up rather than three.** `DEFAULT_RULES_PATH` now walks `parents[1] / "references" / "hvr-rules.md"`. Its fail-closed floors are unchanged: if it cannot parse the standard, it exits 2 rather than reporting a clean scan.
- **Roughly thirty live consumers were repointed.** They include the hub `ROUTER.md` resource map, the `leaf-aliases.json` projection that lets `sk-create-quality-control` reach the file, the generated `leaf-manifest.json`, three hub playbook scenarios, `sk-communication`, `/create:with-human-voice` and `/rewrite:*`, `repo-rules/communication.md`, the `sk-create-readme` and `sk-create-skill` templates and ten spec-kit templates that emit an HVR reference line into generated spec docs.
- **The references and assets now follow `sk-create-skill`'s published shapes.** Both references and the report asset open on `## 1. OVERVIEW`. `contextType` changed from `reference`, which the frontmatter contract does not define, to the enum published by the standard.
- **Frozen surfaces kept their historical paths.** The 614 spec documents under `specs/`, compiled-routing benchmark reports under `sk-doc/benchmark/` and `system-deep-loop/benchmark/` and the `v1.0.0.0` entry were not repointed.
- **The spec-kit golden snapshot preserved unrelated entries.** It was updated by substituting the one changed line, not by a `vitest -u` rerun, so no unrelated or obsolete snapshot entry moved with it.

## Upgrade

No migration required.

# v1.9.15.0, The Efficiency Gates Come Before Related Resources

A review now reads its two efficiency gates before it scopes anything, and RELATED RESOURCES sits last where the skill template puts it. The PR-state reference gained the numbering, intro and orientation the rest of the packet already had.

> Spec folder: `specs/sk-code/018-frontend-upstream-proposals/010-reference-readability-research` (Level 2)

&nbsp;

## Why This Release

The M-1 and M-2 gates decide whether a review runs at all, yet Phase 1 never pointed at them and they sat after RELATED RESOURCES, so a skip could surface only after the work it was meant to prevent. The PR-state reference also had no intro and seven unnumbered peers, which left a reader unable to cite a step and unable to tell which section governed.

&nbsp;

## What's New at a Glance

- **The gates move to section 8.** PR-STATE EFFICIENCY GATES becomes section 8 with subsections 8.1 to 8.3, and RELATED RESOURCES becomes section 9.
- **Phase 1 checks the gates first.** Its first step names both skips, and a skip ends the review with the status line alone.
- **Every section citation follows.** The skill body and the playbook scenarios now point at section 8, including the depth alias and the minimum-evidence scenario.
- **The PR-state reference reads in order.** It gains an intro, Purpose and When to Use, and numbered all-caps sections 2 to 8 with dividers.
- **The checklist index is complete.** The supporting checklists table now lists the fix-completeness checklist.
- **The checklist intro is a hook.** It says what the checklist proves instead of restating its own Purpose.

&nbsp;

## Upgrade

Repoint any local citation that used the old numbers: the efficiency gates were section 9 and are now section 8, and related resources was section 8 and is now section 9.

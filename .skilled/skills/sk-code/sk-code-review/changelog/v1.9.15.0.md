# v1.9.15.0, The Efficiency Gates Come Before Related Resources

A review now reads its two efficiency gates before it scopes anything. RELATED RESOURCES sits last, where the skill template puts it. The PR-state reference gained the numbering, intro and orientation the rest of the packet already had.

&nbsp;

## Why This Release

The M-1 and M-2 gates decide whether a review runs at all. Yet Phase 1 never pointed at them. They sat after RELATED RESOURCES. So a skip could surface only after the work it was meant to prevent. The PR-state reference also had no intro and seven unnumbered peers. A reader could not cite a step or tell which section governed.

&nbsp;

## What's New at a Glance

- **The gates move to section 8.** PR-STATE EFFICIENCY GATES becomes section 8, with subsections 8.1 to 8.3. RELATED RESOURCES becomes section 9.
- **Phase 1 checks the gates first.** Its first step names both skips. A skip ends the review with the status line alone.
- **Every section citation follows.** The skill body and the playbook scenarios now point at section 8. That includes the depth alias and the minimum-evidence scenario.
- **The PR-state reference reads in order.** It gains an intro and Purpose and When to Use. It also gains numbered all-caps sections 2 to 8 with dividers.
- **The checklist index is complete.** The supporting checklists table now lists the fix-completeness checklist.
- **The checklist intro is a hook.** It says what the checklist proves rather than restating its own Purpose.

&nbsp;

## Upgrade

Repoint any local citation that used the old numbers. The efficiency gates were section 9 and are now section 8. Related resources was section 8 and is now section 9.

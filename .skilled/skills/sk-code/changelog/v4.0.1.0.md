Spec-folder authoring docs belong in `system-spec-kit`, their proper domain.

## What's New at a Glance

- **The recipe and checklist moved out of sk-code to new workflow paths.** The recipe moved from `code-implement/assets/opencode/recipes/spec_folder_write.md` to `system-spec-kit/references/workflows/spec-folder-write-recipe.md`. The checklist moved from `code-quality/assets/opencode-checklists/spec_folder_authoring.md` to `system-spec-kit/references/workflows/spec-folder-authoring-checklist.md`.
- **Every inbound reference was repointed to the new home.** That covers the `code-implement` and `code-quality` SKILL load contracts, the `shared/references/smart-routing.md` `RESOURCE_MAP`, the `description.json` keywords and the `/speckit:complete` cross-skill authoring load. In the `RESOURCE_MAP`, the two now-external entries were removed from the machine block and the prose row was rewritten as a system-spec-kit pointer.
- **The remaining checklists move with the surface restructure.** The `{skill,agent,command,mcp_server}` authoring checklists stay in sk-code.

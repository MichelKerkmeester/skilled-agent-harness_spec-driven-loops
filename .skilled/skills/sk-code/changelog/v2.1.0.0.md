# v2.1.0.0, sk-code Adds Surface Evidence

sk-code becomes a two-axis parent hub.

- The two workflow modes, `quality` and `code-review`, are unchanged in what they do.
- The hub now carries two read-only surface evidence packets alongside them.
- They are `code-webflow` and `code-skilled`.
- The hub bundles zero or more of them next to the primary workflow mode.

Two read-only surface packets join the hub.

- `code-webflow/` and `code-skilled/` add surface evidence alongside the workflow modes.
- `routerPolicy.outcomes.surfaceBundle` attaches surface evidence to the primary workflow.

&nbsp;

## Why This Release

The Y-segment bump reflects packets added within the v2 hub architecture.

## Workflow Routing

The registry and router carry the surface axis.

#### Surface Metadata Travels With Packets <a id="c-surface-metadata-travels-with-packets"></a>

- `mode-registry.json` advances to 1.1.0.
- `extensions.surface-axis` declares the two surface entries.
- Every mode carries a `packetKind`.
- Each surface entry uses `packetKind: surface` and `backendKind: evidence-base`.
- It also uses a read-only `toolSurface` and `routingClass: metadata` (advisor-invisible).
- The aliases `webflow implementation` and `opencode system code` moved from the workflow modes to their own surfaces.

#### One Workflow Leads Each Bundle <a id="c-one-workflow-leads-each-bundle"></a>

- For `review my Webflow code for jank`, the hub resolves `[code-review, code-webflow]`.
- The workflow comes first and the surface after it.
- `code-review` remains the review workflow mode and packet.
- There is no breaking change to the routing contract.
- That is because Lane C's projection ignores unknown keys.

#### The Router Vocabulary Covers Surfaces <a id="c-the-router-vocabulary-covers-surfaces"></a>

- `hub-router.json` adds surface `routerSignals` and owned vocabulary classes.
- It also adds the `surfaceBundle` outcome and a full `tieBreak`.
- The vocabulary was reconciled so `parent-hub-vocab-sync` passes clean.
- The counts are 0 orphan aliases, 0 collisions and 0 ownership drift.
- That is down from 13, 4 and 1.

&nbsp;

## Packet Layout

Surface evidence was consolidated out of the workflow modes into the new surface packets.

#### Surface Evidence Moves Into Packets <a id="c-surface-evidence-moves-into-packets"></a>

- Each packet has `SKILL.md`, `README.md`, `references/`, `assets/` and `changelog/`.
- The workflow modes keep only their process contracts, universal checklists and verify scripts.
- The rule-canary CI path and both deep-review agents were repointed in the same change.
- So were the prompt-pack template and `check-rule-copies.js` with its test.
- Hub `SKILL.md`, `README.md` and `description.json` were repointed to the new layout.
- Each is at 2.1.0.0.
- `graph-metadata.json` was repointed too.

&nbsp;

## Smart Routing

Paths in smart routing follow the new packet layout.

#### Paths Follow the Packet Layout <a id="c-paths-follow-the-packet-layout"></a>

- The new form is hub-root-relative and packet-qualified.
- `router-replay.cjs` and the router-sync guard were updated to match.

# v2.2.3.0, Mobile-CLI Leaves the sk-code Hub

The retired `sk-code-mobile-cli` surface is gone. It served Pi Remote Mobile-CLI work. It is removed from the `sk-code` hub's advertised, detected, routed and bundled surfaces. That prevents mis-routed requests and an unreachable mode. The packet directory is retired separately.

&nbsp;

## What's New at a Glance

- The `sk-code` hub has five modes.
  - Two are workflow modes and three are surface modes.
- The routing and detection entries are removed.
  - `ROUTER.md` loses the `MOBILE_CLI` intent key.
  - `shared/references/stack-detection.md` loses the `PI_REMOTE` detection block.
  - `shared/references/stack-detection.md` also loses its markers (`app-mobile/`, `app-relay/`, `packages/pi-*`).
- Pi Remote Mobile-CLI requests resolve to no mobile surface.
  - Requests shaped around `app-mobile/`, Svelte design-system, token or editability topics are included.
- The `v2.2.2.0` notes are marked superseded, not rewritten.
  - The `v2.2.2.0` notes record what the releases did.

&nbsp;

## Upgrade

No migration required.

# v4.1.0.0, sk-code Adds Surface Evidence

sk-code becomes a two-axis parent hub. The two workflow modes, `quality` and `code-review`, are unchanged in what they do. Alongside them the hub now carries two read-only surface evidence packets, `code-webflow` and `code-opencode`, and bundles zero or more of them next to the primary workflow mode.

## Why This Release

The Y-segment bump reflects packets added within the v4 hub architecture.

## What's New at a Glance

- **Two read-only surface packets join the hub.** `code-webflow/` and `code-opencode/` add surface evidence alongside the workflow modes.
- **The hub adds a surface-bundle outcome.** `routerPolicy.outcomes.surfaceBundle` attaches surface evidence to the primary workflow.
- **The mode registry declares the surface axis.** `mode-registry.json` adds the entries through `extensions.surface-axis`.
- **Hub routing adds surface signals.** `hub-router.json` advances to 1.1.0.

---

## Workflow Routing

#### Surface Metadata Travels With Packets

`mode-registry.json` advances to 1.1.0, and `extensions.surface-axis` declares the two surface entries. Every mode carries a `packetKind`. Each surface entry uses `packetKind: surface`, `backendKind: evidence-base`, a read-only `toolSurface` and `routingClass: metadata` (advisor-invisible). The aliases `webflow implementation` and `opencode system code` moved from the workflow modes to their own surfaces.

&nbsp;

#### One Workflow Leads Each Bundle

For `review my Webflow code for jank`, the hub resolves `[code-review, code-webflow]`, with the workflow first and the surface after it. `code-review` remains the review workflow mode and packet. There is no breaking change to the routing contract, because Lane C's projection ignores unknown keys.

&nbsp;

#### The Router Vocabulary Covers Surfaces

`hub-router.json` adds surface `routerSignals`, owned vocabulary classes, the `surfaceBundle` outcome and a full `tieBreak`. The vocabulary was reconciled so `parent-hub-vocab-sync` passes clean, with 0 orphan aliases, 0 collisions and 0 ownership drift, down from 13, 4 and 1.

---

## Packet Layout

#### Surface Evidence Moves Into Packets

Surface evidence was consolidated out of the workflow modes into the new surface packets. Each packet has `SKILL.md`, `README.md`, `references/`, `assets/` and `changelog/`. The workflow modes keep only their process contracts, universal checklists and verify scripts. The rule-canary CI path, both deep-review agents, the prompt-pack template and `check-rule-copies.js` with its test were repointed in the same change. Hub `SKILL.md`, `README.md` and `description.json`, each at 4.1.0.0, and `graph-metadata.json` were repointed to the new layout.

---

## Smart Routing

#### Paths Follow the Packet Layout

Every `RESOURCE_MAP` entry and prose path in `shared/references/smart-routing.md` was normalized to a hub-root-relative, packet-qualified form. `router-replay.cjs` and the router-sync guard were updated to match.

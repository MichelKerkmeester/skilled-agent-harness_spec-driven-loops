# v1.8.9.0, One Vocabulary for the Hub Router

A prompt that stage one sends to a mode used to reach stage two and find no leaf for it, because the root ROUTER.md intents carried fewer words than hub-router.json routes. The intents now carry every routed term, and the three copies that had drifted apart are checked against one authored vocabulary.

> Spec folder: `specs/sk-doc/072-skill-routing-and-clarity/004-single-vocabulary-source` (Level 2)

&nbsp;

## What's New at a Glance

- **Every routed term now reaches stage two.** The root ROUTER.md intents gained 57 terms, and their multi-word forms went into graph-metadata.json intent_signals, where the skill advisor reads them.
- **mcp-obsidian's Health.md, Iconic, Excalidraw and theme terms now load its leaves.** Three words stopped routing instead: `design tokens` now reaches sk-design, and `task list` plus the `dom inspect` alias reach no hub, so the intents keep only the specific forms such as `inspect the dom`.
- **Two bare terms left the vocabulary.** `on its own` and `design reference` are gone from `hub-router.json`, the root `ROUTER.md` intents and the `graph-metadata.json` intent signals, and `design reference` also came off mcp-refero's `Keyword triggers:` line, because both pulled ordinary coding and documentation prompts here. `design reference search` stays.
- **One check now guards the vocabulary.** `.skilled/skills/sk-doc/sk-create-skill/scripts/ci-hub-vocabulary-source.cjs` fails when the registry aliases, the root intents or a packet's `Keyword triggers:` line disagree with hub-router.json.

&nbsp;

## Upgrade

No action is needed. The vocabulary gained more than it lost, and `design reference search` still reaches mcp-refero.

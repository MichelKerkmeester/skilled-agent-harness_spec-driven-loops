`mcp-tooling` folds three flat, independently advisor-routable MCP tool-bridge skills into one advisor identity with two workflow packets and one Figma transport. The work was an 8-phase program.

> Spec folder: `.opencode/specs/skilled-agent-orchestration/126-mcp-tooling-parent`

## Why This Release

`mcp-chrome-devtools`, `mcp-click-up` and `mcp-figma` shared a router-and-gating pattern and reached external tools through the shared Code Mode substrate, but had no structural relationship, shared registry, single advisor identity or hub. The repo's parent-hub canon, already applied to `sk-code`, `sk-design`, `system-deep-loop`, `sk-doc` and `sk-prompt`, exists to fix that shape.

---

## What's New at a Glance

- **The hub declares three bridge modes.** `mcp-chrome-devtools` and `mcp-click-up` are workflows, while `mcp-figma` is a transport.
- **The router has a default bridge.** `routerPolicy.defaultMode: "mcp-chrome-devtools"`.
- **One graph identity replaces three.** `mcp-tooling` combines the former identities' metadata under `family: mcp`.
- **The bridge trees moved under `mcp-tooling`.** The three formerly flat skills now sit beneath the hub.
- **Packet references were adjusted for nesting.** Self-references and cross-skill links account for the new depth.
- **Doctor install guidance points to nested paths.** The stale `mcp-open-design` entry now points to `sk-design/design-mcp-open-design`.
- **Related graph links and advisor examples point to the hub.** Skill references and advisor prompts were retargeted to `mcp-tooling`.
- **The skill catalogs describe the hub.** `.opencode/skills/README.md` and root `README.md` update their catalog rows and prose.
- **`mcp-code-mode` stays flat and standalone.** It remains outside the `mcp-tooling` hub.

## Hub Routing

#### Workflow and Transport Roles

`mode-registry.json` declares `mcp-chrome-devtools` and `mcp-click-up` as `packetKind: "workflow"` with `mutatesWorkspace:true`. It declares `mcp-figma` as `packetKind: "transport"` with `mutatesWorkspace:false` on a `transport-axis` extension that requires a mandatory cross-hub judgment pairing with `sk-design`.

&nbsp;

#### Default and Tie-Break Rules

The router's tie-break orders the two workflow modes before the transport. It declares the base three router outcomes only (`single`, `orderedBundle` and `defer`).

---

## Hub Identity

#### A Shared Graph Identity

The new hub scaffold comprises `mode-registry.json`, `hub-router.json`, `description.json`, `graph-metadata.json` and `SKILL.md`. `mcp-tooling`'s `graph-metadata.json` has `skill_id: mcp-tooling` and `family: mcp`. The three former identities each had `family: mcp`, and the hub combines their intent signals, trigger phrases and outward edges. Those edges include `mcp-figma depends_on sk-design` and the union of `enhances sk-code`. The metadata records `mcp-code-mode` as an external cross-skill dependency rather than a dissolved intra-hub edge. The three bridge packets' own `graph-metadata.json` files were deleted as their independent advisor identities dissolved into the hub's single identity.

---

## Bridge Relocation

#### Content Moves with History

The source trees held 41 files in `mcp-chrome-devtools/*`, 155 in `mcp-click-up/*` and 40 in `mcp-figma/*`. A `git mv` moved their content to `mcp-tooling/mcp-chrome-devtools/*`, `mcp-tooling/mcp-click-up/*` and `mcp-tooling/mcp-figma/*`, preserving full history.

&nbsp;

#### References Match the New Depth

Each packet's absolute `.opencode/skills/mcp-<x>/...` self-references and relative `../` cross-skill links were rewritten for the new nesting depth. `mcp-figma` references to the co-located `mcp-chrome-devtools` sibling needed no depth change because both moved down together. Its references to the untouched `mcp-code-mode` needed one more `../` level.

---

## Connected References

#### Doctor Guidance Uses Nested Paths

`.opencode/commands/doctor/assets/doctor-mcp-install.yaml` repoints each bridge's `skill_dir` and `install_guide` entries to the nested paths. The pre-existing `mcp-open-design` entry pointed to a deleted flat skill and now points to `sk-design/design-mcp-open-design`.

&nbsp;

#### Related Graph Links Point to Hub

In `mcp-code-mode/graph-metadata.json`, `prerequisite_for` and `manual.related_to` point from the three former bridge IDs to `mcp-tooling`. This metadata-only reverse-edge repoint is the only release change to `mcp-code-mode`. In `sk-design/graph-metadata.json`, `siblings`, `prerequisite_for` and `manual.related_to` point from those IDs to `mcp-tooling`.

&nbsp;

#### Advisor Examples Retargeted

`labeled-prompts.jsonl` has three retargeted rows. They change advisor examples from `mcp-chrome-devtools` to `mcp-tooling`.

---

## Standalone MCP Infrastructure

#### Code Mode Stays Separate

`mcp-code-mode` is the fourth `family: mcp` skill but stays flat standalone infrastructure (ADR-005). It is the live native-MCP server in `opencode.json` that the three bridges and other skills reach through the unchanged `code_mode` registration key, serving manuals well beyond this set. It is never a hub member.

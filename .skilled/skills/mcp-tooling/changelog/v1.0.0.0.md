# v1.0.0.0, Three MCP Bridges Move Under One Hub

`mcp-tooling` folds three flat MCP tool-bridge skills into one advisor identity.

- Each skill was independently routable by the advisor.
- The work was an 8-phase program.

The new identity holds two workflow packets and one Figma transport.

- `mcp-chrome-devtools` and `mcp-click-up` are the workflows.
- `mcp-figma` is the Figma transport.

&nbsp;

## Why This Release

The bridge skills shared a router-and-gating pattern and reached external tools through the shared Code Mode substrate.

- `mcp-chrome-devtools`, `mcp-click-up` and `mcp-figma` were the three skills.
- They had no structural relationship or shared registry.
- They had no single advisor identity or hub.
- The repo's parent-hub canon exists to fix that shape.
- It already applies to `sk-code`, `sk-design`, `system-deep-loop`, `sk-doc` and `sk-prompt`.

## Hub Routing

`mode-registry.json` declares each bridge mode and the router's tie-break order.

#### Workflow and Transport Roles <a id="c-workflow-and-transport-roles"></a>

- `mode-registry.json` declares `mcp-chrome-devtools` and `mcp-click-up` as `packetKind: "workflow"`.
- Both have `mutatesWorkspace:true`.
- It declares `mcp-figma` as `packetKind: "transport"` with `mutatesWorkspace:false`.
- That sits on a `transport-axis` extension.
- The extension requires a mandatory cross-hub judgment pairing with `sk-design`.

#### Default and Tie-Break Rules <a id="c-default-and-tie-break-rules"></a>

- The default bridge is `routerPolicy.defaultMode: "mcp-chrome-devtools"`.
- The router's tie-break orders the two workflow modes before the transport.
- It declares only the base three router outcomes.
- Those are `single`, `orderedBundle` and `defer`.

&nbsp;

## Hub Identity

The hub pulls the three old advisor identities into one graph identity.

#### A Shared Graph Identity <a id="c-a-shared-graph-identity"></a>

- The new hub scaffold comprises `mode-registry.json`, `hub-router.json`, `description.json`, `graph-metadata.json` and `SKILL.md`.
- `mcp-tooling`'s `graph-metadata.json` has `skill_id: mcp-tooling` and `family: mcp`.
- The three former identities each had `family: mcp`.
- The hub combines their intent signals, trigger phrases and outward edges.
- Those edges include `mcp-figma depends_on sk-design`.
- They also include the union of `enhances sk-code`.
- The metadata records `mcp-code-mode` as an external cross-skill dependency.
- It is not a dissolved intra-hub edge.
- The three bridge packets' own `graph-metadata.json` files were deleted.
- Their independent advisor identities dissolved into the hub's single identity.

&nbsp;

## Bridge Relocation

The three source trees moved beneath the hub and their links followed.

#### Content Moves with History <a id="c-content-moves-with-history"></a>

- The source trees held 41 files in `mcp-chrome-devtools/*`, 155 in `mcp-click-up/*` and 40 in `mcp-figma/*`.
- A `git mv` moved their content to `mcp-tooling/mcp-chrome-devtools/*`, `mcp-tooling/mcp-click-up/*` and `mcp-tooling/mcp-figma/*`.
- Full history was preserved.

#### References Match the New Depth <a id="c-references-match-the-new-depth"></a>

- Each packet's absolute `.opencode/skills/mcp-<x>/...` self-references were rewritten for the new nesting depth.
- So were its relative `../` cross-skill links.
- `mcp-figma` references to the co-located `mcp-chrome-devtools` sibling needed no depth change.
- Both moved down together.
- Its references to the untouched `mcp-code-mode` needed one more `../` level.

&nbsp;

## Connected References

Doctor guidance, graph links and advisor prompts all point at the new hub.

#### Doctor Guidance Uses Nested Paths <a id="c-doctor-guidance-uses-nested-paths"></a>

- `.opencode/commands/doctor/assets/doctor-mcp-install.yaml` repoints each bridge's `skill_dir` and `install_guide` entries to the nested paths.
- The pre-existing `mcp-open-design` entry pointed to a deleted flat skill.
- It now points to `sk-design/design-mcp-open-design`.

#### Related Graph Links Point to Hub <a id="c-related-graph-links-point-to-hub"></a>

- In `mcp-code-mode/graph-metadata.json`, `prerequisite_for` and `manual.related_to` point from the three former bridge IDs to `mcp-tooling`.
- This metadata-only reverse-edge repoint is the only release change to `mcp-code-mode`.
- In `sk-design/graph-metadata.json`, `siblings`, `prerequisite_for` and `manual.related_to` point from those IDs to `mcp-tooling`.

#### Advisor Examples Retargeted <a id="c-advisor-examples-retargeted"></a>

- `labeled-prompts.jsonl` has three retargeted rows.
- They change advisor examples from `mcp-chrome-devtools` to `mcp-tooling`.

#### Catalogs Describe the Hub <a id="c-catalogs-describe-the-hub"></a>

- `.opencode/skills/README.md` and root `README.md` update their catalog rows and prose.

&nbsp;

## Standalone MCP Infrastructure

`mcp-code-mode` sits outside the hub as standalone infrastructure.

#### Code Mode Stays Separate <a id="c-code-mode-stays-separate"></a>

- `mcp-code-mode` is the fourth `family: mcp` skill.
- It stays flat standalone infrastructure.
- It is the live native-MCP server in `opencode.json`.
- The three bridges and other skills reach it through the unchanged `code_mode` registration key.
- It serves manuals well beyond this set.
- It is never a hub member.

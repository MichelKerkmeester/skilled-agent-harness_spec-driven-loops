# v1.9.7.0, ClickUp Login and the Live Chrome Manual Route to Their Modes

A request to check the ClickUp approval and login routed to sk-code, and a request naming the live Chrome manual stopped at the hub without picking a mode.

> Spec folder: `specs/mcp-tooling/026-clickup-doc-divider-spacing`

&nbsp;

## What's New at a Glance

- **ClickUp readiness phrases reach the hub.** "clickup mcp", "clickup login", "log in to clickup", "clickup oauth", "clickup approval" and four more now sit in the ClickUp vocabulary of `ROUTER.md` and `hub-router.json`, and in the advisor signals.
- **The live Chrome manual has a name the router knows.** `chrome_devtools_live`, "remote debugging", "my own chrome", "signed-in chrome" and "logged in chrome" route to mcp-chrome-devtools.
- **The modes it routes to changed.** mcp-click-up v1.2.15.0, mcp-chrome-devtools v1.0.22.0 and mcp-aside-devtools v1.1.9.0.

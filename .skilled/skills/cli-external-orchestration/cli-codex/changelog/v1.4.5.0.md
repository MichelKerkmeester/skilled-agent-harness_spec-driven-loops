This maintenance release clarifies where `cli-codex` prompt guidance comes from. Shared card content now follows the canonical `sk-prompt` card. Local notes remain. ## What's New at a Glance

- The prompt card delegates shared guidance.
  - `assets/prompt-quality-card.md` keeps cli-codex-specific notes.
  - It points readers to the sk-prompt master card for canonical content.
- `SKILL.md` now states the precedence order.
  - The sk-prompt master card comes first, the cli-codex card second and inline guidance third.
  - `SKILL.md` also adds a cross-link to the sk-prompt hub's canonical card.
- The playbook citations now follow current headings.
  - `references/manual-testing-playbook.md` points readers to headings in the canonical sk-prompt card.
  - The citations now match the card's current structure.

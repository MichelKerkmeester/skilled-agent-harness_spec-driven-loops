Pi's provider guidance now reflects OpenRouter's removal from its closed roster and corrects a stale count of authenticated providers with a confirmed installed catalog.

> Spec folder: `specs/cli-external-orchestration/068-drop-openrouter-provider/`

## What's New at a Glance

- **OpenRouter leaves Pi's closed roster.** `references/providers-and-models.md` no longer has the `### openrouter` section or its `deepseek/deepseek-v4-flash-vision-exp` and `z-ai/glm-5.3-flash` rows. `SKILL.md` now names six reachable providers instead of seven: `openai-codex`, `opencode-go`, `cline-pass`, `llmgateway`, `minimax` and `xiaomi`.
- **The roster note now describes its current source.** It drops `openrouter confirmed 2026-08-17` and describes where the current roster comes from. OpenRouter is no longer in that roster.
- **Cline comparisons use the routes that remain.** The `cline-pass` rows lost their OpenRouter cross-references, which were the only thing making them accurate. The GLM-5.3-Flash per-route ceiling sentence now compares Cline against `opencode-go` and DevPass only, and its "same underlying model" pointer names those two routes instead of OpenRouter.
- **The `llmgateway` rationale retains `opencode-go`.** It no longer names OpenRouter as the per-token alternative that lost the slot. `opencode-go` remains on the roster.
- **The installed-catalog count is six, down from seven.** The provider-breadth paragraph now says only six authenticated providers have a confirmed installed catalog. The old count contained no OpenRouter token, so a token-matching sweep would have left it wrong.
- **The `changelog/v1.4.1.0.md` entry keeps its historical claim.** It records that the OpenRouter three-model allowlist was untouched by the 056 roster retirement. That was true when it was written, and a changelog states what was true then.
- **Other provider and model rows remain unchanged.** DeepSeek V4 Flash and GLM-5.3-Flash keep fan-out routes through DevPass and direct routes through `opencode-go`. No model left the roster with OpenRouter.
- **Pi's model policies remain unchanged.** The `--thinking` lever semantics and flash max-tier pin policy are unchanged, as is §3's "no default model" contract. OpenRouter was never a default, fallback or example command here.
- **The fan-out runtime still maps both models to OpenRouter.** `PI_SUPPORTED_MODELS` (`executor-config.ts`) still allowlists `deepseek/deepseek-v4-flash-vision-exp` and `z-ai/glm-5.3-flash`, while `PI_MODEL_PROVIDERS` (`fanout-run.cjs`) still maps both to `openrouter`. The roster is narrower than that runtime allowlist, which fails closed on the roster side and open on the runtime side. Changing that different skill's contract needs its own packet.

No migration required.

# cli-pi v1.5.1.0

## Changed

- **OpenRouter removed from the closed roster** (spec `specs/cli-external-orchestration/068-drop-openrouter-provider/`). The `### openrouter` section of `references/providers-and-models.md` is gone with both of its rows, `deepseek/deepseek-v4-flash-vision-exp` and `z-ai/glm-5.3-flash`. `SKILL.md` now says **six** providers are reachable rather than seven: `openai-codex`, `opencode-go`, `cline-pass`, `llmgateway`, `minimax` and `xiaomi`.
- The roster's sourcing note dropped its `openrouter confirmed 2026-08-17` clause; it describes where the *current* roster comes from, and the provider is no longer in it.
- **The `cline-pass` rows lost their OpenRouter cross-references, which were the only thing making them accurate.** The GLM-5.3-Flash row's per-route ceiling sentence now compares Cline against opencode-go and DevPass only, and its "same underlying model" pointer names those two routes instead of the OpenRouter one.
- The `llmgateway` fan-out rationale no longer names OpenRouter as the per-token alternative that lost the slot; opencode-go is the one that remains on the roster.
- **A count claim carrying no OpenRouter token still went stale and was fixed.** The provider-breadth paragraph closed with "only the seven authenticated providers above have a confirmed installed catalog"; there are six. A token-matching sweep would have left it wrong.

## Not Changed

- **`changelog/v1.4.1.0.md`.** It records that the OpenRouter three-model allowlist was untouched by the 056 roster retirement. That was true when it was written, and a changelog states what was true then.
- **Every other provider and every model row.** DeepSeek V4 Flash and GLM-5.3-Flash both keep a fan-out route through DevPass and a direct route through opencode-go, so no model left the roster with OpenRouter.
- **The `--thinking` lever semantics, the flash max-tier pin policy, and the "no default model" contract** in §3. OpenRouter was never a default, a fallback, or an example command here.
- **The deep-loop fan-out runtime.** `PI_SUPPORTED_MODELS` (`executor-config.ts`) still allowlists `deepseek/deepseek-v4-flash-vision-exp` and `z-ai/glm-5.3-flash`, and `PI_MODEL_PROVIDERS` (`fanout-run.cjs`) still maps both to `openrouter`. That is a different skill's contract and removing it needs its own packet; until then the roster is narrower than the allowlist, which fails closed on the roster side and open on the runtime side.

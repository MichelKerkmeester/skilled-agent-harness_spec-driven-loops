This release dissolves the standalone `model-dispatch-gpt-5.6.md` reference into `cli-reference.md`, matching the sibling convention: `cli-cursor`, `cli-devin`, and `cli-opencode` all keep model-selection guidance as a section of their `cli-reference.md` and delegation docs rather than a per-model file.

The new `cli-reference.md` §13 MODEL SELECTION replaces and upgrades the old file's content. It documents the confirmed selection surface (`--provider`, `--model` with the `provider/id` and `:<thinking>` forms, and the standalone `--thinking off|minimal|low|medium|high|xhigh|max` flag from the installed help), the live-verified authenticated provider roster (`openai-codex`, `deepseek`, `minimax`, `xiaomi`, with a real `deepseek-v4-flash` dispatch as evidence), and the GPT-5.6 tier caveats. Two claims the old file carried as UNCONFIRMED are now resolved: Pi's reasoning-effort control is the `--thinking` flag, and its scale stops at `max`, so the Codex-side `ultra` ceiling for `gpt-5.6-sol` has no Pi-side value. `gpt-5.6-terra` no longer appears in the installed catalog and is dropped from the roster.

The manual-testing playbook also grew from 19 to 20 scenarios: the new `PI-020` live-traces the five session-lifecycle extension bridges against an authenticated provider, and `PI-014`/`PI-015`/`PI-016` were refreshed to the current eleven-extension surface with their retired provider-credentials blockers cleared.

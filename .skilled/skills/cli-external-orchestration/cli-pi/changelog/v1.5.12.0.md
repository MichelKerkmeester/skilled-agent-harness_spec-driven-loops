MiMo v2.6 Flash joins the roster, and MiMo Pro and Flash are now reachable through OpenCode Go and the Cline Pass as well as DevPass.

> Spec folder: `specs/cli-external-orchestration/076-mimo-v2-6-cutover` (Level 1)

## What's New at a Glance

- **MiMo Flash on DevPass.** `llmgateway/mimo-v2.6-flash` is declared in `.pi/models.json` at $0.14 in and $0.28 out per million tokens. The bare `mimo-v2.6-flash` literal is on the deep-loop allowlist and fans out through `llmgateway`.
- **MiMo on OpenCode Go.** `opencode-go/mimo-v2.6-pro` and `opencode-go/mimo-v2.6-flash` are Pi builtins, now in the picker. They are direct-dispatch routes.
- **MiMo on the Cline Pass.** `cline-pass/cline-pass/mimo-v2.6-pro` and `cline-pass/cline-pass/mimo-v2.6-flash` use the Pass-covered `cline-pass/` model type. Cline's `xiaomi/` ids bill Cline Credits and are left off the roster.
- **Every new route answered live.** Each of the five new routes replied `PONG` to a one-turn smoke at `--thinking high` on 2026-09-30.

## Upgrade

No migration required. MiMo Flash stops at `high`, so pass `--thinking high` for it.

Pi's classifier models gain a documented entry point: `choice`, `bool` and `score` questions from codemode scripts, extensions and the SDK.

> Spec folder: `specs/cli-jev/003-cli-jev-workflow-integration/038-pi-classifier-transport-integration` (Level 2)

## What's New at a Glance

- **`### Classifier Calls` joins `SKILL.md`.** A Pi worker reaches classifier models three ways: `models.classify()` in a codemode script with `"defaultTools": ["+codemode"]`, `ctx.modelRegistry.classify()` in an extension, or the SDK's `ModelRuntime`.
- **The three classifier types are named.** `choice`, `bool` and `score`, per the installed package's `docs/models.md`.
- **One intent row and two rules.** The smart router scores classifier vocabulary under `CLASSIFIER`, `ALWAYS` line 13 routes a classifier question to Pi's own surfaces, and `NEVER` line 10 keeps a classifier answer as evidence rather than permission.
- **Credentials stay in Pi's own store.** A classifier call passes no key, and `result.usage` reports the tokens and cost when the service reports them.

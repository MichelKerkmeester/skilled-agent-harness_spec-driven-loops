Pi's classifier models gain a documented entry point. Codemode scripts, extensions and the SDK can ask `choice`, `bool` and `score` questions.

## What's New at a Glance

- **`### Classifier Calls` joins `SKILL.md`.** A Pi worker reaches classifier models three ways. It can call `models.classify()` in a codemode script with `"defaultTools": ["+codemode"]`. It can call `ctx.modelRegistry.classify()` in an extension. It can use the SDK's `ModelRuntime`.
- **The three classifier types are named.** They are `choice`, `bool` and `score`. The installed package's `docs/models.md` names them.
- **One intent row and two rules.** The smart router scores classifier vocabulary under `CLASSIFIER`. `ALWAYS` line 13 routes a classifier question to Pi's own surfaces. `NEVER` line 10 keeps a classifier answer as evidence, not permission.
- **Credentials stay in Pi's own store.** A classifier call passes no key. `result.usage` reports the tokens and cost when the service reports them.

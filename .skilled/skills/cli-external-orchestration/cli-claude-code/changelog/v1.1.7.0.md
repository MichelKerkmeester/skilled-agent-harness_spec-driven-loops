v1.1.7.0 brings Claude Code's prompt-composition guidance into line with the `sk-prompt` master card v1.3.1.0. It adds two notes about wording and softens the pre-planning density claim in `assets/prompt-quality-card.md`.

> Spec folder: `specs/skilled-agent-orchestration/113-cli-devin-prompt-quality/007-cross-model-validation/`

## Why This Release

The SWE-1.6 empirical source was `specs/skilled-agent-orchestration/113-cli-devin-prompt-quality/003-eval-loop/`. Cross-model validation compared prompt wording on `deepseek-v4-pro` and `kimi-k2.6`.

## What's New at a Glance

- **Bundle-gate strictness now has a dedicated note.** In §3 of `assets/prompt-quality-card.md`, standard wording scored +0.0525 above strict wording for bundle-gate aversion in `deepseek-v4-pro` and +0.0305 in `kimi-k2.6`.
- **Anti-hallucination wording is a secondary lever.** In §3 of `assets/prompt-quality-card.md`, framework wording scored +0.0732 above anti-hallucination wording in `deepseek-v4-pro` and +0.0756 in `kimi-k2.6`.
- **Pre-planning density wording now says *without clear yield*.** It replaces *without yield* because frontier models tied medium with dense while smaller coding-specialized models lost on dense.

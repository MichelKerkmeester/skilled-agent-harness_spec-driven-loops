# cli-claude-code v1.1.6.0: Pre-planning density guidance

This documentation-only addendum brings a cli-devin SWE 1.6 pre-planning result into cli-claude-code's framework-selection guidance.

> Spec folder: `specs/skilled-agent-orchestration/113-cli-devin-prompt-quality/006-cross-cli-rcaf-propagation/`

## Why This Release

`specs/skilled-agent-orchestration/113-cli-devin-prompt-quality/003-eval-loop/` is the empirical source: in the cli-devin SWE 1.6 run, medium-density pre-planning scored ~10-15% higher on aggregate than dense pre-plans. The finding likely generalizes across CLI orchestrators because just enough structure without too much is a model-agnostic prompt-engineering principle.

The other two SWE 1.6 findings, "standard-over-strict bundle-gate" and "framework-dominates-anti-hallucination," were not propagated because they came from a small coding-specialized model and may not generalize to frontier models such as `claude-opus-4.7`, `gpt-5.5` and `deepseek-v4-pro`. `specs/skilled-agent-orchestration/113-cli-devin-prompt-quality/007-cross-model-validation/` will measure whether they hold on `deepseek-v4-pro` and `kimi-k2.6` before any cross-CLI propagation.

## What's New at a Glance

- **The prompt-quality card adds medium-density pre-planning guidance.** The "Pre-planning density" note follows the task-to-framework map in `assets/prompt-quality-card.md` §3 and recommends 3-4 ordered steps with per-step acceptance and verification instead of 4+ steps with full I/O contracts per step. It complements the RCAF default already present, which mirrors `sk-prompt`'s general default.

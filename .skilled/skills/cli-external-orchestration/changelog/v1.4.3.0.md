v1.4.3.0 enforces the operator's max-only rule for DeepSeek V4 Flash and corrects the catalogs' earlier claims that Flash was non-reasoning and `--variant` was ignored. `Flash Max` names an effort level rather than a distinct model ID. Live metadata in `models-store.json` and `opencode models deepseek`/`opencode-go` confirmed `reasoning: true` with a `max` `thinkingLevelMap` entry.

> Spec folder: `specs/cli-external-orchestration/044-deepseek-v4-flash-max-only/`

## What's New at a Glance

- **Fan-out pins DeepSeek V4 Flash to max effort.** Fan-out uses `--thinking max` with bare `deepseek-v4-flash` on `cli-pi` and `--variant max` with the provider-prefixed model on `cli-opencode`, overriding lower or missing lineage effort. The recorded `reasoningEffort` reflects the pin.
- **Catalogs correct Flash's description.** The `cli-pi` and `cli-opencode` `providers-and-models.md` catalogs now describe Flash as a reasoning model pinned to max thinking, correcting the earlier claim that it was non-reasoning and that `--variant` was ignored.
- **cli-devin keeps its enforcement with a policy note.** Its roster already carries only `deepseek-v4-flash-max` for Flash. That tier-baked identifier remains outside the new pin.
- **Routing behavior stays unchanged.** Flash remains in every enforced model roster and only its dispatch effort is pinned.
- **Other CLI behavior stays unchanged.** `cli-cursor` never carried a DeepSeek model. Raw `pi` and `opencode` binary invocations outside fan-out remain unchanged.
- **Other model mappings stay unchanged.** DeepSeek Pro, GLM, GPT-5.6, Grok, SWE, Gemini, MiniMax, MiMo and Qwen keep their existing mappings.

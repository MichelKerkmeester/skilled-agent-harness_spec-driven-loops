v1.4.3.0 enforces the operator's max-only rule for DeepSeek V4 Flash. It also corrects the catalogs' earlier claims. Those claims said Flash was non-reasoning and that `--variant` was ignored. `Flash Max` names an effort level, not a distinct model ID. Live metadata confirmed `reasoning: true` with a `max` `thinkingLevelMap` entry. That metadata came from `models-store.json` and `opencode models deepseek`/`opencode-go`.

&nbsp;

## What's New at a Glance

- Fan-out pins DeepSeek V4 Flash to max effort.
  - On `cli-pi`, fan-out uses `--thinking max` with bare `deepseek-v4-flash`.
  - On `cli-opencode`, it uses `--variant max` with the provider-prefixed model.
  - Both override lower or missing lineage effort.
  - The recorded `reasoningEffort` reflects the pin.
- Catalogs correct Flash's description.
  - The `cli-pi` and `cli-opencode` `providers-and-models.md` catalogs now describe Flash as a reasoning model.
  - Flash is pinned to max thinking.
  - The catalogs correct the earlier claim that it was non-reasoning.
  - The catalogs also correct the claim that `--variant` was ignored.
- cli-devin keeps its enforcement with a policy note.
  - cli-devin's roster already carries only `deepseek-v4-flash-max` for Flash.
  - That tier-baked identifier remains outside the new pin.
- Routing behavior stays unchanged.
  - Flash remains in every enforced model roster.
  - Only its dispatch effort is pinned.
- Other CLI behavior stays unchanged.
  - `cli-cursor` never carried a DeepSeek model.
  - Raw `pi` and `opencode` binary invocations outside fan-out remain unchanged.
- Other model mappings stay unchanged. DeepSeek Pro, GLM, GPT-5.6, Grok, SWE, Gemini, MiniMax, MiMo and Qwen keep their existing mappings.

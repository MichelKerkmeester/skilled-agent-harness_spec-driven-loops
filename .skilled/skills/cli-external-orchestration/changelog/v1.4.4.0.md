`cli-opencode` and `cli-pi` now use GLM-5.3-Flash in place of the retired Ox Alpha routes and share a three-model OpenRouter allowlist. The release also registers GLM on opencode-go and Cline, then updates `cli-pi`'s default and deep-loop fan-out. It corrects the OpenRouter claim in the `cli-opencode` guide.

> Spec folder: `specs/cli-external-orchestration/055-glm-5-3-flash-gemini-roster/`

## What's New at a Glance

- **GLM-5.3-Flash replaces both Ox Alpha routes.** The OpenRouter `stealth/ox-alpha` and Cline `x-ai/ox-alpha` routes are gone from `cli-opencode` and `cli-pi`. The replacement model facts were checked against live `opencode models openrouter|opencode-go|cline-pass` listings, with no Ox Alpha live-dispatch evidence carried onto the new entries.
- **Both CLIs allow exactly three OpenRouter models.** The list is `deepseek/deepseek-v4-flash-latest`, `z-ai/glm-5.3-flash` and `google/gemini-3.7-flash`. DeepSeek V4 Flash stays first and its `max` pin is unchanged.
- **GLM-5.3-Flash is pinned to its top tier.** It is a reasoning model with `low`, `high` and `max` variants, 1.31M context and 131K output. `cli-opencode` uses `--variant max` and `cli-pi` uses `--thinking max`.
- **Gemini 3.7 Flash uses its high tier.** It is a reasoning model with `low`, `medium` and `high` variants and no `max` tier. `cli-opencode` uses `--variant high` and `cli-pi` uses `--thinking high`. The flash max-pin intentionally does not catch Gemini.
- **GLM is registered on opencode-go and Cline.** Its IDs are `opencode-go/glm-5.3-flash` and `cline-pass/z-ai/glm-5.3-flash`. Cline uses the `z-ai/` vendor prefix and has an `xhigh` ceiling because it has no `max` tier. `opencode models cline-pass` does not list the Cline ID, but the operator's Cline runtime logs confirmed it and direct dispatch works.
- **`cli-pi` now defaults to the GLM ID.** The `cline-pass` block in `.pi/models.json` replaces Ox Alpha with `z-ai/glm-5.3-flash`. `.pi/settings.json` sets `defaultModel` to `z-ai/glm-5.3-flash`. `enabledModels` drops both Ox Alpha IDs and adds `openrouter/z-ai/glm-5.3-flash`, `openrouter/google/gemini-3.7-flash`, `opencode-go/glm-5.3-flash` and `cline-pass/z-ai/glm-5.3-flash`.
- **Deep-loop fan-out carries the new model IDs.** `PI_SUPPORTED_MODELS` and `PI_ALLOWED_MODELS` drop both Ox Alpha literals and add `z-ai/glm-5.3-flash` and `google/gemini-3.7-flash` for OpenRouter, plus bare `glm-5.3-flash` for opencode-go. `isFlashMaxPinnedModel` now also matches GLM-5.3-Flash. OpenRouter and Cline share `z-ai/glm-5.3-flash`. The `modelId→provider` map holds one provider per ID, so fan-out routes the shared ID through OpenRouter while Cline GLM remains a direct-dispatch route.
- **The `cli-opencode` guide names all three models.** Its `SKILL.md` no longer says that OpenRouter routes DeepSeek V4 Flash only.
- **Other model mappings stay unchanged.** `cli-cursor` and `cli-devin` never carried Ox Alpha and remain untouched. Every other model and effort mapping, including DeepSeek Pro, GLM-5.2, GPT-5.6, Grok, SWE, MiniMax, MiMo and Qwen, is unchanged.

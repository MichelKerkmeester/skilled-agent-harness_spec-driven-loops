# v1.7.4.0, The Fanout Scenarios Share One Layout

Each fanout-stress scenario laid out its commands, evidence and verdict in its own way. So the package validator could not read them. All fourteen now use the five numbered sections. Every playbook scenario uses those sections. The commands, evidence, verdict and triage sit under the test-execution section. The playbook also says a scenario is not complete until two things are recorded. Those are its outcome and its evidence paths. Both go under the benchmark reports folder.

&nbsp;

## What's New at a Glance

- **The package validator reads every scenario.** The fourteen fanout-stress files pass the strict package check.
- **A finished scenario leaves a record.** The playbook names the reports folder. Each pass, fail or skip is written there with its reason and evidence.
- **The compiled-routing scenario matches its siblings.** The cli-transport ordered bundle uses the same section shape as the other hubs.

&nbsp;

## Upgrade

No migration required. Scenario ids, commands and expected results are unchanged.

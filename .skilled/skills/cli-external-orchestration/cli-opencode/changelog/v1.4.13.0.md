MiMo v2.6 Flash joins the roster, and MiMo Pro and Flash are now listed on OpenCode Go and the Cline Pass as well as DevPass.

> Spec folder: `specs/cli-external-orchestration/076-mimo-v2-6-cutover` (Level 1)

## What's New at a Glance

- **MiMo Flash on DevPass.** `llmgateway/mimo-v2.6-flash` takes `--variant high` like Pro and costs $0.14 in and $0.28 out per million tokens.
- **MiMo on OpenCode Go.** `opencode-go/mimo-v2.6-pro` and `opencode-go/mimo-v2.6-flash` are built in. Their catalog lists no variants, so omit `--variant`.
- **MiMo on the Cline Pass.** `cline-pass/cline-pass/mimo-v2.6-pro` and `cline-pass/cline-pass/mimo-v2.6-flash` use the Pass-covered model type. Cline's `xiaomi/` ids bill Cline Credits and are left off the roster.
- **Every new route answered live.** Each of the five new routes replied `PONG` to a one-turn `opencode run` on 2026-09-30. No config change was needed, since OpenCode ships all five.

## Upgrade

No migration required.

## DevPass DeepSeek

The DevPass DeepSeek row now uses `llmgateway/deepseek-v4.1-flash`. The effort table now lists the route's accepted ladder.

#### Replace the Retired ID <a id="c-replace-the-retired-id"></a>

> [!WARNING]
> The gateway now returns `410` for `llmgateway/deepseek-v4-flash-vision-exp`. Replace it with `llmgateway/deepseek-v4.1-flash` in the DevPass DeepSeek row.

- The replacement is image-capable, like the retired ID.
- It keeps the retired ID's 1.05M context and 384K output.
- This row is the DevPass route only.
- The `opencode-go` and `cline-pass` DeepSeek rows are different routes.
- They remain untouched.

<details>
<summary><b>What it costs</b></summary>

- It costs $0.15 in and $0.60 out per million tokens, with cached reads at $0.003.
- That is below the gateway's Premium threshold.
- So the no-weekly-cap note still holds.

</details>

#### Show the Accepted Ladder <a id="c-show-the-accepted-ladder"></a>

- The previous table omitted `minimal`, `medium` and `xhigh`.
- The route accepts them.
- `minimal` reaches low.
- `medium` and `xhigh` reach high.
- `max` reaches max.
- `none` disables thinking.
- `high` is the default.
- The route rejects `ultra` and the integer form.
- The ladder comes from DeepSeek's thinking-mode guide.
- It does not come from status-code inference.

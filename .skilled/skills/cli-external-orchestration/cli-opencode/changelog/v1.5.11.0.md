# v1.5.11.0, Mistral joins the roster

OpenCode can now reach the operator's Mistral subscription. Two ids are in scope: DeepSeek V4.1 Flash and GLM-5.3-Flash, as Mistral hosts them. The default stays `llmgateway/deepseek-v4.1-flash-1017`.

> Spec folder: `specs/cli-external-orchestration/runtime-lifecycle/004-cli-mistral-vibe-creation/002-mistral-provider-roster`

&nbsp;

## What's New at a Glance

- **Five providers are reachable.** `mistral` joins `opencode-go`, `openai`, `llmgateway` and `minimax`.
- **Two Mistral ids are allowed.** `mistral/deepseek-v4-1-flash-1017` and `mistral/zai-glm-5-3-flash` each replied `OK` at `#max` on OpenCode 2 on 2026-11-14. Every other `mistral` id stays forbidden.
- **Mistral ids use dashes.** The dotted DevPass forms do not resolve on this provider.
- **Billing is unconfirmed.** OpenCode prices a turn at Mistral's list rate. Whether a turn draws on the subscription pool is UNKNOWN until the console counter is read.

&nbsp;

## Upgrade

Connect the `mistral` provider once through OpenCode's connect flow before dispatching to it.

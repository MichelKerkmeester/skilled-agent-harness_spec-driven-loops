The curated provider roster now includes MiMo V2.6 Pro through the existing DevPass LLM Gateway route. Its provider-qualified route is for direct dispatch only, while the bare deep-loop literal continues to resolve through the official Xiaomi route.

## What's New at a Glance

- **`llmgateway/mimo-v2.6-pro` joins the provider roster.** Its catalog entry records 1M context, 131K output, reasoning, image input, published costs and the `none`, `low`, `medium` and `high` variants.
- **Existing access and routing rules stay in place.** LLM Gateway authentication and the one-literal-one-provider fan-out rule (each literal maps to one provider) remain unchanged. The Xiaomi Pro and Pro UltraSpeed routes remain available, and the Hermes roster and historical changelog entries stay unchanged.
- **Catalog visibility does not prove route availability.** A live credentialed dispatch is still needed when route availability must be proven.

# cli-opencode v1.3.4.0: MiniMax prompt guidance and slug correction

This documentation and config addendum gives cli-opencode prompt guidance. The guidance comes from real MiniMax M2.7 dispatches. It also corrects the model slug left as a placeholder in 120/001.

> Spec folder: `.opencode/specs/skilled-agent-orchestration/120-cli-opencode-minimax-optimization/003-minimax-prompt-framework-benchmark`

## What's New at a Glance

- **The MiniMax run favored TIDD-EC with dense pre-planning.** `120/003-minimax-prompt-framework-benchmark` reused the 113 SWE-1.6 eval rig. The rig had 7 fixtures, a 5-dimension rubric and a claude grader. Dispatch changed to `opencode run --model minimax/MiniMax-M2.7`. Across 7 iterations, it made 49 real MiniMax dispatches. Those covered 5 prompt frameworks and a pre-planning hill-climb.
- **TIDD-EC and dense pre-planning lead the recorded results.** TIDD-EC scored 0.767. It came ahead of COSTAR at 0.762, RCAF at 0.742, RACE at 0.704 and CIDI at 0.683. Dense scored 0.775 against medium at 0.767. SWE-1.6 favored RCAF and medium.
- **Template 14 and the quality card carry the winner into prompt use.** `assets/prompt-templates.md` adds the TIDD-EC and dense pre-planning scaffold and invocation. `assets/prompt-quality-card.md` sets it as the override for `minimax/MiniMax-M2.7`. RCAF is the fallback.
- **The corrected slug reaches the related guidance.** `SKILL.md` and `references/cli-reference.md` replace `minimax/minimax-2.7` with `minimax/MiniMax-M2.7`. The live `opencode models minimax` list showed that slug. The version bump comes alongside. `sk-prompt-models/assets/model-profiles.json` gives the `minimax-2.7` profile a `context_length` of 204800. It updates its notes and strengths to reflect the benchmark and corrected slug. `sk-prompt/assets/cli-prompt-quality-card.md` adds the per-model override note. `sk-prompt-models/references/pattern-index.md` adds its row.
- **A missing dependency affected absolute scores equally.** `fix-003` had no `node_modules`. So its D2 hard-gated all variants. This affects absolute scores equally, not the relative ranking.
- **The grader's sample count limits confidence.** It used `fallback_fenced` parse mode and one sample per variant and fixture. Margins of 0.008-0.03 were above the roughly 0.02 noise floor. A re-run would tighten confidence.
- **The result was not cross-model-validated.** MiniMax was the only model on this provider path. No cli-devin/codex cards were updated. That is because MiniMax is cli-opencode-only.

# cli-opencode v1.3.2.0: Pre-planning Density Guidance Propagated

This documentation-only addendum recommends medium-density pre-planning for CLI orchestrators. The cli-devin SWE 1.6 empirical run in `specs/skilled-agent-orchestration/113-cli-devin-prompt-quality/003-eval-loop/` measured that three to four ordered steps with per-step acceptance and verification outperformed dense pre-plans of four or more steps with full I/O (input/output) contracts per step by about 10 to 15 percent on aggregate score. The finding likely generalizes because just enough structure, but not too much, is a model-agnostic prompt-engineering principle.

> Spec folder: `specs/skilled-agent-orchestration/113-cli-devin-prompt-quality/006-cross-cli-rcaf-propagation/`

## Why This Release

The other two SWE 1.6 findings, `standard-over-strict bundle-gate` and `framework-dominates-anti-hallucination`, were not propagated because they came from a small coding-specialized model and may not generalize to frontier models. Pending cross-model validation in `specs/skilled-agent-orchestration/113-cli-devin-prompt-quality/007-cross-model-validation/` will measure whether they hold on `deepseek-v4-pro` (`cli-opencode` + DeepSeek direct API) and `kimi-k2.6` (`cli-opencode` + `opencode-go`), the two models cli-opencode dispatches most often, before any cross-CLI propagation.

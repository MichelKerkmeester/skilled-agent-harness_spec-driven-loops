# cli-opencode v1.3.2.0: Pre-planning Density Guidance Propagated

This documentation-only addendum recommends medium-density pre-planning for CLI orchestrators. The cli-devin SWE 1.6 empirical run in `specs/skilled-agent-orchestration/113-cli-devin-prompt-quality/003-eval-loop/` measured a gap. Three to four ordered steps with per-step acceptance and verification outperformed dense pre-plans. Those dense pre-plans had four or more steps. Each step had full I/O (input/output) contracts. The gap was about 10 to 15 percent on aggregate score. The finding likely generalizes because just enough structure, but not too much, is a model-agnostic prompt-engineering principle.

&nbsp;

## Why This Release

The other two SWE 1.6 findings were not propagated. They are `standard-over-strict bundle-gate` and `framework-dominates-anti-hallucination`. They came from a small coding-specialized model. So they may not generalize to frontier models. Cross-model validation is pending in `specs/skilled-agent-orchestration/113-cli-devin-prompt-quality/007-cross-model-validation/`. It will measure whether they hold on two models. Those are `deepseek-v4-pro` (`cli-opencode` + DeepSeek direct API) and `kimi-k2.6` (`cli-opencode` + `opencode-go`). These are the two models cli-opencode dispatches most often. This comes before any cross-CLI propagation.

- **No Pro tier on the GPT-6 grid** -- the grid now shows two personas at two speed tiers, four slugs, and says plainly that this roster has no Pro tier. It also notes that the catalog lists `openai/gpt-6-astra` and `openai/gpt-6-astra-fast`, which are not on the roster.

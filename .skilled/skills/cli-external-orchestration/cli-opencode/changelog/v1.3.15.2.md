# cli-opencode v1.3.15.2, The GPT-5.6 Model Roster

cli-opencode v1.3.15.2 updates the OpenAI model-selection guidance. Readers use it when choosing a dispatch model.

## Why This Release

GPT-5.5 is no longer the live OpenAI premium option this skill should recommend. GPT-5.6 expanded from one three-tier line into a bare tier plus Sol, Terra and Luna. Keeping GPT-5.5 slugs would route dispatches at a superseded family. Documenting only Sol and Terra would hide the live Luna family and the bare tier.

## What's New at a Glance

- **GPT-5.6 replaces GPT-5.5 in active OpenAI guidance.** The previous family was `openai/gpt-5.5`, `openai/gpt-5.5-fast` and `openai/gpt-5.5-pro`. `opencode models openai` confirmed the twelve live slugs now listed in `SKILL.md`'s Authentication options and Model Selection. `references/cli-reference.md`'s `--model` override example and `--variant` table list them too. The bare tier is `gpt-5.6`, `gpt-5.6-fast` and `gpt-5.6-pro`. Sol is `gpt-5.6-sol`, `gpt-5.6-sol-fast` and `gpt-5.6-sol-pro`. Terra is `gpt-5.6-terra`, `gpt-5.6-terra-fast` and `gpt-5.6-terra-pro`. Luna is `gpt-5.6-luna`, `gpt-5.6-luna-fast` and `gpt-5.6-luna-pro`.
- **Sol is the flagship default.** Single-option default and premium references map `gpt-5.5` to `gpt-5.6-sol`. They map `gpt-5.5-fast` to `gpt-5.6-sol-fast` and `gpt-5.5-pro` to `gpt-5.6-sol-pro`. The provider-missing fallback (option A) and ALWAYS rule 3 override example point at the Sol tier. The pre-flight fallback offer uses `gpt-5.6-sol-pro`. `README.md`'s FAQ answer to "Which model do I pick?" recommends `gpt-5.6-sol` for premium dispatch.
- **Model tier and reasoning effort are separate choices.** An earlier `SKILL.md` `Model Selection` draft incorrectly called base, Fast and Pro "reasoning tiers". The correction names them model tiers selected by the slug. It also describes the separate reasoning-effort lever in `references/cli-reference.md`'s `--variant` row. The settings are `--variant none`/`low`/`medium`/`high`/`xhigh`.
- **GPT-5.6 Fast variants are first-class slugs.** The GPT-5.5 note described its Fast variant under the base slug. It used `experimental.modes:['fast']` in the model cache. That representation was not carried forward for GPT-5.6 without independent verification. Use `opencode models openai` to confirm live slugs and any cache-representation quirks.
- **The Pro effort restriction remains unmeasured.** The documented `gpt-5.5-pro` range was `medium`, `high` and `xhigh`. Base and Fast allowed `none` through `xhigh`. The restriction now covers `gpt-5.6-pro`, `gpt-5.6-sol-pro`, `gpt-5.6-terra-pro` and `gpt-5.6-luna-pro` as a documentation default. `opencode models openai` lists slugs but not variant ranges. `gpt-5.5-pro` is no longer in the live catalog to re-check. So the inline caveat in `references/cli-reference.md` §5 says to try `--variant low` on a GPT-5.6 Pro slug before relying on the restriction. `SKILL.md` directs readers to that caveat.
- **Copilot deep-review guidance calls for an availability check.** Layer 4 of `references/destructive-scope-violations.md` and ALWAYS rule 15 recommend `cli-copilot` with `gpt-5.6-sol --reasoning-effort high`. They recommend it for multi-phase deep-review dispatches. `opencode models openai` does not cover Copilot. The only recorded Copilot-catalog check was for `gpt-5.5`. So availability of `gpt-5.6-sol` remains unverified. Both locations tell readers to verify the Copilot surface and choose an available Copilot model. That is because an unavailable slug could silently fall back to the risky default. No `cli-copilot` skill exists under `.opencode/skills/`. This predates the update and is out of scope.
- **Historical GPT-5.5 changelog entries remain unchanged.** `changelog/v1.1.0.0.md` and `changelog/v1.2.0.0.md` keep past-tense facts. These include the 2026-06 environmental test-skip note. They also include the `github-copilot/gpt-5.4` and `gpt-5.5` live-verification and default-switch narrative. That is because they describe historical state rather than the current roster.

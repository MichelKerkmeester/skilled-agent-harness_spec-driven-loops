# cli-opencode v1.3.15.2, The GPT-5.6 Model Roster

cli-opencode v1.3.15.2 updates the OpenAI model-selection guidance readers use when choosing a dispatch model.

## Why This Release

GPT-5.5 is no longer the live OpenAI premium option this skill should recommend. GPT-5.6 expanded from one three-tier line into a bare tier plus Sol, Terra and Luna, so retaining GPT-5.5 slugs would route dispatches at a superseded family and documenting only Sol and Terra would hide the live Luna family and the bare tier.

## What's New at a Glance

- **GPT-5.6 replaces GPT-5.5 in active OpenAI guidance.** The previous family was `openai/gpt-5.5`, `openai/gpt-5.5-fast` and `openai/gpt-5.5-pro`. `opencode models openai` confirmed the twelve live slugs now listed in `SKILL.md`'s Authentication options and Model Selection and `references/cli-reference.md`'s `--model` override example and `--variant` table: bare (`gpt-5.6`, `gpt-5.6-fast` and `gpt-5.6-pro`), Sol (`gpt-5.6-sol`, `gpt-5.6-sol-fast` and `gpt-5.6-sol-pro`), Terra (`gpt-5.6-terra`, `gpt-5.6-terra-fast` and `gpt-5.6-terra-pro`) and Luna (`gpt-5.6-luna`, `gpt-5.6-luna-fast` and `gpt-5.6-luna-pro`).
- **Sol is the flagship default.** Single-option default and premium references map `gpt-5.5` to `gpt-5.6-sol`, `gpt-5.5-fast` to `gpt-5.6-sol-fast` and `gpt-5.5-pro` to `gpt-5.6-sol-pro`. The provider-missing fallback (option A) and ALWAYS rule 3 override example point at the Sol tier, while the pre-flight fallback offer uses `gpt-5.6-sol-pro` and `README.md`'s FAQ answer to "Which model do I pick?" recommends `gpt-5.6-sol` for premium dispatch.
- **Model tier and reasoning effort are separate choices.** An earlier `SKILL.md` `Model Selection` draft incorrectly called base, Fast and Pro "reasoning tiers". The correction names them model tiers selected by the slug and describes the separate reasoning-effort lever in `references/cli-reference.md`'s `--variant` row, with settings `--variant none`/`low`/`medium`/`high`/`xhigh`.
- **GPT-5.6 Fast variants are first-class slugs.** The GPT-5.5 note described its Fast variant under the base slug with `experimental.modes:['fast']` in the model cache, a representation not carried forward for GPT-5.6 without independent verification. Use `opencode models openai` to confirm live slugs and any cache-representation quirks.
- **The Pro effort restriction remains unmeasured.** The documented `gpt-5.5-pro` range was `medium`, `high` and `xhigh`, while base and Fast allowed `none` through `xhigh`. The restriction now covers `gpt-5.6-pro`, `gpt-5.6-sol-pro`, `gpt-5.6-terra-pro` and `gpt-5.6-luna-pro` as a documentation default. `opencode models openai` lists slugs but not variant ranges, and `gpt-5.5-pro` is no longer in the live catalog to re-check, so the inline caveat in `references/cli-reference.md` §5, where `SKILL.md` directs readers, says to try `--variant low` on a GPT-5.6 Pro slug before relying on the restriction.
- **Copilot deep-review guidance calls for an availability check.** Layer 4 of `references/destructive-scope-violations.md` and ALWAYS rule 15 recommend `cli-copilot` with `gpt-5.6-sol --reasoning-effort high` for multi-phase deep-review dispatches. `opencode models openai` does not cover Copilot and the only recorded Copilot-catalog check was for `gpt-5.5`, so availability of `gpt-5.6-sol` remains unverified. Both locations tell readers to verify the Copilot surface and choose an available Copilot model because an unavailable slug could silently fall back to the risky default. No `cli-copilot` skill exists under `.opencode/skills/`. This predates the update and is out of scope.
- **Historical GPT-5.5 changelog entries remain unchanged.** `changelog/v1.1.0.0.md` and `changelog/v1.2.0.0.md` keep past-tense facts, including the 2026-06 environmental test-skip note and the `github-copilot/gpt-5.4` and `gpt-5.5` live-verification and default-switch narrative, because they describe historical state rather than the current roster.

v1.5.0.0 makes finding dedup title-aware and turns the lag-ceiling gauge into a true stall detector. Both fixes are byte-identical when their existing default-off flags are off, leaving activation for a separate evidence-gated decision.

> Spec folder: `.opencode/specs/system-spec-kit/028-memory-search-intelligence/007-dark-flag-graduation/008-deeploop-finding-dedup/`, `.opencode/specs/system-spec-kit/028-memory-search-intelligence/010-graduation-follow-ups/002-deeploop-gauges-dedup-scale/` and `.opencode/specs/system-spec-kit/028-memory-search-intelligence/011-followup-deep-review/`

## Why This Release

Benchmark 008 put three default-off capabilities through a production-path benchmark and gave graduate verdicts to finding dedup, the lag-ceiling gauge and the progress-heartbeat gauge. Its 17-record synthetic proof set measured precision 1.0 and distinct-recall 1.0 with zero false collapses. Follow-up stress work beyond that set found two defects the benchmark did not exercise, so a graduate verdict alone was not enough to flip the defaults.

## What's New at a Glance

- **Finding dedup now checks titles.** Before, findings with the same body but disjoint subject titles could collapse. `SPECKIT_FANOUT_NEAR_DUP_DEDUP` now collapses repeated bodies from different worker ids only when stopword-stripped title tokens meet a Jaccard overlap of at least 0.15, keeping the strongest severity and distinct conflicting content while separating disjoint subjects from same-point paraphrases that share a subject. The bucketer (`getFindingBucket`) was made title-aware to match. The dedup was scale-validated on a 75-finding free-text set across six workers that covered identical-body and varied-wording restatements, distinct singletons, body-near-miss pairs and title-distinct pairs. The title-only false-collapse rate had been 0.50, with three of six distinct points lost, and it fell to zero while identical-duplicate collapse held at 7 of 7 and body-distinguished distinct recall stayed at 1.0.
- **The lag-ceiling gauge now detects stalls.** It used to measure time since pool start, so a healthy fan-out wider than its concurrency falsely alerted on queue backpressure, including a healthy 10-wide pool at a 1500ms ceiling. It now measures time since the last completion while work is pending and resets on every settlement, staying silent during healthy completions and warning once on a genuine 5s stall with `metric: 'time_since_last_completion'`. The recommended ceiling moves from 5 minutes to 2 minutes (`120000ms`).
- **The progress-heartbeat gauge reports per-lineage events.** Its recommended cadence is 30 seconds, observed at roughly 955 records per hour on a 10-wide run against a 1500-per-hour operator-readable budget. The committed gauge defaults remain 0 because flipping them is a separate graduation step that must also migrate the silent-when-off suites.

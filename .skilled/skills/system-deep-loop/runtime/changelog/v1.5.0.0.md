v1.5.0.0 makes finding dedup title-aware. It also turns the lag-ceiling gauge into a true stall detector. Both fixes are byte-identical when their existing default-off flags are off. That leaves activation for a separate evidence-gated decision.

&nbsp;

## Why This Release

Benchmark 008 put three default-off capabilities through a production-path benchmark. It gave graduate verdicts to finding dedup, the lag-ceiling gauge and the progress-heartbeat gauge. Its 17-record synthetic proof set measured precision 1.0 and distinct-recall 1.0. It had zero false collapses. Follow-up stress work beyond that set found two defects. The benchmark did not exercise those defects. So a graduate verdict alone was not enough to flip the defaults.

&nbsp;

## What's New at a Glance

- Finding dedup now checks titles.
  - Before, findings with the same body could collapse.
  - Their subject titles were disjoint.
  - `SPECKIT_FANOUT_NEAR_DUP_DEDUP` now collapses repeated bodies from different worker ids only under one condition.
  - Stopword-stripped title tokens must meet a Jaccard overlap of at least 0.15.
  - It keeps the strongest severity and distinct conflicting content.
  - It separates disjoint subjects from same-point paraphrases that share a subject.
  - The bucketer (`getFindingBucket`) was made title-aware to match.
  - The dedup was scale-validated on a 75-finding free-text set across six workers.
  - That set covered identical-body and varied-wording restatements, distinct singletons, body-near-miss pairs and title-distinct pairs.
  - The title-only false-collapse rate had been 0.50.
  - Three of six distinct points were lost.
  - The title-only false-collapse rate fell to zero.
  - Identical-duplicate collapse held at 7 of 7.
  - Body-distinguished distinct recall stayed at 1.0.
- The lag-ceiling gauge now detects stalls.
  - It used to measure time since pool start.
  - So a healthy fan-out wider than its concurrency falsely alerted on queue backpressure.
  - That included a healthy 10-wide pool at a 1500ms ceiling.
  - It now measures time since the last completion while work is pending.
  - It resets on every settlement.
  - It stays silent during healthy completions.
  - It warns once on a genuine 5s stall with `metric: 'time_since_last_completion'`.
  - The recommended ceiling moves from 5 minutes to 2 minutes (`120000ms`).
- The progress-heartbeat gauge reports per-lineage events.
  - Its recommended cadence is 30 seconds.
  - That was observed at roughly 955 records per hour on a 10-wide run, against a 1500-per-hour operator-readable budget.
  - The committed gauge defaults remain 0.
  - That is because flipping them is a separate graduation step.
  - That step must also migrate the silent-when-off suites.

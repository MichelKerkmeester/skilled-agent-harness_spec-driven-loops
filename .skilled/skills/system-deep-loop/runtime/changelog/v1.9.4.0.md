The parser that research verification and the fan-out merge share counted every numbered line in a findings section, so a step listed under a finding counted as a finding of its own and an iteration's count could disagree with the findings it claimed. It now counts only findings that start at the left margin and also reads the F### bullet shape.

> Spec folder: `specs/sk-code/011-sk-code-poinytail-based-refinement/011-round-four-remediation/006-deep-loop-findings-parser` (Level 1)

&nbsp;

## What's New at a Glance

- **Sub-steps stay sub-steps.** A numbered line indented under a finding is read as part of that finding, not as a new one.
- **F### bullets are read.** A findings section written as `- **F001**:` bullets now yields its findings instead of none. A bullet with words between the id and the colon is a restatement and is not read, as in the review reducer.
- **One shape per section.** A section that restates its findings in a second shape counts each finding once.
- **Review findings count once.** The review reducer now lets an F### bullet yield to the structured row of its iteration that carries the same id, as numbered findings already yield to the rows of theirs, so a review folder whose narrative restated its structured rows counts each finding once and a finding only the narrative recorded is kept.

## Why This Release

The v1.1.0.0 release-cleanup pass, documented in `changelog/v1.1.0.0.md`, rewrote the README and fixed four focused `SKILL.md` issues, but its audit-and-validate phases missed findings later surfaced in the Phase 5 deep-research audit. The README rewrite also introduced or propagated errors, showing that structural validation checks template conformance rather than content accuracy. The deep-research loop is the fact-checker. The Phase 5 audit synthesis at `.../001-deep-loop-runtime/research/research.md` identified seven transverse patterns.

## What's New at a Glance

- **Documentation claims now match their sources.** The node-kind allow-list, cross-arc citations and Vitest count were corrected.
- **All 17 catalog descriptions were checked against their source modules.** Three drifted descriptions and matching playbook lines were corrected.
- **The council surface now has catalog and playbook coverage.** The feature catalog and manual-testing playbook both gained council content.
- **Targeted test coverage now reaches previously uncovered areas.** Council test coverage also expanded.

## Documentation Accuracy

#### Documentation Claims Corrected

Three high-impact errors came from or were propagated by the README rewrite. The fabricated `code-surface` node kind was replaced with the 10-kind source-of-truth allow-list. Cross-arc citations changed from `129/001` to `131/001/008 ADR-001` at six sites, including the changelog. The Vitest count changed from 22 to 27. Batch A's consolidated cleanup addressed 22 findings.

`references/integration-points.md` gained a §7.1 documenting seven previously hidden consumers and a cross-package test-discovery note. `graph-metadata.json` gained the council domain, `key_topics`, `key_files`, `entities`, `related_to`, a `causal_summary` mention and both changelogs in `source_docs`.

&nbsp;

#### Feature Descriptions Match Their Sources

All 17 `feature_catalog` descriptions were compared with their source-module exports. Three were corrected with matching playbook `OVERVIEW` lines: `fallback-router` now uses quota-pool framing, `bayesian-scorer` describes the split between `computeScore` and `shouldDemote` and `post-dispatch-validate` describes its full validation surface. The other 14 descriptions were accurate.

The measured drift rate was 3 of 17 (18 percent), below the 43 percent projection from Phase 5's sample of three.

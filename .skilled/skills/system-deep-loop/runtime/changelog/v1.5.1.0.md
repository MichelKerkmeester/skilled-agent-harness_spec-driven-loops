Review and research runs now finish through one close-out script instead of four inline copies that had drifted apart. The Cursor executor also accepts the Grok 4.7 models.

> Part of [Skilled v4.0.0.2](../../../../changelog/skilled/v4.0.0.2.md).
>
> Spec folder: `specs/system-skill-advisor/030-pi-skill-orchestrator-based-research-refinement` (Level 2)

## What's New at a Glance

- **One close-out for review and research.** `scripts/synthesis-closeout.cjs` replaces the copies that the review and research workflows each carried inline. Where the copies disagreed it keeps the stricter rule, so review now refuses a symlinked artifact and research also matches a finding by its `findingId` field.
- **The Cursor executor accepts Grok 4.7.** Eight Grok 4.7 model ids, low, medium, high and xhigh each with a `-fast` sibling, join the Cursor allowlist and its fan-out mirror. Each was confirmed by a live dispatch first.

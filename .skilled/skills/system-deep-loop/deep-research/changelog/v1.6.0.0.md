Phase 008 of the 042 Deep Research & Review Runtime Improvement Bundle closes the 12 P0/P1/P2 recommendations surfaced by the 20-iteration deep-research audit. It brings coverage-graph checks into the live `deep-research` loop and carries blocked-stop details into reducer views.

On the live path, `deep_loop_graph_upsert` and `deep_loop_graph_convergence` run in the auto and confirm workflows. Reducer-owned surfaces expose `graphConvergenceScore`, `graphDecision` and `graphBlockers`, while blocked-stop JSONL events appear in the findings registry, dashboard and strategy `next-focus` anchor.

## Why This Release

The workflow is meant to be easier to follow and easier to trust.

## What's New at a Glance

- **Blocked stops become recorded workflow events.** The `blocked_stop` event carries blocker and recovery context into reducers.
- **Pause reasons use named values.** Raw `paused` and `stuck_recovery` values become `userPaused` and `stuckRecovery`.
- **Graph evidence joins the stop decision.** The inline vote and graph decision must agree before STOP.
- **Reducers expose stop history and graph results.** The findings registry gains `blockedStopHistory` and graph-convergence fields.
- **Coverage-graph signals share one source.** The CJS helper adapts to the canonical graph-signal implementation.
- **The dashboard groups blocked stops and graph convergence.** Reducer-collected event data supplies both views.
- **Session-scoped reads separate graph results.** `sessionId` can be included in the aggregation scope.
- **A recovery fixture captures an interrupted research session.** Its third iteration is cut off mid-write.

## Deep Research Workflows

#### Blocked Stops Carry Context

Both research workflows emit a first-class `blocked_stop` JSONL event through `step_emit_blocked_stop`. Its `blockedBy`, `gateResults`, `recoveryStrategy` and full session run history give downstream reducers the exact blocker and recovery context.

&nbsp;

#### Pause Reasons Stay Constrained

At emission, `step_normalize_pause_events` maps raw `paused` and `stuck_recovery` to `userPaused` and `stuckRecovery` using the frozen `STOP_REASONS` enum. The live workflow no longer passes free-form strings into `synthesis_complete.stopReason`.

&nbsp;

#### Graph Checks Gate Stops

In `deep_research_auto.yaml` and `deep_research_confirm.yaml`, `deep_loop_graph_upsert` runs after `step_reduce_state` and persists iteration `graphEvents` to the SQLite store. `deep_loop_graph_convergence` runs before the inline three-signal vote in `step_check_convergence`, and `STOP_BLOCKED` routes to `blocked_stop` emission. The OpenCode `deep-research` agent gains explicit graph tool permissions, and the Claude agent mirrors them.

## Coverage Graph and Reducers

#### Graph Signals Share One Authority

The MCP handler is the main implementation. `scripts/lib/coverage-graph-convergence.cjs` is a thin adapter to the authoritative `mcp-server/lib/coverage-graph/coverage-graph-signals.ts` implementation, where `sourceDiversity`, `contradictionDensity`, `questionCoverage` and `evidenceDepth` are harmonized.

&nbsp;

#### Stop Events Reach Reducers

The reducer no longer filters non-iteration JSONL rows. Its `events` stream preserves `blocked_stop`, `userPaused` and `stuckRecovery` for the blocked-stop history builder and graph-convergence rollup builder.

&nbsp;

#### Graph Results Reach the Registry

`buildGraphConvergenceRollup(events)` consumes `graph_convergence` JSONL events and adds `graphConvergenceScore`, `graphDecision` and `graphBlockers` beside `convergenceScore`. Stop events also contribute `blockedStopHistory`. `state_format.md` §5 documents the registry fields, the `blocked_stop` and `graph_convergence` event schemas and the combined-stop rule.

&nbsp;

#### Dashboard Shows Both Outcomes

The render dashboard helper emits `BLOCKED STOPS` and `GRAPH CONVERGENCE` sections. Both use event data collected by the reducer.

&nbsp;

#### Next Focus Names Recovery

When a blocked stop is the latest loop event, the update strategy content helper overrides the `next-focus` anchor with its `recoveryStrategy`. Operators see the blocker and recovery hint before they choose the next iteration direction.

## Session-Scoped Graph Reads

#### Session Reads Stay Compatible

Shared coverage-graph reads in `coverage-graph-query.ts`, `coverage-graph/convergence.ts`, `coverage-graph/query.ts` and `coverage-graph/status.ts` accept `sessionId` and scope aggregation by `specFolder + loopType + sessionId`. When `sessionId` is absent, backward-compatible behavior is preserved.

## Recovery Scenarios

#### Interrupted Sessions Have a Fixture

The new `scripts/tests/fixtures/interrupted-session/` fixture is a deep-research packet with two complete iterations and a third truncated mid-write. It includes truncated Markdown and a malformed JSONL tail, and demonstrates reducer recovery under `--lenient`.

New manual-testing scenarios were added under `manual-testing-playbook/04--convergence-and-recovery/`: `blocked-stop-reducer-surfacing.md` and `graph-aware-stop-gate.md`.

## Upgrade Notes

No migration is required for existing research packets. The new registry fields (`blockedStopHistory`, `graphConvergenceScore`, `graphDecision` and `graphBlockers`) are additive only. Existing v1.5.0.0 packets resume cleanly under the new reducer logic and remain readable. New emissions use the normalized pause forms.

v1.3.0.0 splits review mode out of `deep-research` into the new standalone `deep-review` skill. `deep-research` is now research-only, and all of its review keywords, commands and references were removed or redirected.

## Why This Release

The split is intended to make the workflow easier to follow and easier to trust.

## What's New at a Glance

- **Review content has a dedicated skill.** `deep-review` owns the review-specific files and scenarios.
- **`/deep:research` uses `:auto` and `:confirm`.** Review examples and the review path now lead to `/deep:review`.
- **The quick reference redirects review guidance.** Its review section points to the `deep-review` reference.
- **Review keywords route to `deep-review`.** Research keywords were unchanged.
- **Review scenarios have a dedicated playbook.** The root manual testing playbook now has 30 scenarios.

---

## Deep Research Skill

#### Review Has a Separate Home

Review triggers, `REVIEW_*` intent signals, architecture diagrams, rules, success criteria and references were removed from `SKILL.md`. `deep_review_strategy.md`, `deep_review_dashboard.md` and `review_mode_expected behavior.yaml` moved to `deep-review`. Sections 1 and 8 now use the cross-reference `For code review, see deep-review`.

---

## Research Command

#### Review Uses Its Own Command

`/deep:research` no longer handles `:review` suffixes or asks review-specific setup questions. Its only mode suffixes are `:auto` and `:confirm`. The removed suffixes are `:review`, `:review:auto` and `:review:confirm`. Review examples and the command chain's review path now point to `/deep:review`.

---

## Reference Documentation

#### Review Guidance Has a Redirect

The 80-line Review Mode section in `references/quick-reference.md` was replaced by a redirect to `deep-review/references/quick-reference.md`. Its H2 sections were numbered according to `skill_reference_template.md`.

&nbsp;

#### Shared Protocols Keep Review Sections

Review-specific sections in `references/loop-protocol.md`, `convergence.md` and `state_format.md` were retained as shared protocol documentation. `deep-review` cross-references them.

---

## Skill Advisor Routing

#### Review Routes to Deep Review

Review keywords including `deep review`, `review mode`, `iterative review`, `code audit` and `:review` now route from `deep-research` to `deep-review` in both Public and Barter repos. The `autoresearch`, `deep research` and `research loop` keywords were unchanged.

---

## Manual Testing Playbooks

#### Review Scenarios Move to Deep Review

The `07--review-mode/` playbook moved to `deep-review` and was restructured from 11 scenarios into 28 across 6 categories. The manual testing playbook was reduced from 41 scenarios to 30 across 6 categories after review scenarios moved. The root playbook document removed Section 13 (Review Mode) and renumbered the remaining sections.

## Upgrade Notes

- **Breaking:** Replace `/deep:research:review` with `/deep:review`. The `:review` suffix is no longer recognized by the `deep-research` command.

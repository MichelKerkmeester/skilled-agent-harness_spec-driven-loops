# v2.1.5.0, Landed Iterations Are Not Dispatched Twice

A research or review iteration could be dispatched again after it had landed. The second worker would find its files written. It would then refuse to overwrite them. The loop waited on a run that could not succeed.

&nbsp;

## What's New at a Glance

- Three verify failures skip the redispatch. They are `executor_missing`, `findings_not_enumerated` and `ledger_backing_missing`.
- Those failures appear only after the iteration's narrative, state record and delta exist.
- Research and review follow the same rule.
- Every other failure still gets one redispatch, including route proof.

&nbsp;

## How It Was Checked

Seven new cases cover the landed-iteration skip across the verify, counter and reducer suites. Those suites pass. The existing append-only redispatch case still passes.

&nbsp;

## Upgrade

No migration required. Expect one dispatch fewer for these three failures.

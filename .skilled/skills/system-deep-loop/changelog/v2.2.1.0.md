# v2.2.1.0, Gemini 3.7 Flash High for Cursor and Devin

v2.2.1.0 adds Gemini 3.7 Flash High to the curated model choices for Cursor and Devin, making both model ids available in their respective scopes. The ids were confirmed verbatim in live `cursor-agent --list-models` and `devin models list` output on 2026-08-15. The rationale doc comments now record that probe dispatch returned a live model response for both ids with exit 0 on 2026-08-15, unlike the 2026-08-14 additions, which were list-verified only.

> Spec folder: `specs/cli-external-orchestration/043-roster-update-luna-deepseek-glm-gemini/004-gemini-3-7-flash-high/`

## What's New at a Glance

- **Cursor adds Gemini 3.7 Flash High.** `CURSOR_SUPPORTED_MODELS` grows from 20 to 21 with `gemini-3.7-flash-high`, the first Gemini id in Cursor's curated scope. Its display name is "Gemini 3.7 Flash" and the High tier is in the id suffix.
- **Devin adds Gemini 3.7 Flash High.** `DEVIN_SUPPORTED_MODELS` grows from 23 to 24 with `gemini-3-7-flash-high`, the first Gemini uid in Devin's curated scope and its sixth curated family.
- **The fanout-run mirrors stay in sync.** The hand-duplicated `CURSOR_ALLOWED_MODELS` / `DEVIN_ALLOWED_MODELS` mirrors in `runtime/scripts/fanout-run.cjs` stay byte-identical to the source allowlists in `runtime/lib/deep-loop/executor-config.ts`.
- **The allowlist enforcement mechanism is unchanged.** Its sort order and fail-closed rejection of off-list ids are unchanged. Cursor's `gemini-3.7-flash-low`/`-medium` and Devin's `gemini-3-7-flash-minimal`/`-low`/`-medium` tiers remain rejected.
- **Other executor model rosters do not change.** DeepSeek, GLM-5.2, GPT-5.6 Luna Max, Grok 4.5 / 4.6, SWE-1.7, the Pi roster and every other executor config surface are unchanged.

`system-deep-loop` v1.5 retires the alignment mode. It also retires the conformance-benchmark capability that mode powered. The mode key and commands were public surfaces. So their removal is a major release.

## Why This Release

The alignment mode was not standalone. It held the shared conformance engine. `/deep:command-benchmark` and the `/create:benchmark` conformance axis ran on that engine. Removing the mode alone would have left those surfaces pointing at a deleted engine. The operator approved the cascade.

---

## What's New at a Glance

- **The alignment mode and conformance capability are retired.** The removed 126-file `deep-alignment/` mode packet included the command-behavior matrix scheduler.
- **Conformance benchmarking leaves its public surfaces.** `/deep:alignment`, `/deep:command-benchmark` and the `/create:benchmark` conformance axis are removed.
- **`sk-create-benchmark` loses its conformance family.** Conformance authoring templates are removed with it.

---

## Deep Loop

#### The Hub Registers Six Modes

The `alignment` mode key is removed. The hub goes from seven registered modes to six. The surviving modes are `research`, `review`, `ai-council`, `agent-improvement`, `model-benchmark` and `skill-benchmark`. Their convergence math, state shapes and artifacts are unchanged. The improvement lanes are unchanged. `shipped-census` counts were reconciled from eight to seven. Alignment was removed from roughly twenty-five shared files. The `deep-alignment` leaf agent is removed across all six runtime directories. Three alignment state-backend rows were dropped from the frozen baseline census. That census is the packet-001 `state-backend-census.json`. Leaving the rows would have broken a surviving test.

---

## Benchmarking

#### Conformance Ends on Both Surfaces

Both `/deep:alignment` and `/deep:command-benchmark` are removed. The hub's `/deep:*` commands go from eight to six. The `/create:benchmark` `conformance_benchmark` axis is also removed. So is the `sk-create-benchmark` conformance family. `mcp_promotion` is the surviving `/create:benchmark` family.

&nbsp;

#### Other Benchmark Families Continue

The behavior, model, skill and agent-improvement benchmark families are unchanged. The shared `shared/behavior-benchmark/` framework is unchanged. It is independent of the retired mode.

---

## Documentation

#### References Match the New Scope

The hub now advertises six user modes and four workflow families. The runtime and shared-progress READMEs no longer name `alignment` as a live mode. So do the behavior-benchmark references and the benchmark authoring guide. The scenario template also no longer names `alignment` as a live mode. The behavior-benchmark prefix set is now `RSB`, `RVB`, `ACB` and `IMB`.

&nbsp;

#### Historical and Follow-Up Records

Historical changelogs, benchmark reports and spec packets keep their original references as immutable records. Two orphaned prompt mirrors survived the first removal pass. They sit under `.codex/prompts/` and `.pi/prompts/`. Dangling command-mirror symlinks also survived. They sit under `.claude/commands/deep/` and `.cursor/commands/`. Both survived because the mirror generators were not re-run. Review found both, and follow-up commits closed them.

---

## Upgrade Notes

- **Breaking:** Stop invoking `/deep:alignment` or `/deep:command-benchmark`. Neither has a replacement.
- `/create:benchmark` still authors `mcp_promotion` packages. A future need for conformance benchmarking requires reintroducing an engine.

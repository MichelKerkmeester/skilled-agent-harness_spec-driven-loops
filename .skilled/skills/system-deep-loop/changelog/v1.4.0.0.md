# v1.4.0.0, Expanded Cursor and Devin Model Rosters

v1.4.0.0 expands the curated rosters for `cli-cursor` and `cli-devin`. Every added id was confirmed present verbatim in live output. That output came from `cursor-agent --list-models` and `devin models list` on 2026-08-14. The rationale doc comments now record an operator decision. These ids were list-verified only. They were not dispatch-tested, unlike the Grok entries above them.

> Spec folder: `specs/cli-external-orchestration/043-roster-update-luna-deepseek-glm-gemini/`

## What's New at a Glance

- **Cursor adds its first GPT-5.6 persona.** Its curated scope adds `gpt-5.6-luna-max` and `gpt-5.6-luna-max-fast`. The count moves from 18 to 20 models.
- **Devin expands its curated roster.** It adds DeepSeek V4 max tiers `deepseek-v4-flash-max` and `deepseek-v4-pro-max`. It also adds GPT-5.6 ids `gpt-5-6-luna-max` and `gpt-5-6-luna-max-priority`. The roster goes from 19 to 23 models. Curated families go from four to five. Devin's Fast suffix is `-priority`.
- **The fanout-run mirrors stay in sync.** The mirrors are hand-duplicated. `CURSOR_ALLOWED_MODELS` and `DEVIN_ALLOWED_MODELS` live in `runtime/scripts/fanout-run.cjs`. They stay byte-identical to the source allowlists in `runtime/lib/deep-loop/executor-config.ts`.
- **Grok 4.5 / 4.6, GLM-5.2, SWE-1.7 and the Pi roster are unchanged.** The allowlist enforcement mechanism is unchanged. So are sort order and fail-closed rejection of off-list ids. Every other executor config surface is unaffected.

# v1.4.1.0, Gemini 3.7 Flash High for Cursor and Devin

v1.4.1.0 adds Gemini 3.7 Flash High to the curated model choices for Cursor and Devin. Both model ids are available in their respective scopes. The ids were confirmed verbatim in live `cursor-agent --list-models` and `devin models list` output on 2026-08-15. The rationale doc comments now record that probe dispatch returned a live model response for both ids. That response had exit 0 on 2026-08-15. That is unlike the 2026-08-14 additions, which were list-verified only.

&nbsp;

## What's New at a Glance

- Cursor adds Gemini 3.7 Flash High.
  - `CURSOR_SUPPORTED_MODELS` grows from 20 to 21 with `gemini-3.7-flash-high`.
  - That is the first Gemini id in Cursor's curated scope.
  - Its display name is "Gemini 3.7 Flash".
  - The High tier is in the id suffix.
- Devin adds Gemini 3.7 Flash High.
  - `DEVIN_SUPPORTED_MODELS` grows from 23 to 24 with `gemini-3-7-flash-high`.
  - That is the first Gemini uid in Devin's curated scope.
  - The Gemini uid is also Devin's sixth curated family.
- The fanout-run mirrors stay in sync.
  - The hand-duplicated `CURSOR_ALLOWED_MODELS` / `DEVIN_ALLOWED_MODELS` mirrors live in `runtime/scripts/fanout-run.cjs`.
  - They stay byte-identical to the source allowlists in `runtime/lib/deep-loop/executor-config.ts`.
- The allowlist enforcement mechanism is unchanged.
  - Its sort order is unchanged.
  - So is its fail-closed rejection of off-list ids.
  - Cursor's `gemini-3.7-flash-low`/`-medium` tiers remain rejected.
  - So do Devin's `gemini-3-7-flash-minimal`/`-low`/`-medium` tiers.
- Other executor model rosters do not change.
  - DeepSeek, GLM-5.2, GPT-5.6 Luna Max, Grok 4.5 / 4.6 and SWE-1.7 are unchanged.
  - So are the Pi roster and every other executor config surface.

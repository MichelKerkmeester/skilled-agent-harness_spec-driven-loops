`system-deep-loop` v3 retires the alignment mode and the conformance-benchmark capability it powered. The mode key and commands were public surfaces, making their removal a major release.

> Spec folder: `specs/system-deep-loop/036-deep-loop-innovation/025-deprecate-deep-alignment` (Level 2)

## Why This Release

The alignment mode was not standalone: it held the shared conformance engine that `/deep:command-benchmark` and the `/create:benchmark` conformance axis executed. Removing the mode alone would have left those surfaces pointing at a deleted engine, and the operator approved the cascade.

---

## What's New at a Glance

- **The alignment mode and conformance capability are retired.** The removed 126-file `deep-alignment/` mode packet included the command-behavior matrix scheduler.
- **Conformance benchmarking leaves its public surfaces.** `/deep:alignment`, `/deep:command-benchmark` and the `/create:benchmark` conformance axis are removed.
- **`sk-create-benchmark` loses its conformance family.** Conformance authoring templates are removed with it.

---

## Deep Loop

#### The Hub Registers Six Modes

The `alignment` mode key is removed, taking the hub from seven registered modes to six. The surviving modes are `research`, `review`, `ai-council`, `agent-improvement`, `model-benchmark` and `skill-benchmark`. Their convergence math, state shapes and artifacts are unchanged, as are the improvement lanes. `shipped-census` counts were reconciled from eight to seven. Alignment was removed from roughly twenty-five shared files. The `deep-alignment` leaf agent is removed across all six runtime directories. Three alignment state-backend rows were dropped from the frozen baseline census, the packet-001 `state-backend-census.json`, because leaving them would have broken a surviving test.

---

## Benchmarking

#### Conformance Ends on Both Surfaces

Both `/deep:alignment` and `/deep:command-benchmark` are removed, reducing the hub's `/deep:*` commands from eight to six. The `/create:benchmark` `conformance_benchmark` axis and the `sk-create-benchmark` conformance family are also removed. `mcp_promotion` is the surviving `/create:benchmark` family.

&nbsp;

#### Other Benchmark Families Continue

The behavior, model, skill and agent-improvement benchmark families are unchanged. The shared `shared/behavior-benchmark/` framework, which is independent of the retired mode, is unchanged.

---

## Documentation

#### References Match the New Scope

The hub now advertises six user modes and four workflow families. The runtime and shared-progress READMEs, behavior-benchmark references and benchmark authoring guide no longer name `alignment` as a live mode. The scenario template also no longer names `alignment` as a live mode. The behavior-benchmark prefix set is now `RSB`, `RVB`, `ACB` and `IMB`.

&nbsp;

#### Historical and Follow-Up Records

Historical changelogs, benchmark reports and spec packets keep their original references as immutable records. Two orphaned prompt mirrors under `.codex/prompts/` and `.pi/prompts/`, plus dangling command-mirror symlinks under `.claude/commands/deep/` and `.cursor/commands/`, survived the first removal pass because the mirror generators were not re-run. Both were found by review and closed in follow-up commits.

---

## Upgrade Notes

- **Breaking:** Stop invoking `/deep:alignment` or `/deep:command-benchmark`. Neither has a replacement.
- `/create:benchmark` still authors `mcp_promotion` packages. A future need for conformance benchmarking requires reintroducing an engine.

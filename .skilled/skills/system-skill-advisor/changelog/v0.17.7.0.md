# v0.17.7.0, A Confidence Floor Is No Longer a Tie

The advisor flagged 110 of 261 labelled routing prompts as ambiguous. Most of those flags came from the task-intent confidence floor, which lifts any skill with modest evidence to 0.82 and so within 0.05 of a top that earned its confidence. A typed `/deep:research :auto` prompt was flagged as well, against a skill the typed-command bound had held one millionth below the command's owner.

> Spec folder: `specs/system-speckit/054-weak-model-spec-authoring/024-typed-command-ambiguity` (Level 2)

&nbsp;

## What's New at a Glance

- **A floored skill clusters on score alone.** When the top earned its confidence from its own evidence, a skill whose confidence only reached a floor joins the ambiguity cluster through the 0.05 score margin, not the confidence margin. Two floored skills still cluster.
- **A held skill is not a tie.** A skill the typed-command bound capped below the command's owner records that owner as `heldBelow` and stays out of the owner's cluster.
- **The brief trusts the scorer.** It uses the scorer's `ambiguous` answer and re-derives only when the field is missing, and "vs" names a skill that is actually in the cluster.
- **Under-specified prompts still abstain.** The low-information abstention reads the wider cluster, so a skill the floor rule leaves out cannot become its top.
- **Measured over 261 labelled prompts.** Flags fell from 110 to 78, the correct top skill stayed at 202, and no top skill changed.

&nbsp;

## Upgrade

No action is needed. Scores and routing are unchanged; only the ambiguity flag and the brief's wording move.

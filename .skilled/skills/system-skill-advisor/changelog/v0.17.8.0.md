# v0.17.8.0, The Watcher Announces Its First Scan

The skill-graph watcher now says when its first scan has finished and its watches are armed. Before, a caller had no way to tell and waited a guessed interval, so a skill folder created too early could go unseen. The runtime's dependencies move to their current releases in the same step.

> Spec folder: `specs/system-speckit/053-ci-workflow-efficiency/011-ci-health-followups` (Level 2)

&nbsp;

## What's New at a Glance

- **The watcher reports when its first scan is done.** `ready` resolves once the scan has finished and the watches are armed, so a caller waits on a signal rather than on a delay. The listener is registered before any other, so a fast scan cannot announce itself before anyone is listening.
- **The runtime's dependencies are on current releases.** zod moves to 4.6.7 and vitest to 4.1.12. The lockfile brings vite to 8.3.5 with the audit fixes.

&nbsp;

## Upgrade

No action is needed. A caller that waits a fixed delay after creating the watcher can await `ready` instead.

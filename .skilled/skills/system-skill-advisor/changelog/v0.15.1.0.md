# v0.15.1.0, OpenCode Plugin Sends the Workspace Surface

The surface hint that `advisor_recommend` accepts since v0.15.0.0 now has a caller. The OpenCode plugin knows the session's workspace and renders the compiled route in its brief, so it is the one caller that can name the surface and show what the hint changes.

> Spec folder: `specs/sk-code/011-sk-code-poinytail-based-refinement/015-closeout/008-advisor-residuals-and-surface-hint` (Level 1)

&nbsp;

## What's New at a Glance

- **The plugin names the workspace surface.** A workspace inside the hub's `.skilled/` folder sends `OPENCODE`, one with an Obsidian `manifest.json` (`minAppVersion`) or `esbuild.config.mjs` sends `OBSIDIAN`, and one with `src/2_javascript/` or `wrangler.toml` sends `WEBFLOW`. Any other workspace sends no hint.
- **The hint only orders surfaces the prompt matched.** The front door keeps its targets and bundle kind. Without a hint the compiled tie-break order applies, as before.
- **The Claude and Codex hook briefs do not send one.** Their brief does not render compiled targets, so a hint would change nothing they show.

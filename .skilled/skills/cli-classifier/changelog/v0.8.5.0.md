# Long Prose Lines Are Now Short Sentences and Lists

Three lines in `SKILL.md` each carried a whole section in one sentence: the offline measurement note, the mode reference list and the surface router note. You can now read them a sentence at a time, and the reference list is a list.

The hub also carries a Terms glossary as the first subsection of How It Works, where each routing term it uses is defined once.

> Spec folder: `specs/sk-doc/072-skill-routing-and-clarity/010-prose-clarity-pass` (Level 2)

&nbsp;

## What's New at a Glance

- **The measurement note reads in three parts.** One sentence says what the benchmark asks, the next says what it costs to run, and the last says what the transport does with `choice` and `noul` questions.
- **The mode references are a list.** `cli-jev` and its four reference pages each sit on their own line under the mode bullet.
- **The surface router note is one sentence per line.** Stage two, what it maps, what a future mode adds and the two-layer split are now separate sentences.

## Upgrade

No action is needed. Every path, command, score and keyword is the same, and routing and behavior are unchanged.

# v2.0.6.0, One Vocabulary for Every Route

A prompt that stage one sends to a design mode now always reaches that mode's own guidance, because the root `ROUTER.md` intents carry every term `hub-router.json` routes to that mode. A new check fails when a copy of the vocabulary drifts from that one authored source.

> Spec folder: `specs/sk-doc/072-skill-routing-and-clarity/004-single-vocabulary-source` (Level 2)

&nbsp;

## What's New at a Glance

- **55 terms now reach their mode.** The root `ROUTER.md` stage-two intents carry every term `hub-router.json` routes to each mode, and their multi-word forms joined the `intent_signals` list in `graph-metadata.json`, where the skill advisor reads them.
- **The diagram and chart modes gained the words their packets advertised.** sk-design-diagram now routes `/design:diagram`, `create:diagram`, `theme diagram`, `repaint diagram`, `brand the diagram`, `data model`, `state machine`, `venn` and `mermaid`. sk-design-chart routes `theme a chart` and `measured site's look`.
- **One check keeps the copies honest.** `.skilled/skills/sk-doc/sk-create-skill/scripts/ci-hub-vocabulary-source.cjs` fails when the mode registry aliases, the root `ROUTER.md` intents or a packet's `Keyword triggers:` line disagree with `hub-router.json`.

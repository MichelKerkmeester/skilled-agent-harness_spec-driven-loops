The chart corpus now reads as a product card rather than a template: source instructions sit outside the visible source line, cartesian plots use a taller frame, tables fold behind a disclosure, findings show direction and large ticks use compact values with a declared unit.

## What's New at a Glance

- **Source instructions leave the visible line.** Every template puts the retarget instruction in a comment above `CHART_DATA`, where the `source-line` family keeps it.
- **Cartesian plots use taller frames.** Sixteen forms use a 392-unit frame, or 380 for the distribution strip, with baselines, ticks and legends moved to match. Ten shape-bound forms keep their heights, recorded per form.
- **Data tables fold behind a disclosure.** Every table sits in `<details class="data">` with a "Show the data" summary. The disclosure is open on inert forms and closed on tooltip forms. The `table-disclosure` family holds the change, and the three proof sheets follow.
- **Findings show their direction.** A `FINDING` block declares `trend`, and the footer draws an inline arrow for up or down. The `finding-cue` family holds the block and the read.
- **Large ticks use compact values and declared units.** `READOUT.unit` prints after the card value. `compact()` renders ticks at or above five digits as `12k` or `1.2M`, and the `number-format` family requires the unit field.
- **Contract fade figures match the shipped values.** The contract identifies the shipped single-area fade as 0.35 to 0.04 and flat 0.4 stacks as ours. It keeps 0.8 to 0.1 as the frozen shadcn measurement.

The chart corpus now reads as a product card, not a template. Source instructions sit outside the visible source line. Cartesian plots use a taller frame. Tables fold behind a disclosure. Findings show direction. Large ticks use compact values with a declared unit.

## What's New at a Glance

- **Source instructions leave the visible line.** Every template puts the retarget instruction in a comment above `CHART_DATA`. The `source-line` family keeps it there.
- **Cartesian plots use taller frames.** Sixteen forms use a 392-unit frame. The distribution strip uses 380. Baselines, ticks and legends moved to match. Ten shape-bound forms keep their heights, recorded per form.
- **Data tables fold behind a disclosure.** Every table sits in `<details class="data">` with a "Show the data" summary. The disclosure is open on inert forms. It is closed on tooltip forms. The `table-disclosure` family holds the change. The three proof sheets follow.
- **Findings show their direction.** A `FINDING` block declares `trend`. The footer draws an inline arrow for up or down. The `finding-cue` family holds the block and the read.
- **Large ticks use compact values and declared units.** `READOUT.unit` prints after the card value. `compact()` renders ticks at or above five digits as `12k` or `1.2M`. The `number-format` family requires the unit field.
- **Contract fade figures match the shipped values.** The contract identifies the shipped single-area fade as 0.35 to 0.04. It names flat 0.4 stacks as ours. It keeps 0.8 to 0.1 as the frozen shadcn measurement.

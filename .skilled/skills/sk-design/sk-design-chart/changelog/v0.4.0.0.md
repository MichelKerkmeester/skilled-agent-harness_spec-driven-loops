The chart corpus now carries one measured visual register. It runs from card chrome through marks, readouts and legends. It covers all twenty-six forms and their deliveries. This makes the browser-facing surface coherent. It keeps the existing data, palette and geometry contracts. Palette values, palette JSON, geometry blocks and numeric gates are preserved.

&nbsp;

## What's New at a Glance

- Every form uses a five-part, shadcn-informed card register.
  - The parts come in order: headline, subtitle, figure, footer and source.
  - Titles, descriptions, ticks, notes, hero values and ring totals use the measured type scale.
  - Findings and sources share the footer, with a consistent divider and gap.
- Tooltip-bearing forms render positioned HTML cards.
  - Each padded, rounded card sits inside the figure.
  - It carries a per-series indicator, the `READOUT` label and the formatted value.
  - Tooltip copy is no longer painted as SVG text.
- Multi-series forms render keyed legends.
  - Each declared series gets one centered entry.
  - The entry has an 8px keyed swatch, a muted 12px label and a 16px gap.
- Gallery frames pass the requested scheme to templates.
  - They pass `?scheme=light` or `?scheme=dark`.
  - They apply the corresponding `data-scheme` attribute inside the frame.
  - Templates can honour it consistently.
- Cartesian forms use measured axes and marks.
  - They use bare axes and muted tabular ticks.
  - They use an 8px tick margin and horizontal dashed grids.
  - They use 2px lines and the measured bar and area radii.
  - Single-series cartesian marks use the first categorical token.
  - Emphasis remains reserved for highlighted marks.
- Area fills use a 0.8-to-0.1 vertical gradient.
  - Stacked areas use flat 0.4 opacity.
  - Ordinary line points remain invisible unless the form's contract calls for a highlighted point.
- The corpus checker adds `legend` and `tooltip-card` families.
  - It retunes `type-scale`, `radius` and `card-parts` to the measured register.
  - No prior assertion floor is lowered.
- The render pass remains dependent on Chrome launching in the sandbox.
  - If Chrome cannot launch there, existing captures remain untouched for the conductor to regenerate.

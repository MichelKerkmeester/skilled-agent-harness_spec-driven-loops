Three questions the chart corpus could not answer now have forms: what a number has been doing lately, whether each period held and which of these is biggest when the names are long. `spark`, `tracker` and `bar-list` add those views.

## What's New at a Glance

- **`spark` adds a KPI-sized trend.** It has no axis, gridlines or tick labels, marks the last reading and carries the value in the metric header. Its three declared variants are line, area and bar, with twenty-four readings in a 176-unit frame.
- **`tracker` shows status across periods.** It uses one block per period across sixty periods, with a legend and tooltip per block. Green means held, amber degraded and the alarm colour failed. Status classes are named rather than indexed, so each status takes the colour its meaning asks for. The conductor moved `tracker` off the ordered ramp after capture showed three adjacent steps a reader could not tell apart at block size.
- **`bar-list` is a ranked list where long names cost no horizontal room.** Labels sit inside bars and slip outside when a bar is too short. Values are right-aligned in the mono stack and the largest row is emphasised.
- **The catalogue gives each new form a row.** Each row carries the reader question it answers. The corpus is 29 chart forms, and all three pass every existing family without needing a new one. GLM-5.3-Flash built one form per brief through pi.

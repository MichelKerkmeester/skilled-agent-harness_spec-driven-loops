The packet carried two Style References and one of them had stopped being true. The stock palette
has been derived from evilcharts since v0.18.0.0 and `--default` has themed from it since then, yet
the cursor copy was still on disk, three references still called it the stock, and the corner-ladder
argument was still made from its numbers. This round leaves one reference, drops the worked
deliveries, opens every data table, and fixes the gallery's frames.

## What Changed

#### Breaking

- **One Style Reference.** `assets/style-reference/cursor/` is removed. `assets/style-reference/
  evilcharts/` is the reference the stock palette is derived from, the one `--default` themes from,
  and the only one the packet carries. `SKILL.md`, `references/color-system.md` and
  `references/design-md-theming.md` said cursor in the places that matter and now say what the
  palette source and the applicator already did. The corner-ladder passage is rewritten from the
  ladder the applicator actually reproduces: 4.4px on track, swatch and pill, 8.4px on the card,
  the 2px mark corner the corpus's own.
- **No worked deliveries.** `assets/examples/` and its captures are removed; the templates are the
  corpus. The seven families scoped to templates-or-examples are scoped to templates, the one hand
  list that named a delivery no longer does, and the mutation case that read a themed delivery from
  disk now builds one with the applicator instead, so the fixture is made by the code under test.
- **Every data table starts open.** The disclosure used to follow the interaction register: closed
  wherever a tooltip answered the pointer, open only on inert forms. That left the tooltip as the
  only visible source of the values on twenty forms, one hover per number. `table-disclosure` now
  requires `open` on every form and says so; the contract and the checker comment carry the reason.

#### Fixes

- **The gallery clipped most of its frames.** Every frame was 560px tall, and most forms ran past
  it, so the finding and source were cut off on the majority of tiles — worse now that tables open.
  A page cannot measure a frame on disk, because each file is its own origin, so every form posts
  its own height to a parent on load and on every change, and the gallery sizes the frame that sent
  it. `frame-height` holds the template side, `gallery` holds the listener, and three mutation cases
  stand behind them.

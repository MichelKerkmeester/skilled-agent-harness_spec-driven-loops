sk-design-chart v0.21.0.0 aligns its palette guidance with the stock palette source, removes worked deliveries, makes table values directly visible and gives gallery frames the height their forms need.

## Why This Release

The packet carried two Style References and one of them had stopped being true. Data tables were closed wherever a tooltip answered the pointer, which left the tooltip as the only visible source of the values on twenty forms, one hover per number. Every gallery frame was 560px tall, and most forms ran past it.

## What's New at a Glance

- **The packet now carries one Style Reference.** It is evilcharts.
- **Worked deliveries are removed.** They came from `assets/examples/`, and their captures are removed too.
- **`table-disclosure` now requires `open` on every form.** Table values are visible without relying on a hover.
- **Gallery frames now fit each form.** The finding and source were cut off on the majority of tiles.

---

## Style Reference

#### One Source for the Palette

**Breaking:** `assets/style-reference/cursor/` is removed. The stock palette has been derived from evilcharts since v0.18.0.0, and `--default` has themed from it since then. Three references still called cursor the stock. `SKILL.md`, `references/color-system.md` and `references/design-md-theming.md` now say what the palette source and applicator already did. The corner-ladder argument had still been made from the cursor reference's numbers, and the passage now follows the ladder the applicator reproduces: 4.4px on the track, swatch and pill, 8.4px on the card and the corpus's own 2px mark corner.

---

## Templates and Tables

#### Templates Replace Deliveries

**Breaking:** The captures for `assets/examples/` are removed with the worked deliveries. The templates are now the corpus. Seven families scoped to templates-or-examples are now scoped to templates, and the hand list that named a delivery no longer does. The mutation case that read a themed delivery from disk now builds one with the applicator instead, so the fixture is made by the code under test.

&nbsp;

#### Values Visible Without Hovering

**Breaking:** Every data table starts open. `table-disclosure` now requires `open` on every form and says so. The disclosure used to follow the interaction register and was open only on inert forms. The contract and the checker comment carry the reason.

---

## Gallery

#### Frames Fit Their Forms

The clipping was worse after the tables opened. Because each file is its own origin, a page cannot measure a frame on disk. Each form posts its own height to a parent on load and on every change, and the gallery sizes the frame that sent it. `frame-height` holds the template side and `gallery` holds the listener.

## Upgrade Notes

No upgrade needed.

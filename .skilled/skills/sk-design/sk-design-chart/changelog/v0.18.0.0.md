This release corrects chart colors, wording and text that conflicted with what the figures showed. It also stops capturing the gallery's contact sheet.

## Why This Release

Every rendered capture was read by a fresh reviewer rather than by the person who changed the palette. Twelve defects came back. The checks had been green throughout.

## What's New at a Glance

- **A figure never spends its data marks on the ink.** The ranked system's first series now takes the hue and the ink becomes its emphasis.
- **Magnitude captions no longer reverse their ramps.** `calendar-grid`'s wording matches its legend.
- **`ramp-prose` rejects lightness-direction prose.** It excludes wording that is true in one theme and false in the other.
- **Dumbbell pairs now show movement direction.** The before is the quiet dot and the after is the bright one, so each pair leans the way it moved.
- **Dumbbell emphasis now follows the accompanying text.** The row that moved against the rest gets the emphasis.
- **Tracker severity now rises from teal through orange to red.** Its subtitle names held, degraded and failed.
- **The candlestick header arrow uses the correct color cue.** It no longer labels "up" with the token that means "closed down."
- **`independent-percentages`'s screen-reader description is corrected.** It gives the values reported by the chart.
- **Axis titles and treemap labels no longer obscure other content.** The axis keeps its leading digit and a treemap label stays within its cell.
- **A template note now describes its line accurately.** It says what the figure does and why the line is left alone.
- **The gallery is no longer captured.** What the sheet owes is completeness, and the `gallery` family asserts that against the directory.

## Palette and Scale

#### The Subject Gets Its Color

**Breaking:** Half the corpus drew its readings in the ink colour, with one accent on the mark the headline was about. The only colour in the picture sat on the exception and none on the subject. Eighteen forms change from near-white marks with one orange to orange marks with one white. The steps behind the first series are unchanged and rank by lightness as before.

&nbsp;

#### Separation Across Every Pair

**Breaking:** The first series carries the reading, so it is not part of the lightness ladder. Separation is still held across every adjacent pair, including the one between the hue and the first grey.

&nbsp;

#### Captions Follow Magnitude

On `calendar-grid`, the caption read "Darker is more" while the legend ran dark to bright from less to more. On a magnitude ramp, colour is the only encoding, so the caption inverted the figure's meaning. Four more sentences carried the same assumption: `bullet`'s deepening shades, `heat-matrix` and the calls delivery shading "darker for higher", and the ordered proof sheet's headline.

&nbsp;

#### Lightness Claims Are Refused

A magnitude ramp runs toward whichever ground the theme uses, so its strong end is darker on paper and lighter on ink. The lightness-direction wording was false in five places at once.

## Comparison and Status Cues

#### Before and After Show Direction

Both ends of every pair had the same colour, so nothing in the figure said which pipelines got faster. The headline's whole claim was unverifiable from the picture.

&nbsp;

#### The Reversing Row Gets Emphasis

The emphasis fell on the widest change. The sentence underneath was about the one pipeline that reversed.

&nbsp;

#### Status Colors Match Severity

After the palette change, a degraded day was drawn in a hotter colour than a failed one, while the subtitle still called it amber. The candlestick header arrow used the same token the legend defines as a down candle for "up". The arrow and legend were nine lines apart in one figure.

---

## Labels and Descriptions

#### Spoken Values Match the Data

The screen-reader description said ninety-four percent and eleven. The data, the bars and the finding all say 97 and 9.

&nbsp;

#### Axis and Treemap Text Stay Clear

An axis title painted over its own tick labels, eating the leading digit so 1,800 read as 800. A treemap label ran into the next cell when a name was wider than its cell.

---

## Chart Notes and Gallery

#### Notes Match Their Lines

The template's own note described a line that clears the columns. It crosses them from the sixth period on.

&nbsp;

#### A Page Replaces the Capture

A viewport-sized shot of the twenty-nine-form contact sheet showed its first tile, despite sitting in the capture set looking covered. A shot tall enough to hold all of it would be megabytes and rewritten on every palette change. The sheet is a page to open, not a picture to keep.

&nbsp;

#### Three Choices Stay Deliberate

Three choices were judged deliberate and left:

- the invisible surface swatch on the proof sheets
- `grouped-bars` marking its outlier by pattern rather than colour
- the ordered ramp sitting at its separation floor by construction

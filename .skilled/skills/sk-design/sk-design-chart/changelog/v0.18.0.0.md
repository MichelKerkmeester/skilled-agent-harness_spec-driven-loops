# v0.18.0.0, Charts Match Their Figures

Charts give the subject its own color and captions that match.

- The ranked first series takes the hue.
- The ink becomes its emphasis.
- Magnitude captions follow their legends.

Comparison and status figures show what their text claims.

- Dumbbell pairs lean the way they moved.
- Severity rises from teal through orange to red.
- The candlestick arrow uses the right color cue.

Labels, notes and the gallery match what they describe.

- Spoken values match the data.
- Axis and treemap text stays clear.
- The gallery becomes a page to open.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> 2 changes can break an existing setup. Read these before you update.
>
> 1. The ranked first series now takes the hue instead of ink. [The Subject Gets Its Color](#c-the-subject-gets-its-color)
> 2. The first series is not part of the lightness ladder. [Separation Across Every Pair](#c-separation-across-every-pair)

&nbsp;

## Why This Release

Every rendered capture was read by a fresh reviewer. That reviewer was not the person who changed the palette. Twelve defects came back. The checks had been green throughout.

- This release corrects chart colors, wording and text that conflicted with what the figures showed.
- It also stops capturing the gallery's contact sheet.

## Palette and Scale

#### The Subject Gets Its Color <a id="c-the-subject-gets-its-color"></a>

> [!WARNING]
> Half the corpus drew its readings in the ink colour.

- A figure never spends its data marks on the ink.
- One accent sat on the mark the headline was about.
- The only colour in the picture sat on the exception.
- None sat on the subject.
- The ranked system's first series now takes the hue.
- The ink becomes its emphasis.
- Eighteen forms change from near-white marks with one orange to orange marks with one white.
- The steps behind the first series are unchanged.
- They rank by lightness as before.

#### Separation Across Every Pair <a id="c-separation-across-every-pair"></a>

> [!WARNING]
> The first series carries the reading. So it is not part of the lightness ladder.

- The first series carries the reading. So it is not part of the lightness ladder.
- Separation is still held across every adjacent pair.
- That includes the pair between the hue and the first grey.

#### Captions Follow Magnitude <a id="c-captions-follow-magnitude"></a>

- On `calendar-grid`, the caption read "Darker is more".
- The legend ran dark to bright from less to more.
- On a magnitude ramp, colour is the only encoding.
- So the caption inverted the figure's meaning.
- Four more sentences carried the same assumption.
- `bullet`'s deepening shades is one.
- So is `heat-matrix` and the calls delivery shading "darker for higher".
- So is the ordered proof sheet's headline.
- Magnitude captions no longer reverse their ramps.
- `calendar-grid`'s wording matches its legend.

#### Lightness Claims Are Refused <a id="c-lightness-claims-are-refused"></a>

- A magnitude ramp runs toward whichever ground the theme uses. So its strong end is darker on paper and lighter on ink.
- The lightness-direction wording was false in five places at once.
- `ramp-prose` rejects lightness-direction prose.
- It excludes wording that is true in one theme and false in the other.

## Comparison and Status Cues

#### Before and After Show Direction <a id="c-before-and-after-show-direction"></a>

- Both ends of every pair had the same colour. So nothing in the figure said which pipelines got faster.
- The headline's whole claim was unverifiable from the picture.
- Dumbbell pairs now show movement direction.
- The before is the quiet dot.
- The after is the bright one.
- So each pair leans the way it moved.

#### The Reversing Row Gets Emphasis <a id="c-the-reversing-row-gets-emphasis"></a>

- The emphasis fell on the widest change.
- The sentence underneath was about the one pipeline that reversed.
- Dumbbell emphasis now follows the accompanying text.
- The row that moved against the rest gets the emphasis.

#### Status Colors Match Severity <a id="c-status-colors-match-severity"></a>

- After the palette change, a degraded day was drawn in a hotter colour than a failed one.
- The subtitle still called it amber.
- The candlestick header arrow used the same token the legend defines as a down candle for "up".
- The arrow and legend were nine lines apart in one figure.
- Tracker severity now rises from teal through orange to red.
- Its subtitle names held, degraded and failed.
- The candlestick header arrow uses the correct color cue.
- It no longer labels "up" with the token that means "closed down."

&nbsp;

## Labels and Descriptions

#### Spoken Values Match the Data <a id="c-spoken-values-match-the-data"></a>

- The screen-reader description said ninety-four percent and eleven.
- The data, the bars and the finding all say 97 and 9.
- `independent-percentages`'s screen-reader description is corrected.
- It gives the values reported by the chart.

#### Axis and Treemap Text Stay Clear <a id="c-axis-and-treemap-text-stay-clear"></a>

- An axis title painted over its own tick labels.
- It ate the leading digit, so 1,800 read as 800.
- A treemap label ran into the next cell.
- That happened when a name was wider than its cell.
- Axis titles and treemap labels no longer obscure other content.
- The axis keeps its leading digit.
- A treemap label stays within its cell.

&nbsp;

## Chart Notes and Gallery

#### Notes Match Their Lines <a id="c-notes-match-their-lines"></a>

- The template's own note described a line that clears the columns.
- It crosses them from the sixth period on.
- A template note now describes its line accurately.
- It says what the figure does and why the line is left alone.

#### A Page Replaces the Capture <a id="c-a-page-replaces-the-capture"></a>

- A viewport-sized shot of the twenty-nine-form contact sheet showed its first tile.
- It did so despite sitting in the capture set looking covered.
- The sheet is a page to open, not a picture to keep.
- The gallery is no longer captured.
- What the sheet owes is completeness.
- The `gallery` family asserts that against the directory.

The mutation suite covered the families this line of work had touched and left the other thirty resting on nobody having broken them yet. It now covers every family the checker registers. It also checks that its cases and exemptions match those families and reaches families the usual route cannot.

## What's New at a Glance

- **All but five registered families have mutation cases.** The five families that need a browser are named with the reason they cannot have one. Coverage is one case per family, not per assertion. A family with six assertions is held against one of them. That is a floor, and it is stated rather than implied.
- **The suite checks that cases and exemptions stay current.** Adding a family without a case fails with its name. A case for a family that no longer exists or an exemption for a family that has gone also fails.
- **Hard-to-reach families use a package-copy route.** Several families are scoped to shipped directories. One family fails on any copy because two files then declare the same identity. These cases mutate the file where it lives, inside a package copy.

No migration required.

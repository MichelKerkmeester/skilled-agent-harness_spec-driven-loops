Four of the five things the last release called boundaries turned out to be reachable. This release holds those four and settles the fifth with a measurement instead of leaving it open.

## What's New at a Glance

- **Ramp interiors are held by even steps.** Eight values previously sat outside the derivation as "placed by equal contrast" with no check on evenness. Steps now have to stay within 0.08 of one another. The shipped ramps hold at 0.010 and 0.015. A rung pushed toward either end fails.
- **A guide has to open its card on a reading.** This was called a runtime fact no static check could see, twice. It is visible: a form registers its cards from its readings or from its series, and the two loops are distinguishable. `stacked-area` flipped to `guide: true` now fails on the exact reason it declares `false`.
- **The evilcharts reference can be traced to its source.** The two custom-property blocks it was read from are now carried beside it as `source-globals.css`. They are hash-pinned like everything else in that directory. Each converted hex can be checked against the `oklch()` it came from without reaching for the library clone.
- **The reference-drawing path is tested.** Nine of the eleven forms carrying a `REFERENCE` block ship an empty list, so that loop had never run. A test now renders one of them with an entry injected and asserts the rule and its label reach the document.
- **The corner ladder stays a floor.** Its reason is now stated. Mapping by the reference's own element names was measured and rejected. The stock reference publishes `cards` at 4px and `modals` at 8px. The corpus card rung is 8px because the stock ladder deliberately took the larger value. A name mapping would hand the card rung 4px. The stock reference would stop reproducing the stock ladder, the one property that says the default is still the reference the corpus came from.

Four of the five things the last release called boundaries turned out to be reachable. They are
held now, and the fifth is settled with a measurement rather than left as an open question.

## What Changed

#### New Features

- **The ramp interiors are held by the arithmetic they were exempted under.** Eight values sat outside the derivation as "placed by equal contrast" with nothing checking the evenness. Their steps now have to stay within 0.08 of each other; the shipped ramps hold 0.010 and 0.015, and a rung pushed toward either end fails.
- **A guide has to open its card on a reading.** This was called a runtime fact no static check could see, twice. It is visible: a form registers its cards from its readings or from its series, and the two loops are distinguishable. `stacked-area` flipped to `guide: true` now fails on the exact reason it declares `false`.
- **The evilcharts reference is traceable.** The two custom-property blocks it was read from are carried beside it as `source-globals.css`, hash-pinned like everything else in that directory, so every converted hex can be checked against the `oklch()` it came from without reaching for the library clone. All eight sampled conversions reproduce from it exactly.
- **The reference-drawing path is tested.** Nine of the eleven forms carrying a `REFERENCE` block ship an empty list, so that loop had never run. A test now renders one of them with an entry injected and asserts the rule and its label reach the document.

#### Notes

- **The corner ladder stays a floor, and now says why.** Mapping by the reference's own element names was measured and rejected: the stock reference publishes `cards` at 4px and `modals` at 8px, and the corpus card rung is 8px because the stock ladder deliberately took the larger. A name mapping would hand the card rung 4px, and the stock reference would stop reproducing the stock ladder — the one property that says the default is still the reference the corpus came from.

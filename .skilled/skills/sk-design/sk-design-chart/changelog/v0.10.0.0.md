Four chart choices readers could misread were implicit in the paint code. Each is now declared beside the data. A check holds each one. Card colours are corrected. One value label's placement is corrected too. So readers can tell measures apart. They can also read the label separately from its mark.

## What's New at a Glance

- **`MARKS` now declares each form's mark choices.** It sets `points` to `none`, `sparse` or `all`. It sets `fill` to `none`, `gradient` or `flat`. It sets `zero` to `baseline` or `meaningful`. One line explains why for each. The `mark-policy` check holds it.
- **Each declared series names its key kind.** `indicator`, `swatch` or `rule` sets the kind drawn by the legend chip and the card row. So a stroked series no longer looks like one more bar. The vocabulary has two kinds, not three. A card whose rows are statistics of one series already carries `data-single-series` in its markup. It draws no key. So `none` would repeat what the markup says. The `tooltip-indicator` check holds key kinds.
- **`REFERENCE` marks the levels a headline claims.** Forms built on one vertical value scale and a drawn grid declare `{ value, label, why }` lines across the plot. Each line is labeled at the right edge. `daily-line` marks the first week's average. `orders-after-the-price-change` marks the level before the change. Both are the levels their headlines claim. The `reference-line` check holds `REFERENCE`. The planned consumer for `REFERENCE` was `bullet`. It turned out not to be one. Its target is per row against four different scales. A chart-wide list cannot express that. It stays in its data. `daily-line` is the worked case instead.
- **`guide` adds a cursor hairline where a chart can use it.** It is declared in `MARKS`. It marks the hovered reading. It is permitted only with two or more series or more than twenty readings. The `cursor-guide` check holds the cursor guide. `stacked-area` clears the guide's density bar four times over. But it declares `false`. Its card opens on a band rather than a column. So a hairline would sit at the band's middle every time. Density is necessary and not sufficient. The check cannot see the rest.
- **Card rows now carry their own series token.** Previously, every row in a multi-series card took the hovered series' colour. So in `bar-line-composed` and `population-pyramid`, both figures appeared to belong to the mark under the pointer. One belonged to the other measure. This also fixed a card that had always painted every status the same colour.
- **The orders delivery's marked-low value now sits below its mark.** Its previous position was beside the dot, at the dot's own height. That made it read as part of the mark. The new position follows the template the chart was built from.

## Upgrade

No migration required.

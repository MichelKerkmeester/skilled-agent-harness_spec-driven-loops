Four chart choices readers could misread were implicit in the paint code. Each is now declared beside the data and held by a check. Card colours and one value label's placement are corrected so readers can distinguish measures and read the label separately from its mark.

## What's New at a Glance

- **`MARKS` now declares each form's mark choices.** It sets `points` to `none`, `sparse` or `all`, `fill` to `none`, `gradient` or `flat`, and `zero` to `baseline` or `meaningful`, with one line explaining why for each. It is held by the `mark-policy` check.
- **Each declared series names its key kind.** `indicator`, `swatch` or `rule` determines the kind drawn by both the legend chip and card row, so a stroked series no longer looks like one more bar. The vocabulary has two kinds, not three: a card whose rows are statistics of one series already carries `data-single-series` in its markup and draws no key, so `none` would repeat what the markup says. Key kinds are held by the `tooltip-indicator` check.
- **`REFERENCE` marks the levels a headline claims.** Forms built on one vertical value scale and a drawn grid declare `{ value, label, why }` lines across the plot, labeled at the right edge. `daily-line` marks the first week's average. `orders-after-the-price-change` marks the level before the change. Both are the levels their headlines claim. `REFERENCE` is held by the `reference-line` check. The planned consumer for `REFERENCE` was `bullet`, and it turned out not to be one: its target is per row against four different scales, which a chart-wide list cannot express. It stays in its data, and `daily-line` is the worked case instead.
- **`guide` adds a cursor hairline where a chart can use it.** Declared in `MARKS`, it marks the hovered reading and is permitted only with two or more series or more than twenty readings. The cursor guide is held by the `cursor-guide` check. `stacked-area` clears the guide's density bar four times over but declares `false`: its card opens on a band rather than a column, so a hairline would sit at the band's middle every time. Density is necessary and not sufficient, and the check cannot see the rest.
- **Card rows now carry their own series token.** Previously, every row in a multi-series card took the hovered series' colour, making both figures appear to belong to the mark under the pointer in `bar-line-composed` and `population-pyramid` when one belonged to the other measure. This also fixed a card that had always painted every status the same colour.
- **The orders delivery's marked-low value now sits below its mark.** Its previous position beside the dot at the dot's own height made it read as part of the mark, and the new position follows the template the chart was built from.

## Upgrade

No migration required.

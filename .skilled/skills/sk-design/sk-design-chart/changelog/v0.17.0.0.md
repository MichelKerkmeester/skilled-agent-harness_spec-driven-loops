# sk-design-chart v0.17.0.0

v0.17.0.0 shifts the chart corpus from cursor to evilcharts.

- Every form changes colour.
- Every delivered file looks different.
- Flame, teal, violet and rose colour the marks.
- Each hue clears a mark on both grounds.

The dark ground changes to `#141110` from the reference's `#090909`.

- It forced only one dark value to move.
- That value was the fourth neutral step.

The ranked neutral ladder clears its rank-readability floor.

- The check now refuses anything under the 1.3 floor.
- Dark-token rows now map nine roles.
- The rows no longer carry their theme outside the backticks.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> 3 changes can break an existing setup. Read these before you update.
>
> 1. `--default` reads the evilcharts stock register. [Evilcharts Sets the Stock](#c-evilcharts-sets-the-stock)
> 2. The derivation records the ground as a decision. [The Dark Ground Decision](#c-the-dark-ground-decision)
> 3. The neutral system encodes importance. [Categories Replace Neutral Greys](#c-categories-replace-neutral-greys)

&nbsp;

## Why This Release

The corpus is derived from evilcharts now, not cursor.

- One form changes what it was claiming about its data.

## Chart Palette

#### Evilcharts Sets the Stock <a id="c-evilcharts-sets-the-stock"></a>

> [!WARNING]
> `--default` reads the evilcharts stock register.

- Near-white paper, near-black ink and hairline grey rules frame the marks.
- All colour is spent on them.
- The cursor capture is carried beside the stock as an alternative.

#### The Dark Ground Decision <a id="c-the-dark-ground-decision"></a>

> [!WARNING]
> The derivation records the ground as a decision.

- Pure black reads harsh behind a card of figures.
- It is not a gate-forced departure.
- It adds a fourth origin beside three others: verbatim, gate-forced departure and arithmetic.

#### Categories Replace Neutral Greys <a id="c-categories-replace-neutral-greys"></a>

> [!WARNING]
> The neutral system encodes importance.

- But men against women is not a ranking.
- `population-pyramid` was claiming an order the data does not have.
- It was paying for that with two adjacent greys.
- `population-pyramid` switches from neutral to categorical colour.
- The template changes what it claims about its data.

&nbsp;

## Chart Checks and Application

#### Ranked Steps Clear the Floor <a id="c-ranked-steps-clear-the-floor"></a>

- The neutral system's dark steps sat 1.18 and 1.26 apart.
- That is under the 1.3 rank-readability floor.
- Separation was asserted for the magnitude system only.
- The direction check added for ranked systems never gained its pair.
- Two bars meant to be told apart came out one colour.
- Every gate passed, because each step cleared the ground on its own.
- The new ladder holds 2.48, 1.81 and 1.41.

#### Dark Token Rows Become Readable <a id="c-dark-token-rows-become-readable"></a>

- The evilcharts reference placed dark-token theme names outside backticks.
- So every parser reading the table skipped them.
- The cells became single spans.
- That made nine roles mappable.

#### The Applicator Derives One System <a id="c-the-applicator-derives-one-system"></a>

- A stock palette could not be applied by running the applicator alone.
- The stock palette needs three systems on two grounds with a ramp.
- The applicator derives one system.
- The ladder, ramp and departures were derived and gated here.

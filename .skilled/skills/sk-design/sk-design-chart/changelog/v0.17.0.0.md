The corpus is derived from evilcharts now, not cursor. Every form changes colour, and one of them
changes what it was claiming about its data.

## What Changed

#### Breaking

- **The stock register is evilcharts.** Near-white paper and near-black ink, hairline grey rules, and all of the colour spent on the marks: flame, teal, violet and rose, the four hues that clear a mark on both grounds. `--default` reads it. Every delivered file looks different.
- **The dark ground is `#141110`, not the reference's `#090909`.** Pure black reads harsh behind a card of figures. This is a decision rather than a gate, so the derivation records it as one: a fourth origin beside verbatim, gate-forced departure and arithmetic, and the only dark value it forced to move was the fourth neutral step.
- **`population-pyramid` is categorical, not neutral.** Men against women is not a ranking, and the neutral system encodes importance. It was claiming an order the data does not have, and paying for it with two adjacent greys.
- **The cursor capture is now the alternative,** carried beside the stock and applied by naming its path.

#### Fixes

- **The ranked ladder's steps are held apart.** The neutral system's dark steps sat 1.18 and 1.26 apart against each other, under the 1.3 rank-readability floor, and nothing checked it: separation was asserted for the magnitude system only, and the direction check added for ranked systems never gained its pair. Two bars a reader was meant to tell apart came out one colour, with every gate passing because each step cleared the ground on its own. The new ladder holds 2.48, 1.81 and 1.41, and the check now refuses anything under the floor.

#### Notes

- The evilcharts reference's dark token rows carried their theme outside the backticks, so every parser that reads that table skipped them. Nine roles were unmappable until the cells were made single spans.
- Applying it was not a matter of running the applicator: a stock palette needs three systems on two grounds with a ramp, and the applicator derives one system. The ladder, the ramp and the departures were derived here and gated here.

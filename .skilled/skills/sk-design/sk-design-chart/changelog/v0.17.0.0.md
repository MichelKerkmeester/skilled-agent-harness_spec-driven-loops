v0.17.0.0 shifts the chart corpus from cursor to evilcharts. Every form changes colour. Every delivered file looks different.

## Why This Release

The corpus is derived from evilcharts now, not cursor. One form changes what it was claiming about its data.

## What's New at a Glance

- **Flame, teal, violet and rose colour the marks.** Each hue clears a mark on both grounds.
- **The dark ground changes to `#141110` from the reference's `#090909`.** It forced only one dark value to move. That value was the fourth neutral step.
- **`population-pyramid` switches from neutral to categorical colour.** The template changes what it claims about its data.
- **The ranked neutral ladder clears its rank-readability floor.** The check now refuses anything under the 1.3 floor.
- **Dark-token rows now map nine roles.** The rows no longer carry their theme outside the backticks.
- **The stock palette needs three systems on two grounds with a ramp.** The applicator derives one system.

---

## Chart Palette

#### Evilcharts Sets the Stock

**Breaking:** `--default` reads the evilcharts stock register. Near-white paper, near-black ink and hairline grey rules frame the marks. All colour is spent on them. The cursor capture is carried beside the stock as an alternative.

&nbsp;

#### The Dark Ground Decision

Pure black reads harsh behind a card of figures. **Breaking:** The derivation records the ground as a decision. It is not a gate-forced departure. It adds a fourth origin beside three others: verbatim, gate-forced departure and arithmetic.

&nbsp;

#### Categories Replace Neutral Greys

**Breaking:** The neutral system encodes importance. But men against women is not a ranking. `population-pyramid` was claiming an order the data does not have. It was paying for that with two adjacent greys.

---

## Chart Checks and Application

#### Ranked Steps Clear the Floor

The neutral system's dark steps sat 1.18 and 1.26 apart. That is under the 1.3 rank-readability floor. Separation was asserted for the magnitude system only. The direction check added for ranked systems never gained its pair. Two bars meant to be told apart came out one colour. Every gate passed, because each step cleared the ground on its own. The new ladder holds 2.48, 1.81 and 1.41.

&nbsp;

#### Dark Token Rows Become Readable

The evilcharts reference placed dark-token theme names outside backticks. So every parser reading the table skipped them. The cells became single spans. That made nine roles mappable.

&nbsp;

#### The Applicator Derives One System

A stock palette could not be applied by running the applicator alone. The ladder, ramp and departures were derived and gated here.

On request, the gallery is gone. So is the work that existed only to serve it.

&nbsp;

## What's New at a Glance

- `assets/gallery.html` and `scripts/build-gallery.cjs` are removed.
  - The packet's own contract already says a gallery is a workbench.
  - The gallery is never a deliverable.
- Height-poster scripts are removed from every template.
  - Twenty-nine forms carried a script.
  - The script's only reader was the gallery's frame sizer.
  - The `?scheme=` reader beside it stays for the screenshot renderer.
- The `gallery` and `frame-height` families are removed, with the renderer's gallery exclusion.
  - Nothing framed the corpus any more.
  - So nothing had a frame to hold.
- Screenshots are now the review surface.
  - There is one capture per form.
  - That replaces the gallery's two views, one for each colour scheme.
- Every screenshot capture was re-rendered from the final sources.
  - The set was rebuilt from empty: 32 sources, 32 captures, none stale.

&nbsp;

## Upgrade

No migration required.

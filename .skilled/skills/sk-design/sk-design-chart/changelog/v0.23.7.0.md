# v0.23.7.0, The References Describe Only the Chart Work

The catalog, colour system, template contract and the evilcharts origin note no longer cite research files or name the phase that made a decision. The decisions stay.

> Spec folder: `specs/sk-design/021-deprecate-fundamentals` (Level 2)

&nbsp;

## What's New at a Glance

- **The role split states its own case.** The colour system keeps the standalone categorical gates and their measured hue and contrast figures, without the comparison that produced them.
- **Radar and pie stay out on their reasoning,** with no phase or source line attached.
- **The stacked-area card is one row,** stated as the rule rather than as a correction to an earlier figure.

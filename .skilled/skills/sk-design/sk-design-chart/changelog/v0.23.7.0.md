# v0.23.7.0, The References Describe Only the Chart Work

The catalog, colour system, template contract and the evilcharts origin note no longer cite research files. They no longer name the phase that made a decision. The decisions stay.

> Spec folder: `specs/sk-design/021-deprecate-fundamentals` (Level 2)

&nbsp;

## What's New at a Glance

- **The role split states its own case.** The colour system keeps the standalone categorical gates. It keeps their measured hue and contrast figures. The comparison that produced them is gone.
- **Radar and pie stay out on their reasoning.** No phase or source line is attached.
- **The stacked-area card is one row.** It states the rule, not a correction to an earlier figure.

The stock chart register is now the cursor Style Reference.

## What's New at a Glance

- **Palette and corners follow the reference.** Every template, delivery and proof sheet carries the reference's parchment, ink and accents in its stock palette blocks and rounds its corners on its ladder. `assets/color/palettes.json` chrome, dark chrome, corner ladder and the three systems are derived from `sk-design-md-generator/styles/library/bundles/cursor/DESIGN.md`, and the source names the four values moved to clear a gate.
- **Body text uses the reference's typeface stack.** Every stock file sets its body text in `CursorGothic, Inter, system-ui, "Helvetica Neue", sans-serif`. The mono stack for numerals is unchanged.
- **The ordered ramp on paper is fixed.** Its far end sits at `#E64B02` so five rungs clear the separation floor above the parchment ground.
- **Themed copies match the stock look.** Copies made with `apply-design-md.cjs --default` now match because both come from the same reference.

No migration required.

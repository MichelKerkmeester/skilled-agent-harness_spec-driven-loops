The stock chart register is now the cursor Style Reference.

&nbsp;

## What's New at a Glance

- Palette and corners follow the reference.
  - Every template, delivery and proof sheet carries the reference's parchment, ink and accents.
  - They sit in its stock palette blocks.
  - Each one rounds its corners on its ladder.
  - `assets/color/palettes.json` chrome, dark chrome, corner ladder and the three systems are derived from `sk-design-md-generator/styles/library/bundles/cursor/DESIGN.md`.
  - The source names the four values moved to clear a gate.
- Body text uses the reference's typeface stack.
  - Every stock file sets its body text in `CursorGothic, Inter, system-ui, "Helvetica Neue", sans-serif`.
  - The mono stack for numerals is unchanged.
- The ordered ramp on paper is fixed.
  - Its far end sits at `#E64B02`.
  - So five rungs clear the separation floor above the parchment ground.
- Themed copies match the stock look.
  - Copies made with `apply-design-md.cjs --default` now match.
  - That is because both come from the same reference.

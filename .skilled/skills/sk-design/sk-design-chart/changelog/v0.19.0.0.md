The repaint in v0.18.0.0 landed after reviewers had read the captures, so eighteen forms shipped in colours nobody had looked at. A second round of fresh readers found three defects in the repainted forms and, while chasing the first, uncovered a rule the corpus had followed by habit rather than by check. v0.19.0.0 corrects those defects and enforces the emphasis rule.

## What's New at a Glance

- **`emphasis-budget` enforces one emphasis mark and a used lead rule.** Emphasis identifies the mark the sentence is about, so using it on two marks erases that distinction and puts more than half the ink in the exception colour because the emphasized mark tends to be largest. `emphasis-budget` rejects more than one marked row and any lead rule no row reaches, though ten forms already obeyed both rules.
- **`bar-columns` and `staff-hours-by-service` now make claims that match their emphasis marks.** `bar-columns` claimed two of seven sites move more than half but marked only the largest, whereas `staff-hours-by-service` paired home visits with the day centre in its headline and finding but marked only home visits, so the sentence and its colour told different stories. Marking the second mark in each would put 52% and 66% of the ink in the emphasis colour. Both sentences now make the one-subject claim the paint makes, and both claims are true of the same data: LDS ships 30% of the week, and home visits take more than the four smallest services together.
- **The box-plot median no longer splits the box.** Its page-colour, three-unit line read as a gap that split every box into two stacked bars, especially short boxes where it took an eighth of the height. It is now a one-and-a-half-unit line on the box.
- **The dumbbell left the top half of its axis unlabelled.** Thinning dropped every second rung, but the last even rung landed past the readings and was skipped as out of range, so the rung below it was dropped too and the axis stopped at 40 while pipelines ran to 64. Thinning now stops when no surviving rung sits above the one being dropped, so the axis reads 0, 40, 60.
- **The neutral-step contrast is a real but unexercised case.** The third and fourth neutral steps sit 1.33:1 apart on the dark ground, the ladder's tightest rung against a 1.3:1 floor. A reader flagged this as the pair that would blur first at small mark size. No form paints that pair: the ten forms drawing both a third and fourth series use either the ordered ramp, covered by the magnitude separation gate, or the categorical set, separated by hue, so the hypothetical is not exercised by the corpus.

The repaint in v0.18.0.0 landed after the reviewers had read the captures, so eighteen forms shipped
in colours nobody had looked at. A second round of fresh readers went over the repainted forms.
Three defects came back, and chasing the first one found a rule the corpus had been keeping by
habit rather than by check.

## What Changed

#### Added

- **A form spends its emphasis colour once.** The emphasis is not a series: it is the value that
  says which mark the sentence is about, so marking two marks is not twice the emphasis but none.
  It also has a size: the emphasised mark tends to be the largest one, so two of them put more than
  half the ink on the page in the colour reserved for the exception. `emphasis-budget` holds both
  directions — more than one marked row fails, and a lead rule in the styles that no row ever
  reaches fails too, because a colour defined and never painted is a decision nobody made. Ten
  forms already obeyed this; nothing enforced it. Two mutation cases stand behind it.

#### Fixes

- **Two forms made a two-subject claim and marked one mark.** `bar-columns` said two of the seven
  sites move more than half and painted only the largest; `staff-hours-by-service` paired home
  visits with the day centre in both its headline and its finding and painted only home visits. A
  reader scanning colour got a different story from the one in the sentence. Marking the second
  mark in each was the obvious repair and the wrong one: it would have put 52% and 66% of the ink
  in the emphasis colour. Both sentences now make the one-subject claim the paint makes, and both
  claims are true of the same data — LDS ships 30% of the week, home visits take more than the four
  smallest services together.
- **The box plot's median cut a hole through its box.** Drawn in the page colour at three units, the
  median read as a gap rather than a marker, so every box came apart into two stacked bars — worst
  on the short ones, where the band ate an eighth of the height. It is a line on the box now, at
  one and a half.
- **The dumbbell left the top half of its axis unlabelled.** The ladder thins itself by dropping
  every second rung, but the last even rung landed past the readings and was skipped for being out
  of range, so the rung below it was dropped too and the axis stopped at 40 while pipelines ran to
  64. Thinning now stops once no rung survives above the one being dropped: the axis reads 0, 40, 60.

#### Checked and left alone

- **The third and fourth neutral steps sit 1.33:1 apart on the dark ground**, the tightest rung in
  the ladder against a 1.3:1 floor. A reader flagged it as the pair that would blur first at small
  mark size. No form paints it: the ten forms that draw both a third and a fourth series carry
  either the ordered ramp, which the magnitude separation gate covers, or the categorical set, which
  separates by hue. The hypothetical is real and the corpus does not exercise it.

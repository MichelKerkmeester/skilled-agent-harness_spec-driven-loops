The repaint in v0.18.0.0 landed after reviewers had read the captures. So eighteen forms shipped in colours nobody had looked at. A second round of fresh readers found three defects in the repainted forms. While chasing the first, they uncovered a rule the corpus had followed by habit rather than by check. v0.19.0.0 corrects those defects and enforces the emphasis rule.

&nbsp;

## What's New at a Glance

- `emphasis-budget` enforces one emphasis mark and a used lead rule.
  - Emphasis identifies the mark the sentence is about.
  - Using it on two marks erases that distinction.
  - Using it on two marks also puts more than half the ink in the exception colour.
  - That is because the emphasized mark tends to be largest.
  - `emphasis-budget` rejects more than one marked row.
  - `emphasis-budget` also rejects any lead rule no row reaches.
  - Ten forms already obeyed both rules.
- `bar-columns` and `staff-hours-by-service` now make claims that match their emphasis marks.
  - `bar-columns` claimed two of seven sites move more than half.
  - `bar-columns` marked only the largest.
  - `staff-hours-by-service` paired home visits with the day centre in its headline and finding.
  - `staff-hours-by-service` marked only home visits.
  - So the sentence and its colour told different stories.
  - Marking the second mark in each would put 52% and 66% of the ink in the emphasis colour.
  - Both sentences now make the one-subject claim the paint makes.
  - Both claims are true of the same data.
  - LDS ships 30% of the week.
  - Home visits take more than the four smallest services together.
- The box-plot median no longer splits the box.
  - The median's page-colour, three-unit line read as a gap.
  - That gap split every box into two stacked bars.
  - This was especially so in short boxes, where it took an eighth of the height.
  - The median is now a one-and-a-half-unit line on the box.
- The dumbbell left the top half of its axis unlabelled.
  - Thinning dropped every second rung.
  - But the last even rung landed past the readings.
  - The last even rung was skipped as out of range.
  - So the rung below it was dropped too.
  - The axis stopped at 40 while pipelines ran to 64.
  - Thinning now stops when no surviving rung sits above the one being dropped.
  - So the axis reads 0, 40, 60.
- The neutral-step contrast is a real but unexercised case.
  - The third and fourth neutral steps sit 1.33:1 apart on the dark ground.
  - That is the ladder's tightest rung against a 1.3:1 floor.
  - A reader flagged this as the pair that would blur first at small mark size.
  - No form paints that pair.
  - The ten forms drawing both a third and fourth series use the ordered ramp or the categorical set.
  - The ordered ramp is covered by the magnitude separation gate.
  - The categorical set is separated by hue.
  - So the hypothetical is not exercised by the corpus.

Two of the three fixes in v0.19.0.0 shipped before the reader who checked them came back, and both were wrong. v0.20.0.0 corrects them. The same pattern has now occurred twice in a row: a fix read by its maker shipped, then someone else found it had not worked.

## What's New at a Glance

- **The box plot median now contrasts with its box.** v0.19.0.0 thinned the stroke from three units to one and a half and called the median repaired, but the width was never the reason it remained a hole: the page-coloured stroke read as the ground showing through however thin it was drawn, leaving every box still split into two stacked blocks. A filled box now takes the ink and the emphasised box, which is filled with the ink, takes the hue, never the page colour, under one rule across both grounds.
- **The box plot's finding was checked and left alone.** It names three teams and the emphasis marks one. v0.19.0.0 repaired this shape in two other forms by rewriting the sentence, but those rewrites do not apply here: the cluster claim reads from median heights, which the first fix restores, and Complex is emphasised because its 85-day span is the sentence's one hard number. Position carries the first half of the claim and colour the second.
- **The white box was checked and left alone.** It is 44% of the plot's ink, measured by a reader counting pixels. That is under the line, and it follows from Complex's genuinely widest quartile range rather than from a colour decision.
- **The dumbbell axis now reads 0, 20, 40, 60.** v0.19.0.0 stopped thinning from dropping the last rung, recovering 60 and fixing that symptom but leaving the axis at 0, 40, 60. Thinning exists to relieve crowding, but this four-step ladder had nothing to relieve, so it spent half the rungs and left nine of the ten rows inside one unbroken span with no interior mark to read a distance against, while every rung inside the readings is now drawn.

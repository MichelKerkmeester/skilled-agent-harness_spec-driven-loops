# v1.2.0.0: The visible gap, focal balance and a label rule that sees the corpus

Version 1.2.0.0 corrects mismatches between diagram rules and the drawings they describe. It updates label masks, focal accents, legend conventions, themed blocks, starter figures and capture review.

## Why This Release

Capture review failed the corpus on the visible-gap read, and it was right. Three focal-balance findings survived two earlier passes, while rules had been measured in one place and assumed in another.

## What's New at a Glance

- **Swimlane arrow labels now have clearance.** A fourth label, overpainted by a box, was removed rather than relocated.
- **The rule family is now `label-mask-clearance`.** The 1.1.0.0 entry called it `short-connector-labels`, a name based on the first defect it caught rather than what it checks.
- **Focal accents now mark the intended shapes.** The integration topology, venn and full starter carry the corrected accents.
- **Legend rules now span the drawings they sit under.** Their scope was measured across the library.
- **The library converged on one legend typography.** Three had coexisted, and a reader learning the convention from one file met a different one in the next.
- **The high-level map's two swatches now match their arrowhead connectors.** The guide also records a measurement for the three keyed fills.
- **Themed blocks now declare the system that painted them.** A themed block that cannot say which reference painted it, and prove it with a hash, fails by name.
- **A delivery is judged as the form it came from.** Copied starters and legacy files follow their source forms.
- **The applicator now applies the mark-text gate at write time.** The applicator and the checker cannot disagree about whether a delivery is clean.
- **Moved roles carry their translucent forms, and starter figures name declared roles.** Two custom properties stopped naming colours they are not.
- **Capture review now reads whole pages and measures the page rather than the crop.** The two captures committed before their forms settled were re-shot.

## Diagram Labels

#### Swimlane Labels Gain Clearance

All three swimlane arrow labels sat on their own connector where the skill's own rule demands six pixels of clearance. One of them erased a forty-unit leg outright. The clearance floor is four units, set by rendering the tightest form in the library rather than by rounding up.

A box overpainted the fourth label until only the bottom sliver of its glyphs rendered. That label was removed rather than relocated, because the colour and the legend already said what it said.

&nbsp;

#### More Masks Enter the Rule

Recognition depended on a class marker carried by only two forms, so the rule missed most of the library. It now recognizes any ground-filled rectangle with its label immediately after, which took it from 168 assertions to 820. The first full run found 46 violations. Thirteen were masks that moved, and thirty-three were sentences that described behaviour the corpus does not have, which were corrected.

## Focal Balance

#### The Integration Topology Marks Boxes

It spent its accent on nine connectors and declared it twice under two meanings. It now marks two boxes, and the legend says so once.

&nbsp;

#### The Venn Gains a Focal Mark

The venn had no focal mark at all, only an unused declaration. It now carries the accent on its one focal intersection. The focal label measures 10.57:1 where it measured 2.18:1.

&nbsp;

#### The Full Starter Uses One Accent

The full starter taught six accents to everyone who copied it, against a ceiling of two. It now carries one.

---

## Legends

#### Rules Span Their Drawings

Two legend rules now span the drawing they sit under, measured rather than assumed. Sixteen forms shared the suspect value, and only two fell short of their content.

&nbsp;

#### Legend Conventions Match

Two swatches in the high-level map drew a bare line while every connector they keyed carried an arrowhead. The swatches now show arrowheads, so the legend shows a direction where the drawing does.

&nbsp;

#### Keyed Fills Have a Measured Comparison

A reviewer's complaint that three keyed fills read as one grey was answered with a measurement rather than a change. Flattened against the paper, they sit within four percent, and the guide now says so. The next reviewer will measure the same fills and reach the same conclusion.

---

## Theming

#### Theme Tokens Scope Equality

A themed file is supposed to differ from the stock palette, so checking it against the stock palette checked it against the thing it was built not to match. A block declares the system that painted it. Without that token nothing changes. With it, equality is skipped for that block alone while every other rule and all four gates still run.

&nbsp;

#### Deliveries Follow Their Source Forms

A copied starter keeps the exemption its starter earns. A copied legacy file keeps the off-grid allowance its source recorded.

&nbsp;

#### The Front Door Names the Applicator

The checker already applied the mark-text gate to a themed block. The sibling packet's front door named the applicator, and this one did not. This packet's front door now names the applicator.

&nbsp;

#### Roles Carry Their Translucency

When a role moves, its translucent forms travel with it in 75 places across 32 of the 38 forms. Eighty literals across the four starters hid the roles those starters declare. One starter declared ten roles and referenced none of them inside its figure. It now names its role in eighty places, so repainting a palette block repaints the drawing rather than the header alone.

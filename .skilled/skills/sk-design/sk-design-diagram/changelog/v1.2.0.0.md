# v1.2.0.0 - The visible gap, focal balance, and a label rule that sees the corpus

**Released:** 2026-09-11

The capture review failed the corpus on the visible-gap read, and it was right: all three swimlane
arrow labels sat on their own connector where the skill's own rule demands six pixels of clearance,
one of them erasing a forty-unit leg outright, and a fourth label was overpainted by a box until only
the bottom sliver of its glyphs rendered. That label was removed rather than relocated, because the
colour and the legend already said what it said. This release closes that read, the three
focal-balance findings that survived two earlier passes, and the rules that were measured in one
place and assumed in another.

## The visible gap

Three swimlane masks sat on the connectors they label, and the rule that holds them could not see
most of the library: it recognised a mask by a class marker only two of the forms carry. It now
recognises any ground-filled rect with its label immediately after, which took it from 168 assertions
to 820, and the first full run found 46 violations. Thirteen were masks that moved; thirty-three were
sentences that described behaviour the corpus does not have. The clearance floor is 4 units, set by
rendering the tightest form in the library rather than by rounding up, and the focal label on the
venn now measures 10.57:1 where it measured 2.18:1.

The family the 1.1.0.0 entry called `short-connector-labels` is now `label-mask-clearance`, named for
what it checks rather than for the first defect it caught.

## Focal balance

The integration topology spent its accent on nine connectors and declared it twice under two
meanings; it now marks two boxes and the legend says so once. The venn had no focal mark at all, only
an unused declaration, and now carries the accent on its one focal intersection. The full starter
taught six accents to everyone who copied it, against a ceiling of two; it now carries one.

## The legend doctrine

Two legend rules now span the drawing they sit under, measured rather than assumed: sixteen forms
shared the suspect value and only two fell short of their content. The library converged on one
legend typography, because three coexisted and a reader learning the convention from one file met a
different one in the next. Two swatches in the high-level map drew a bare line while every connector
they key carried an arrowhead, so the legend taught a line where the drawing shows a direction. A
reviewer's complaint that three keyed fills read as one grey was answered with a measurement rather
than a change: flattened against the paper they sit within four percent, and the guide now says so,
because the next reviewer will measure the same fills and reach the same conclusion.

## Theming

A themed file is supposed to differ from the stock palette, so checking it against the stock palette
was checking it against the thing it was built not to match. A block now declares the system that
painted it: without that token nothing changes, and with it equality is skipped for that block alone
while every other rule and all four gates still run. A themed block that cannot say which reference
painted it, and prove it with a hash, fails by name. A delivery is also judged as the form it came
from, so a copied starter keeps the exemption a starter earns and a copied legacy file keeps the
off-grid allowance its source recorded.

The applicator now applies the mark-text gate at write time, as the checker already did to a themed
block, so the two cannot disagree about whether a delivery is clean. The front door names the
applicator, which the sibling packet's did and this one did not. When a role moves, its translucent
forms travel with it, in 75 places across 32 of the 38 forms. Two custom properties stopped naming
colours they are not. And a starter that declared ten roles and referenced none of them inside its
figure now names its role in eighty places, so repainting a palette block repaints the drawing rather
than the header alone.

## Captures that show the whole page

The capture review now reads whole pages and measures the page rather than the crop, which is what
hid two serious defects from the reviewer reading the committed images. The two captures committed
before their forms settled were re-shot.

## Fixed

The three swimlane masks painted across their connectors and the label that a box had overpainted.
The unreadable focal label on the venn. The misplaced accents on the integration topology, the venn
and the full starter. Two legend swatches that keyed an arrowhead with a bare line. Two custom
properties naming colours they are not. Eighty literals across the four starters that hid the roles
those starters declare. And thirty-three sentences that described behaviour the corpus does not have.

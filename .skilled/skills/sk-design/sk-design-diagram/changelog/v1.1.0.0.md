# v1.1.0.0, One Form Library, a Style Reference and Rules That Hold Themselves

Diagram forms now live in one library, and the visual language can be swapped.

- `assets/templates/` and `assets/examples/` are now `assets/diagrams/`, holding 38 forms.
- Each entry is a complete working diagram to copy and adjust rather than a strict template.
- `assets/style-reference/harness-diagram/` holds the visual language as one bundle.
- Another language is a sibling directory with the same four files.

New checks cover rules the packet stated but did not check.

- Two checker families bring the corpus check to twelve: `legend-fidelity` and `short-connector-labels`.
- They compare legend swatches with what they key.
- They check masks over short connectors.
- A `text-on-mark` gate now exists at 4.5:1.

Theming, full-page captures and defect fixes round out the release.

- `apply-design-md.cjs` themes chosen forms from any local Style Reference.
- A reference may be measured by `sk-design-md-generator` or written by hand.
- The shared renderer adds opt-in `--full-page` captures.
- The release fixes 52 defects across the corpus.

&nbsp;

## Why This Release

The release closes three gaps.

- The corpus was split into templates and examples for no reason a reader could act on.
- One visual language had no way to be swapped.
- The packet had rules it stated and did not check.

## Diagram Forms

Forms moved into one shared library.

#### One Library, Clear Roles <a id="c-one-library-clear-roles"></a>

- Prefixes are gone.
- The four skin skeletons are named `starter-light`, `starter-dark`, `starter-terminal` and `starter-full`.
- The code stopped keying on the directory.
- Both applicators branch on whether a file carries a palette block.
- The checker calls a file a starter or a form by its name.
- The catalog indexes what answers a question.
- That is why the starters are not in it.
- `--all` means all 38.
- The examples flag is gone.

&nbsp;

## Style Reference

The language lives in one replaceable bundle.

#### A Visual Language You Can Replace <a id="c-a-visual-language-you-can-replace"></a>

- `DESIGN.md` states the language.
- `origin.md` says where it came from.
- `diagram-palette.json` carries the tokens.
- `icons.html` carries the glyphs.
- Theming with `apply-design-md.cjs` is gated before anything is written.
- A reference naming our role tokens fills them verbatim.
- Anything it does not name falls back to selection rules over its colour table.
- Pure rules cannot reproduce this language.
- A reference may be measured by `sk-design-md-generator` or written by hand.
- Theming from the carried reference reproduces the stock palette exactly.
- The lightest background row in a faithful reference is the near-white service fill, not the off-white ground.

## Corpus

The corpus gained checks, cleaner docs and defect fixes.

#### Keep Legends and Connectors Clear <a id="c-keep-legends-and-connectors-clear"></a>

- `legend-fidelity` refuses a legend swatch drawn unlike the thing it keys.
- `short-connector-labels` refuses a label mask painted across a connector under about 60px.
- That is because the mask erases the stroke the reader was told to follow.
- It found a real instance on its first run.
- Two reading passes had missed it.

#### Give Chip Labels Their Own Gate <a id="c-give-chip-labels-their-own-gate"></a>

- A reader had been measuring chip labels against the text gate by analogy.
- The gate was missing.
- A `text-on-mark` gate now exists at 4.5:1.
- The text-gate analogy was right.

#### Drop Unused Starter Hashes <a id="c-drop-unused-starter-hashes"></a>

- Starter hashes were deleted rather than refreshed.
- Nothing read them.
- All four hashes were stale.
- Byte-identity already catches the drift they claimed to catch.
- A hash would have flagged every legitimate geometry fix.

#### Bring the Docs in Line <a id="c-bring-the-docs-in-line"></a>

- Four documents stopped stating values the corpus does not hold.
- The dot pattern is documented as the default because 26 of 34 forms use it.
- The structural role stopped carrying text in 50 places.
- The last form exempt from generation was tokenized.
- So the corpus now has no exemption at all.

#### Fix Defects Across the Corpus <a id="c-fix-defects-across-the-corpus"></a>

- The release fixes 52 defects across the corpus.
- A full manual reading of every form found 34 defects.
- A verification pass found 18 more.
- The 18 included two that never landed.
- They also included nine fixes that had introduced something new.
- One was a connector routed through the node it pointed at.

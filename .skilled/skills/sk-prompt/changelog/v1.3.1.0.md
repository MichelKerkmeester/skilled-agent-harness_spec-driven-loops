# sk-prompt v1.3.1.0, Cross-CLI Prompt Guidance

This documentation addendum carries cross-CLI composition findings into the prompt-quality guidance. Packet 113/006 had propagated only the medium pre-plan finding cross-CLI, holding bundle-gate strictness and anti-hallucination wording pending cross-model validation. This release adds three new composition-guidance notes to §3: pre-planning density, bundle-gate wording and anti-hallucination language.

> Spec folder: `specs/skilled-agent-orchestration/113-cli-devin-prompt-quality/003-eval-loop/` and `specs/skilled-agent-orchestration/113-cli-devin-prompt-quality/007-cross-model-validation/`

## What's New at a Glance

- **The pre-planning note in `assets/cli-prompt-quality-card.md` favors medium density.** Section 3 softens the v-002 BUILD-dense claim from "without yield" to "without clear yield" and says medium matches or beats dense on every measured model. On deepseek-v4-pro, v-002 was 0.7129 versus v-004 at 0.7600, a close result that still favors medium.
- **Standard bundle-gate wording beats strict wording.** The note says verbose constraints push models toward defensive output. The finding held on deepseek-v4-pro (+0.0525) and kimi-k2.6 (+0.0305).
- **Framework choice is the stronger lever.** The guidance says framework choice has about 2.4 times the effect of anti-hallucination wording. The finding held on deepseek-v4-pro (+0.0732) and kimi-k2.6 (+0.0756).

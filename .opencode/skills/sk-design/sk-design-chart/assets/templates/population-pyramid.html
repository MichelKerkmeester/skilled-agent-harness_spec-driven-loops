Nearly twice as many men as women in their twenties, and the reverse past eighty

Registered patients by age band and sex across the four surgeries in the network, men mirrored left of the centre line and women right. Each band is read as a pair: how far the two bars reach tells you which side that age group is heavier in.

Men aged 20 to 29 outnumber women 1,204 to 688, while women lead 246 to 142 past eighty.

Source: patient registration system, all four surgeries in the network.

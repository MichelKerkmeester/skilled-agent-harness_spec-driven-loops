The corpus reads as a product card rather than a template: the authoring note has left the visible
source line, cartesian plots fill a taller frame, the data table folds behind a disclosure, the
finding carries a direction cue, and large ticks read compactly with a declared unit.

## What Changed

#### New Features

- **Clean source line.** The retarget instruction moved from the visible source text into a comment above `CHART_DATA` in every template; the `source-line` family keeps it there.
- **Taller plots.** Sixteen cartesian forms draw in a 392-unit frame (distribution strip 380), baselines, ticks and legends moved with the frame; ten shape-bound forms keep their heights, recorded per form.
- **Folded table.** Every data table sits in `<details class="data">` with a "Show the data" summary, open on inert forms and closed on tooltip forms; the `table-disclosure` family holds it and the three proof sheets follow.
- **Finding cue.** A `FINDING` block declares `trend`; the footer draws an inline arrow for up or down; the `finding-cue` family holds the block and the read.
- **Units and compact ticks.** `READOUT.unit` prints after the card value; `compact()` renders ticks at or above five digits as `12k` or `1.2M`; the `number-format` family requires the unit field.

#### Fixes

- **Contract fade figures.** The contract states the shipped single-area fade of 0.35 to 0.04 and flat 0.4 stacks as ours, with 0.8 to 0.1 kept as the frozen shadcn measurement.

#### Notes

- Built by GLM-5.3-Flash through pi from one short brief per move, each new assertion proved on a mutated copy before the corpus passed it; the conductor ran the render gate and the captures.

The chart corpus now carries one measured visual register from card chrome through marks, readouts and legends. The upgrade keeps the existing data, palette and geometry contracts while making the browser-facing surface coherent across all twenty-six forms and their deliveries.

## What Changed

#### New Features

- **A shadcn-informed card register.** Every form now uses five card parts in order: headline, subtitle, figure, footer and source. Titles, descriptions, ticks, notes, hero values and ring totals use the measured type scale, while findings and sources share the footer with a consistent divider and gap.
- **Positioned tooltip cards.** Tooltip-bearing forms render a padded, rounded HTML card inside the figure. Each card carries a per-series indicator, the `READOUT` label and the formatted value; tooltip copy is no longer painted as SVG text.
- **Keyed multi-series legends.** Every multi-series form renders one centered legend entry per declared series, with an 8px keyed swatch, muted 12px label and 16px gap.
- **Gallery scheme propagation.** Gallery frames pass `?scheme=light` or `?scheme=dark` to their templates and apply the corresponding `data-scheme` attribute inside the frame, which is the selector the templates can honour consistently.

#### Fixes

- **Unified cartesian marks.** Cartesian forms now use bare axes, muted tabular ticks, an 8px tick margin, horizontal dashed grids, 2px lines and the measured bar and area radii. Single-series cartesian marks use the first categorical token; emphasis remains reserved for highlighted marks.
- **Area and point treatment.** Area fills use the vertical 0.8-to-0.1 gradient, stacked areas use flat 0.4 opacity, and ordinary line points remain invisible unless the form's contract calls for a highlighted point.
- **Static enforcement.** The corpus checker gained `legend` and `tooltip-card` families and retuned `type-scale`, `radius` and `card-parts` to the measured register without lowering any prior assertion floor. Mutation runs recorded the exact failures before the corpus was restored.

#### Notes

- Palette values, palette JSON, geometry blocks and numeric gates were preserved.
- The static corpus check passes. The render pass remains dependent on Chrome being able to launch in the sandbox; when that launch is unavailable, existing captures remain untouched for the conductor to regenerate.

A chart whose data has an honest baseline can lead with its number: eight forms now print a value,
a signed delta and the period above the plot, and every form declares whether it does.

## What Changed

#### New Features

- **Metric-and-delta header.** A `METRIC` block beside `READOUT` carries `value`, `valueLabel`, `delta`, `deltaLabel`, `period`, `trend` and a `why` line; eight forms declare `present: true` and render the header between the subtitle and the figure.
- **The metric rung.** `typeScale.roles` in the palette source gains `metric: 26px`, so the value prints at a published size rather than a template-local one.
- **`metric-block` family.** Errors on a missing block, a `present: true` without its labels, a trend outside the set, a script that never reads the value, and a `present: false` that still ships the markup.
- **`card-parts` accepts an optional metric.** The part may sit between the subtitle and the figure and nowhere else.

#### Notes

- Every value and delta is computed from that form's own data block and matches a number in its table; two were recomputed independently by the conductor.
- Built by GLM-5.3-Flash through pi from two short briefs, the assertion proved on a mutated copy before the corpus passed it.

## Files Changed

- 26 templates and 7 deliveries under `assets/`, `assets/color/palettes.json`, `scripts/check-corpus.cjs`, `references/template-contract.md`, `screenshots/**`.

The gallery is gone, on request. With it go the things that existed only to serve it.

## What Changed

#### Removed

- **`assets/gallery.html` and `scripts/build-gallery.cjs`.** The page framed every form twice, one
  per colour scheme, and the packet's own contract already says a gallery is a workbench and never a
  deliverable. The captures under `screenshots/` are the review surface now, one per form.
- **The height poster in every template.** Twenty-nine forms carried a script whose only reader was
  the gallery's frame sizer. It is removed; the `?scheme=` reader beside it stays, because the
  screenshot renderer is what needs it.
- **The `gallery` and `frame-height` families** and their three mutation cases, and the renderer's
  gallery exclusion. Nothing framed the corpus any more, so nothing had a frame to hold.

#### Fixes

- **Every capture re-rendered from the final sources.** The capture set was rebuilt from empty:
  32 sources, 32 captures, none stale.

## Verification

- `scripts/check-corpus.cjs` — 42 families, 6,091 assertions, `RESULT: PASSED`.
- `node --test scripts/tests/` — 84 tests, 0 failures.
- `render-screenshots.cjs` — 32 rendered from empty, 32 covered.

Four decisions a reader can misread were implicit in the paint code. Each is now declared beside
the data, held by a check, and proved against a mutated copy.

## What Changed

#### New Features

- **`MARKS`.** Every form declares what its marks do: `points` (`none`, `sparse`, `all`), `fill` (`none`, `gradient`, `flat`) and `zero` (`baseline`, `meaningful`), with one line of why. Held by `mark-policy`.
- **Key kinds.** Every declared series carries `indicator`, `swatch` or `rule`, and both the legend chip and the card row draw the kind the series names. A stroked series no longer reads as one more bar. Held by `tooltip-indicator`.
- **`REFERENCE`.** Every cartesian form declares a list of `{ value, label, why }` lines drawn across the plot and named at the right edge. `daily-line` ships one at the first week's average, which is what makes its headline visible. Held by `reference-line`.
- **Cursor guide.** A hairline at the hovered reading on the forms that earn it, declared as `guide` in the `MARKS` block and permitted only with two or more series or more than twenty readings. Held by `cursor-guide`.

#### Fixes

- **A multi-series card painted every row in the hovered series' colour.** `bar-line-composed` and `population-pyramid` told a reader that both figures belonged to the mark under the pointer when one belonged to the other measure. A card row now carries its own series.

#### Notes

- The planned consumer for `REFERENCE` was `bullet`, and it turned out not to be one: its target is per row against four different scales, which a chart-wide list cannot express. It stays in its data, and `daily-line` is the worked case instead.
- The vocabulary for key kinds is two, not three. A card whose rows are statistics of one series already carries `data-single-series` in its markup and draws no key, so a `none` kind would have been a second way to say what the markup says.
- `stacked-area` clears the guide's density bar four times over and declares `false`: its card opens on a band rather than a column, so a hairline would sit at the middle of the band every time. Density is necessary and not sufficient, and the check cannot see the rest.

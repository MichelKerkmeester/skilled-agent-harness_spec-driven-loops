Two of the three fixes in v0.19.0.0 shipped before the reader who checked them came back, and both
were wrong. This corrects them. The pattern is now twice in a row: a fix read by the person who made
it, shipped, and then read by someone else who found it had not worked.

## What Changed

#### Fixes

- **The box plot's median was still a hole, and the width was never the reason.** v0.19.0.0 thinned
  the stroke from three units to one and a half and called it repaired. It was not: the stroke was
  painted in the page colour, and a line the colour of the ground reads as the ground showing
  through however thin it is drawn. Every box still came apart into two stacked blocks. The median
  now contrasts with the box it marks and never takes the page colour — a filled box takes the ink,
  and the emphasised box, which is filled with the ink, takes the hue instead. One rule across both
  grounds.
- **The dumbbell's axis was thinning a ladder that was never crowded.** v0.19.0.0 stopped the
  thinning from dropping the last rung, which recovered the 60 and left the axis reading 0, 40, 60.
  It fixed the symptom. Thinning exists to relieve crowding and this ladder has four steps, so there
  was nothing to relieve: it was spending half the rungs and leaving nine of the ten rows inside one
  unbroken span with no interior mark to read a distance against. Every rung inside the readings is
  drawn now, and the axis reads 0, 20, 40, 60.

#### Checked and left alone

- **The box plot's finding names three teams and the emphasis marks one.** That is the shape v0.19.0.0
  repaired in two other forms by rewriting the sentence, and it does not apply here: the cluster
  claim reads from the median heights, which is what the first fix above restores, and the emphasis
  marks Complex because the sentence's one hard number is Complex's 85-day span. Position carries
  the first half of the claim and colour carries the second.
- **The white box is 44% of the plot's ink**, measured by a reader counting pixels. Under the line,
  and it follows from Complex's genuinely widest quartile range rather than from a colour decision.

The stock chart register is now the cursor Style Reference. Every template, delivery and proof
sheet carries cursor's parchment, ink and accents in its stock palette blocks, sets its body text
in the reference's typeface stack, and rounds its corners on the reference's ladder.

## What Changed

#### New Features

- **Cursor as the stock palette.** `assets/color/palettes.json` chrome, dark chrome, corner ladder and the three systems are derived from `sk-design-md-generator/styles/library/bundles/cursor/DESIGN.md`; the source names the four values moved to clear a gate.
- **Body typeface.** Every stock file sets `CursorGothic, Inter, system-ui, "Helvetica Neue", sans-serif`; the mono stack for numerals is unchanged.

#### Fixes

- **Ordered ramp on paper.** The far end sits at `#E64B02` so five rungs clear the separation floor above the parchment ground.

#### Notes

- Themed copies made with `apply-design-md.cjs --default` now match the stock look, since both come from the same reference.
- Static and render gates pass; the palette proof sheets and every capture are regenerated.

## Files Changed

- `assets/color/palettes.json`, the 35 stock HTML files' palette blocks and body font stacks, `references/color-system.md`, `references/template-contract.md`, `references/design-md-theming.md`, `screenshots/**`.

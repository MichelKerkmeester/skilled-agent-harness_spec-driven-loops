Every rendered capture was read by a fresh reviewer rather than by the person who changed the
palette. Twelve defects came back. The checks had been green throughout.

## What Changed

#### Breaking

- **A figure never spends its data marks on the ink.** Half the corpus drew its readings in the ink colour with a single accent on the one mark the headline was about, so the only colour in the picture was on the exception and none of it on the subject. The ranked system's first series now takes the hue and the ink becomes its emphasis: eighteen forms turn from near-white marks with one orange, to orange marks with one white. The steps behind the first series are unchanged, and rank by lightness as before.
- **The ranked system's ordering rule starts behind its first series.** That series carries the reading and takes a hue, so it is not part of the lightness ladder. Separation is still held across every adjacent pair, including the one between the hue and the first grey.

#### Fixes

- **A heatmap said the opposite of what it drew.** `calendar-grid`'s caption read "Darker is more" while its own legend ran dark to bright for less to more. On a magnitude ramp colour is the only encoding, so the sentence a reader decodes the figure with was inverted. Four more sentences carried the same assumption: `bullet`'s deepening shades, `heat-matrix` and the calls delivery shading "darker for higher", and the ordered proof sheet's headline.
- **A dumbbell could not show direction.** Both ends of every pair were the same colour, so nothing in the figure said which pipelines got faster — the headline's whole claim was unverifiable from the picture. The before is now the quiet dot and the after the bright one, and a pair leans the way it moved.
- **The dumbbell also marked the wrong row.** The emphasis fell on the widest change while the sentence underneath was about the one pipeline that reversed. It now marks the row that moved against the rest, which is what the sentence is about.
- **The tracker's severity ran backwards.** After the palette change a degraded day was drawn in a hotter colour than a failed one, and the subtitle still called it amber. Held, degraded and failed now escalate teal, orange, red, and the sentence names them.
- **A candlestick painted "up" in the colour that means "closed down".** The header arrow took the same token the legend defines as a down candle, nine lines apart in one figure.
- **A screen-reader description carried two wrong numbers.** `independent-percentages` described ninety-four percent and eleven where the data, the bars and the finding all say 97 and 9.
- **An axis title was painted over its own tick labels,** eating the leading digit so 1,800 read as 800.
- **A treemap label ran into the next cell.** A name wider than its cell is no longer drawn into its neighbour.
- **A template's own note described a line that clears the columns.** It crosses them from the sixth period on. The note now says what the figure does and why that is left alone.

#### New Features

- **`ramp-prose`.** A magnitude ramp runs toward whichever ground the theme uses, so its strong end is darker on paper and lighter on ink. Prose naming a lightness direction is therefore true in one theme and false in the other. It was false in five places at once; the rule refuses it.

#### Notes

- **The gallery is no longer captured.** A viewport-sized shot of a twenty-nine-form contact sheet showed its first tile while sitting in the capture set looking covered, and a shot tall enough to hold all of it is megabytes rewritten on every palette change. What the sheet owes is completeness, and the `gallery` family asserts that against the directory. It is a page to open, not a picture to keep.
- Three things the reviewers raised were judged deliberate and left: the invisible surface swatch on the proof sheets, `grouped-bars` marking its outlier by pattern rather than colour, and the ordered ramp sitting at its separation floor by construction.

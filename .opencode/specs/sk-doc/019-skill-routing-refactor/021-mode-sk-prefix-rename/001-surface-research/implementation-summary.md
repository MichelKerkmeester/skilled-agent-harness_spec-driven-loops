<!-- ANCHOR:what-built -->
## What Was Built

Two independent research lineages (Grok 4.5 high via cli-cursor, GLM 5.2 via cli-devin, five
iterations each) enumerated the consumer surface of the twenty packet directories and twenty-one
workflowMode keys: registries, routers, generated manifests, advisor metadata, benchmark gold,
command bindings, agent definitions, runtime mirrors, hooks and prose. Their union became the
phase 002 contract and the frozen rename map.
<!-- /ANCHOR:what-built -->


---

<!-- ANCHOR:how-delivered -->
## HOW IT WAS DELIVERED

Executed under the phase 002 contract: orchestrator-performed git moves, dispatched-model edit passes, and orchestrator verification of every claim against the Lane C gate and link baseline before commit.
<!-- /ANCHOR:how-delivered -->

<!-- ANCHOR:decisions -->
## KEY DECISIONS

| Decision | Rationale |
|----------|-----------|
| Hold pre-existing verdicts constant (including BLOCKED states) | The rename must be behavior-preserving; fixing unrelated gold would blend two changes |
| Regenerate generated artifacts instead of editing them | Hand-edits to manifests drift from their generators |

-- AC-013 the labels were read --
Two passes, because the corpus check does not look at the picture.
1. Measured: a probe appended to a copy of each file walks every SVG text node, takes getBBox()
   and reports overlapping pairs. Three overlaps were found, in parallel-axes, scatter and
   van-age-against-repair-cost. The same probe run against the committed versions reports the same
   three, and the horizontal figure is smaller after the change (14.0 to 13.2, 28.0 to 26.5,
   7.0 to 6.6) because mono digits are narrower than proportional ones at these sizes. Two of the
   three are a rotate(-90) axis name, where getBBox reports the untransformed box, so they are an
   artefact of the measurement rather than a collision. None is new and none is caused by the face.
2. Looked at: all twenty-nine files were rendered to PNG at 840x700 and read. One defect was found
   this way and fixed: progress-single's 56px headline figure in a mono face put the decimal point
   in a cell as wide as a digit, so 6.7 read as 6 . 7. That figure now keeps the body face.

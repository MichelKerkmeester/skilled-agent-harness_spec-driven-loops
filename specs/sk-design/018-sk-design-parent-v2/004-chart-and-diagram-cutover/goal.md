# Goal: chart and diagram as sk-design modes

<!-- SPECKIT_TEMPLATE_SOURCE: goal | v2.2 -->

**Inherits the parent `goal.md`. Where this file and that one disagree, that one wins.**

<!-- ANCHOR:directive -->
## 1. DURABLE DIRECTIVE

`sk-create-chart` and `sk-create-diagram` become `sk-design` modes, and a request for a chart reaches
`sk-design` instead of `sk-doc`.

### Decisions

**Why both hubs move in one commit.** A router signal naming a packet that is not on disk fails
check five on whichever hub is wrong. Edit `sk-doc` and `sk-design` together or leave the shared
branch broken between two commits.

**Do not rename anything.** `sk-create-chart` keeps its name inside `sk-design`. A prefix rename
doubles the path rewrite across four mirrors, the shim, the bridges and the canaries, and buys
nothing. Record the prefix mismatch as a known deviation.

### Operator copy

Chart and diagram move under `sk-design`, in one commit, without a rename.
<!-- /ANCHOR:directive -->

<!-- ANCHOR:binding -->
## 2. BINDING

### The surfaces that must both change

Registry rows and their counts in prose, router signals and vocabulary classes and the tie-break
order, root `ROUTER.md` intents and their resource maps, `graph-metadata.json` vocabulary,
`description.json` keywords and prose, the hub `SKILL.md` mode tables, `command-metadata.json` for
`/create:chart` and `/create:diagram`, both leaf manifests regenerated, the Python scorer shim which
hardcodes `diagram` and `flowchart` to `sk-doc`, the command bridges, the four runtime command
mirrors regenerated by their own scripts rather than by hand, and the compiled-routing canaries on
the `sk-doc` side.

### Two silent windows to close explicitly

The advisor daemon keeps serving its previous generation until rebuilt, and `sk-doc` keeps serving
legacy compiled routing until its manifest is re-minted. Neither reports itself. Close both by name.

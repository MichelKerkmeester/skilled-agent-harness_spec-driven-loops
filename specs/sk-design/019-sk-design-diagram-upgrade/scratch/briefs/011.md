# Phase 011 — full-page-capture

**Folder:** `specs/sk-design/019-sk-design-diagram-upgrade/011-full-page-capture`

**Objective (parent decisions D17 and S10).** Captures stop cutting pages off, every diagram screenshot
is re-shot from the merged library, and the capture review runs again with its earlier findings closed.

**The defect.** `.opencode/skills/sk-design/shared/scripts/render-screenshots.cjs` takes a fixed
1280×900 viewport with no full-page flag. Five forms are taller than that and lose their bottom
sections in the committed images: the full starter, the three OAuth sequences and the terminal loop,
plus the consultant quadrant. That crop is not cosmetic — it hid both P1 findings from anyone reviewing
from the committed screenshots, which is exactly what those images are for.

**The constraint that shapes the fix.** The renderer is shared with `sk-design-chart`, and parent
decision D12 holds that nothing changes in the chart skill. So full-page capture arrives as an opt-in
flag the diagram packet passes, and the plan must prove the chart's images are byte-identical after the
change — re-run the chart capture into a scratch directory and diff it against the committed set.

**Deliverables.**
- The flag in the shared renderer, with the measurement it uses to size the viewport stated in the
  script's own comment.
- Every diagram screenshot re-shot from `assets/diagrams/` into `screenshots/diagrams/`, one per form,
  none cropped.
- CAP-001 re-run from the manual testing playbook, producing a second dated report under
  `benchmark/reports/`. The five findings the first report recorded — focal balance on the integration,
  venn and full-starter forms, the swimlane label mask under a substituted font, the renderer crop —
  are each either closed with evidence or carried forward with a reason.
- A note in the playbook that a capture review reads full-page images, so the next reviewer does not
  repeat this discovery.

**What must hold.** No committed screenshot is cut off, verified by comparing each image's height
against its form's rendered content height; the chart skill's images are unchanged, verified by diff;
the second capture report exists with no hand-authored markdown and no empty-reason skip.

**Ordering.** This phase runs last: it shoots the corpus after 007 fixed it, 008 settled its doctrine,
009 moved it and 010 gave it a second way to be painted.

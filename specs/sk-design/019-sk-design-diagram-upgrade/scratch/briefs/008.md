# Phase 008 — doctrine-reconciliation

**Folder:** `specs/sk-design/019-sk-design-diagram-upgrade/008-doctrine-reconciliation`

**Objective.** Each systemic pattern S1–S9 in the manual review is resolved in ONE direction, the
losing document or the losing files are edited to match, and no document is left stating a value the
corpus does not hold. Where a rule can be held by a check, it becomes one.

**The nine, with the direction already chosen. Plan the work, not the debate.**

- **S1 series palette in non-chart types.** The style guide's sanctioned list is what is stale. Widen
  it to multi-series charts plus typed-chip vocabularies, and name the four files that rely on it.
  `example-dp-integration.html:10` also mislabels two hues `--custom-red` / `--custom-blue`; rename
  them to what they are.
- **S2 `soft` carries text in ten files.** The recorded departure wins: `soft` stays structural at
  3.48:1 and every `<text>` fill using it is repointed to muted `#4f5d75`, measured at 6.11:1. The
  departure text in `diagram-palette.json` and `derivation-record.md` stays as written.
- **S3 legend swatches do not match what they key.** Fix the instances (dash arrays `4,3`/`3,3`
  against elements at `5,4`/`4,4`; two legend lines with no `marker-end`; three keyed fills that are
  one grey to the eye), then hold the narrow half with a checker family: a legend swatch's dash array
  must equal some element's dash array in the same file.
- **S4 three legend typographies.** Converge on sentence-case sans, the majority form.
- **S5 legend rule inset from its content.** Set each file's legend rule to that drawing's own
  bounding box.
- **S6 dot pattern on in 26 of 34.** The corpus wins: the guide's sentence becomes dots-on by
  default, with the opt-out named.
- **S7 `rule-solid` disagrees.** The template is the source of truth, as the palette source already
  says. Correct `derivation-record.md` and the style guide's token table to `rgba(79,93,117,0.25)`,
  and delete the `#f7591f` accent citation that appears nowhere in the corpus.
- **S8 labels masked onto short connectors erase them.** One rule fixes F4, F6 and F14: under about
  60 px, the label goes beside the connector with no mask. Promote it to a checker family that
  measures the connector and refuses a mask over it.
- **S9 starters declare tokens then paint from literals.** Every declared role in a starter must be
  referenced at least once inside its drawing, through the CSS classes a starter owns. SVG
  presentation attributes keep literals — they cannot hold a variable, which criterion 4 already
  records — so this is about what a copied starter teaches, not about repainting attributes.

**Two new checker families.** `legend-fidelity` (S3) and `short-connector-labels` (S8). Each needs a

must be green BEFORE a family is added — the suite refuses a case whose base already fails, and that
refusal is the packet's standing rule, not an obstacle to work around.

## Common proof-or-retire decision rule

A classifier candidate advances only when **all** of the following are true:

1. **Population validity:** a consented, time-separated sample of actual eligible events, with the feature's exact eligibility predicate recorded (real Gate 1 request, actual clarify, or below-threshold save of a named path/class).
2. **Independent labels:** final outcomes labeled independently of Jev, with approver/reference and row-level provenance; preserve abstention/none rather than folding it into named-class accuracy.
3. **Pre-registration:** freeze corpus and split, router/scorer/model identity, option/question hashes, strongest safe baseline, alpha, MDE, power, overall and class-specific absolute floors, coverage/abstention limits, and rollback behavior before opening the holdout.
4. **Adequate and honest statistics:** power the relevant paired comparison from its planned effect and eligible volume; report intervals and class/track/hub breakdowns. The 30-row gates are minimum eligibility checks, not evidence of power. Keep a `stop` for low coverage, insufficient rows, or margin uncertainty distinct from a valid prespecified `kill`.
5. **Controls and consumer safety:** pass feature-specific negative controls (including the 022 state mask/distractor, 020 replay/always-none checks, and 017 track/paraphrase checks); retain pinned reports and call provenance; test off, missing-credential, error, timeout, and malformed responses at the live consumer seam.
6. **Staged authorization:** shadow first, then a limited canary with explicit rollback and user confirmation where user-visible, then live only after predeclared shadow/canary floors hold. A scorer `keep` or insertion in the default-on gate is not itself authorization.

**Decision semantics:** a `keep` earns only the next evidence stage when it passes the entire feature-specific external-validity gate; a `kill` retires the tested backend/question at the tested identity only when its prespecified evidence is valid; a `stop` or insufficient eligible sample is unresolved and must not be relabeled as a kill or a keep.

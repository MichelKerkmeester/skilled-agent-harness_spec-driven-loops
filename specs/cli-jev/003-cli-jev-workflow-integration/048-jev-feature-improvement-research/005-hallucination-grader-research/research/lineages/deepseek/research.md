# Jev hallucination grader research — DeepSeek lineage synthesis

Research topic: improve, refine and expand the Jev hallucination grader (cli-jev feature 024), whose agreement arm `score-d4-agreement.cjs` measured `verdict jev: keep K=56 M=56 A=55 B=47 W=8 L=0 F=1 p_win=0.003906` against majority (47/56) and the deterministic check (22/56). Five iterations were run because `stopPolicy: max-iterations` makes early convergence telemetry only; this file consolidates iteration 1-5.

Evidence labels: **Confirmed** — read from source, recorded run data, or reproduced by command; **Derived** — follows from the confirmed counts by stated arithmetic; **Inferred / Unknown** — labeled inline.

## 1. Executive summary

The keep is real but narrower than the headline reads. The grader's 55/56 comes from 47/47 specificity plus 8/9 sensitivity; the comparison baseline collapsed to majority-only because every fixture is missing the allowlist the deterministic check runs on; and the corpus is a 47:9 design in which all positives are one hallucination family. The three highest-value moves are to populate fixture allowlists (which repairs the baseline, anchors the production grader rubric, and unlocks a cheaper cascade), to fix the failed-grade 0.0 before anything is turned on by default, and to add a second-rater audit plus per-class reporting so the next keep is as trustworthy as its arithmetic.

## 3. What drove the measured result (Q1)

Three separable drivers, all Confirmed against the recorded run:

- **The grader discriminated cleanly on this corpus.** Per-call nouls: no-class median 0.07 / p95 0.23 / max 0.43; yes-class median 0.71 / min 0.40; only 8 of 168 calls in [0.40, 0.60). A=55 = 47/47 no rows + 8/9 yes rows. The miss is a threshold false negative on a real hallucination (`require('../../lib/path/split')` + `splitOnSlashes` in `harder-normalize-path.run3`). F=1 comes from `validate-semver.run3` (0.55/0.41/0.54). [SOURCE: file:~/.skilled/.labels/runs/047-024-jev-20261002/calls.jsonl]
- **The baseline collapsed to majority-only.** `chooseBaseline` takes the better of check and majority; check=22 < majority=47, so B is the majority class `no`, right on every no row and none of the 9 yes rows. W=8/L=0 follow structurally. The check's 22 was reproduced exactly (tp=9 tn=13 fp=34 fn=0) and is an allowlist artifact: 0 of 21 fixtures carry allowlists, so every extracted token is unverified and 34 of 47 honest rows are flagged, including the tasks' own function names. [SOURCE: file:.skilled/skills/system-deep-loop/deep-improvement/scripts/model-benchmark/scorer/score-d4-agreement.cjs:240-258] [SOURCE: command:in-memory reproduction]
- **The corpus and the frozen rule convert that into the verdict.** 56 = 42 honest (21 fixtures x 2) + 14 careless run3; the 9 yes labels are exactly the nine hallucinating run3 files; 5 run3 outputs are careless but clean. Gates passed (K=56>=30, 9>=5, 47>=5; headroom `470 > 504` false), and all five keep checks passed: coverage 560>=504, no kill (p_loss=1.0), margin 80>=56, sign 2^-8<0.05, flips 10<=168. ## 4. Raising accuracy or lowering cost (Q2)

Accuracy: allowlists (rec 1), confidence-aware reruns and held-out threshold calibration (rec 3), the production-path failure semantics fix (rec 2). Cost: the arm's cost is linear in reruns x rows — 169 calls = 1 auth + 3 x 56, ~93,003 estimated input tokens, app latency p50 326 ms / p95 434 ms — and the rerun sampling is 168 of those calls. Dropping to 2 reruns would save 33% but F would vanish and 1-1 ties would need a third call anyway; the cascade saves 23% today and more once allowlists cut false positives; answer caching is the remaining lever but it freezes the sampling that F measures, so it belongs in regression reruns, not agreement claims. Multi-row batching and cheaper `JEV_PROVIDER` are plausible but unmeasured (Unknown).

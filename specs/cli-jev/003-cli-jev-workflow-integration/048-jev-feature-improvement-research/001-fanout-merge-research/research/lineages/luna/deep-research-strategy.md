# Deep Research Strategy: Jev fan-out merge

## Research Topic

Explain the measured Jev keep result, find accuracy and cost improvements, assess label and sample trust, identify adjacent .skilled uses, and define the requirements, costs, and risks for any default-on integration.

## Known Context

- This detached lineage is bound directly to the configured lineage artifact directory. The artifact root resolver is skipped.
- All created and modified files stay inside this Luna lineage directory. The parent packet, shared skills, scorer, workflow, continuity state, and git state are outside the write boundary.
- The forced-depth contract is three iterations, with a 0.05 convergence threshold and max-iterations stop policy. Convergence before the cap is telemetry only.
- The scorer reuses mergeResearchRegistries and mergeReviewRegistries, measures only eligible candidate pairs, and compares Jev with the better of the current dedup-on and dedup-off settings.
- The measured set was 60 cross-body pairs with 48 same and 12 different labels. Near-line pairs were absent.
- The incumbent says different on all cross-body pairs. Jev scored 53/60, versus 12/60 for the incumbent.
- A constant-same reference would score 48/60 on this label mix. It is a derived reference, not a measured arm.
- The arbiter was an operator-delegated Opus 5.5 medium run. Two blind drafts agreed on 57/60.
- The sample is bounded by lexical candidate rules and is not a full merge-population estimate.
- The 60-pair evaluation planned 181 Jev calls. The repository does not contain provider spend or all row-level run artifacts.
- The scorer's research and review rows are aggregated in one keep line. The evidence does not establish review-specific performance.
- The existing merge remains deterministic and the Jev arm is still a separate shadow measurement. There is no named reader or promotion authority.
- RRF fusion and context compaction are possible .skilled evaluation sites, but they need separate gold labels.
- The packet resource map is absent; the coverage gate is skipped.

## Key Questions

- What drove the result?
- How can accuracy improve or call cost fall?
- How trustworthy is the measurement and what would improve it?
- Which other .skilled judgment surfaces could benefit?
- What would default-on integration require, cost, and risk?

## Answered Questions

- Iteration 1: The incumbent misses the 48 same-labeled cross-body pairs. Jev is 53/60 overall, five correct pairs above the majority-label reference. The result says nothing about near-line pairs.
- Iteration 2: The score is valid for its adjudicated lexical candidate set. Improve trust with balanced class and run strata, held-out runs, an operator audit, and per-class metrics. Production cost can fall with a candidate shortlist and a separately measured low-call policy.
- Iteration 3: Review merging and spec-kit retrieval or compaction are possible use cases, but need separate evaluation. A production model stage needs a new authorization and privacy contract, bounded cost and time, per-pair audit data, a named reader, and deterministic no-loss fallback.

## What Worked

- Reading the scorer and production merge together clarified what the counts measure.
- The 030 arbiter log supplied the pair-class makeup and its same/different rule.
- Iteration 1: tracing candidate classes exposed the unmeasured near-line case.
- Iteration 2: matching the label protocol to the private artifact trail exposed the exact reproducibility gap and call count.
- Iteration 3: reading both fan-out paths alongside retrieval and compaction clarified the distinct semantic tasks and default-on constraints.

## What Failed

- Aggregate output does not expose Jev's full confusion matrix by label or by source run.
- The repository result does not include row-level labels, observed wall times, provider spend, or review-specific accuracy.

## Exhausted Approaches

- The current report line alone cannot establish general accuracy outside the sampled candidate filter.
- The existing same-body flag cannot implement semantic cross-body judging.

## Ruled-Out Directions

- Rerunning the scorer or collecting a new measurement is outside this phase.
- Changing the scorer, live merge, workflow, or dedup default is outside this research-only phase.
- Claiming the result validates near-line matching is unsupported because that class was empty.
- Claiming dollar cost from call count or token estimate alone is unsupported.
- Treating the keep as permission to promote a live model decision is unsupported because the result names no reader.
- Synthesizing before three iterations is prohibited even if convergence telemetry falls below the threshold.

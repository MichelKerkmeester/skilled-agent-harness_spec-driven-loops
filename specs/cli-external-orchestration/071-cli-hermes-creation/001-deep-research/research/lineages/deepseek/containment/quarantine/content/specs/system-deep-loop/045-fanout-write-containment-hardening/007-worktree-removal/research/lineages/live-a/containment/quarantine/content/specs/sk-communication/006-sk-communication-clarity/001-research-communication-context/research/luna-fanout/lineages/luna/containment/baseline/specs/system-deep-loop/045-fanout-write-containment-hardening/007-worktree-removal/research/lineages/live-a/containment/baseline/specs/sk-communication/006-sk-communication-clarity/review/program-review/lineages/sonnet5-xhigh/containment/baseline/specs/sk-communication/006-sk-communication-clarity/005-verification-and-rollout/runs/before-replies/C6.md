Twelve rule files under repo-rules/, each listed with what it governs.

- **blast-radius.md.** Reversibility: the stakes read, the ladder from trivially reversible to irreversible, the rollback sentence, what still speaks the old contract.
- **communication.md.** How a reply reads: sentence shape, plain words, punctuation, length, filler.
- **delegation-and-orchestration.md.** Work handed to another runtime: the brief carries evidence, the return is unverified until you check it.
- **evidence-and-proof.md.** What counts as proof: receipts for load-bearing claims, how a green run lies, the honest close-out.
- **handoff-and-questions.md.** The end of a turn: what is now the operator's to do, and when a question becomes a structured choice.
- **presenting-decisions.md.** How a finding, fork or synthesis is handed over: verdict first, one recommended path, the intended path stated before long work.
- **prevent-overengineering.md.** Whether a thing should exist and at what size: the smallest thing that solves the stated problem.
- **prose-mechanics.md.** How a sentence reads at the mechanical level: paragraph shape, word choice, punctuation, the connective tissue between sentences.
- **root-cause-and-debugging.md.** Fixing failures: fix the producer, not the symptom, and stop patching when attempts repeat without new evidence.
- **scope-discipline.md.** What you may change: the requested scope is the deliverable, adjacent defects get named, not fixed.
- **skill-hub-routing.md.** What you may claim about routing you wired into a parent-hub skill: registered is not routed, and you check the hub you actually changed.
- **uncertainty-and-honesty.md.** What you may claim when unsure: never fabricate, mark the confidence you have, halt on contradictions.

Nothing is blocked on you.

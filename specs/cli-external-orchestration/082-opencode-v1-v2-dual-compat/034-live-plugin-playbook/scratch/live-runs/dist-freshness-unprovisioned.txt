Side finding: a changed dist in a package with no node_modules of its own reads as unprovisioned, not stale.
Reproduced 2026-10-20 in worktree 174 with the shared checker (.skilled/skills/system-spec-kit/runtime/cli/lib/dist-freshness.cjs).

before the change:
system-spec-kit/runtime fresh stale=false node_modules=false
after appending one byte to runtime/dist/tsconfig.tsbuildinfo:
system-spec-kit/runtime unprovisioned stale=false node_modules=false
after restoring the file:
system-spec-kit/runtime fresh stale=false node_modules=false

## 2. PROBLEM & PURPOSE

### Problem Statement

OpenAI's GPT-6 Luna and Sol are live. Codex's model cache lists `gpt-6-luna` and `gpt-6-sol`, `opencode models openai` lists both plus their `-fast` variants, and LLM Gateway's `/v1/models` lists both as bare ids. The ids are `gpt-6-luna` and `gpt-6-sol`; the "6.0" the request used is not part of the id. The cli-codex, cli-opencode, cli-pi and cli-hermes documents, the deep-loop allowlists that decide whether a Pi, Hermes or Codex dispatch resolves at all, `.pi/settings.json`, and the pi fast-mode extension all still name `gpt-5.6-luna` and `gpt-5.6-sol`. Pi reaches Luna only through the `openai-codex` subscription route, and its bundled catalog carries no GPT-6 Luna or Sol id on any provider, so a renamed `openai-codex/gpt-6-luna` entry would not resolve without a definition. cli-claude-code's living docs name `claude-opus-4-6` and `claude-opus-4-8`; they hold no "opus 5.0" string, and the current Opus id is `claude-opus-5-5`.

### Purpose

Every living dispatch surface names the GPT-6 Luna and Sol ids and every paired roster copy moves with it. Pi and Hermes both reach `gpt-6-luna` through LLM Gateway, and cli-claude-code names `claude-opus-5-5` as its one Opus id.

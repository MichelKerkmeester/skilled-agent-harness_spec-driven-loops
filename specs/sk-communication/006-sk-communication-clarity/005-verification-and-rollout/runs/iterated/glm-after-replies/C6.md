Twelve rule files under repo-rules/, counted from the directory itself rather than the injected router. They expand the root REPO RULES.md, which routes to them and holds no rules of its own.

**Building and wiring**
- prevent-overengineering.md: whether a thing should exist at all and at what size.
- scope-discipline.md: what you may change. The requested scope is the deliverable, adjacent defects get named, not fixed.
- blast-radius.md: what a change can break, what undoing costs and when you must stop for a yes.
- skill-hub-routing.md: when a skill mode may be called routed. Registration is only the first of two stages.

**Evidence and failure**
- evidence-and-proof.md: what counts as proof, what a green run hides and what a close-out owes.
- uncertainty-and-honesty.md: what to do when you do not know. Never fabricate, mark the confidence you actually have, halt on contradictions.
- root-cause-and-debugging.md: what to do when something fails. Fix the producer and name the mechanism.

**Handing over**
- delegation-and-orchestration.md: what changes when work goes to another model. You brief it with evidence and you verify what returns.
- presenting-decisions.md: how a decision reaches the reader. Verdict first, one recommended path, assumptions made visible.
- handoff-and-questions.md: how a turn ends. Its last line names what is the operator's to do, in the form that lets them do it.

**How the reply reads**
- communication.md: the reply as a whole. Its first line carries the payload, and no table stands in it.
- communication-prose.md: the sentence. One idea each, plain words, no em dash, no semicolon, no serial comma.

Nothing here is yours to do.

**The qualifier, as it stands at evidence-and-proof.md:71-76:**

> When a report leads with a cause, the cause carries its standing, and the cause-then-fix order holds. Where no run confirms the cause, label it as suspected and name the next check. A confirmed cause is reported as a finding. Against the tiers, a suspected cause is INFERRED, and the INFERRED tier already demands what would confirm it, so the clause adds a reporting shape and takes nothing from the proof. There is no unmarked case: a cause is either a finding with its receipt or suspected with its next check.

**The three adversarial readings the qualifier record's risk row demands, and why each fails:**

1. **A cause with no status.** It fails because the clause leaves no unmarked case, a cause is either a finding with its receipt or suspected with its next check, so a bare cause satisfies neither.
2. **A status word that reads as confirmation.** It fails because the clause fixes the two reportable forms, the finding that a receipt backs and the suspected marking with its next check, and a hedge satisfies neither.
3. **A fix offered with no cause.** It fails because the clause governs reports that lead with a cause, so where no cause was identified there is nothing to mark, and the next check is where the question stays open.

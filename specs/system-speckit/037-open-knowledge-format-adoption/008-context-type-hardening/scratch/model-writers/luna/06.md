# Fix Job Queue Claim Race

## Problem

When workers poll the queue concurrently, more than one worker can observe the same job as available and start it. This can cause duplicate side effects, inconsistent job state, or wasted work.

## Goal

Ensure that each queued job is claimed by at most one worker at a time, including when multiple workers attempt to claim it concurrently.

## Requirements

- Claiming a job must be atomic: only one worker may transition a job from queued to running.
- A worker that loses the claim race must not execute the job.
- A successful claim must record enough information to identify the worker and the claim time.
- Existing retry and failure behavior must continue to work.
- If the queue uses expiring claims or leases, an expired claim must allow the job to be claimed again.
- Claim attempts must not leave a job stuck in a running state when the claim fails.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Two or more workers racing to claim one queued job result in exactly one successful claim.
- Only the winning worker executes the job.
- Concurrent claims for different jobs can proceed independently.
- A failed or retried job follows the existing retry policy and can be claimed again when eligible.
- A claim that expires under the queue’s existing lease policy can be recovered without allowing the expired worker to overwrite a newer claim.

## Out of Scope

- Changing job ordering or scheduling policy.
- Changing job payloads or worker APIs beyond what atomic claiming requires.
- Preventing duplicate side effects caused by a worker retry after it has already begun execution.

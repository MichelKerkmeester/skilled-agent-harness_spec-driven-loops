# Document an Incident Review

## Purpose

Create a clear record of what happened during an incident, its impact, how responders handled it, and what actions will reduce the chance or cost of recurrence.

## Requirements

- Record established facts separately from hypotheses and unresolved questions.
- Describe events and decisions without assigning personal blame.
- Include enough detail for readers who did not participate to understand the incident.
- Give each follow-up action an owner and a target date, or mark these as unassigned.
- Protect sensitive information and link to restricted evidence rather than copying it into a broadly accessible review.

## Review template

### Summary

- **Incident:** Short name
- **Date and duration:** Include time zone
- **Severity:** Use the organization’s severity scale
- **Status:** Draft, under review, or complete
- **Review owner:** Name or team

Summarize what happened, who or what was affected, and how service or operations were restored.

### Impact

Describe the scope, duration, and user, customer, business, or operational effects. Include known quantities where available; label estimates.

### Timeline

List significant events in chronological order. Include timestamps with time zone and distinguish detection, response, mitigation, and recovery.

### Response

Record the key actions taken, decisions made, and any delays or obstacles. Explain what helped and what made response harder.

### Findings

Describe confirmed causes and contributing conditions. Note evidence, remaining uncertainty, and questions requiring further investigation. Avoid presenting assumptions as facts.

### Follow-up actions

| Action | Owner | Target date | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| | | | |

Actions should address prevention, detection, response, or recovery. Keep them specific and verifiable.

## Completion criteria

A review is complete when:

- The summary and impact are understandable and supported by available evidence.
- The timeline covers the significant events and includes time zones.
- Findings distinguish confirmed facts from uncertainty.
- Follow-up actions have owners and target dates, or are explicitly marked unassigned.
- The review has been checked for sensitive information and shared with its intended audience.

<!-- ANCHOR:adr-001-context -->
### Context

The research cut list marks five rows as verified. One (`if_cli_copilot`) was already removed in phase 006, one (`CROSS_ANCHOR_CONTAMINATION`) is assigned to phase 010, and the rest are "child evidence only". The lead set the rule for sk-design: `rg` the whole repository outside `specs/` and `node_modules` for each name and stem, and delete a file only when its sole references are the leaf-manifest entry and its own screenshot.

### Constraints

- `source-globals.css` is cited by `origin.md` (four lines, including a hash row) and the v0.14.0.0 changelog.
<!-- /ANCHOR:adr-001-context -->

---

<!-- ANCHOR:adr-001-decision -->
### Decision

**We chose**: delete `branch-leases-waves` with its suite, and the ordered and categorical palette sheets with their screenshots; keep `source-globals.css`.

**How it works**: the two type-only importers declare the structural fields they read; docs and the leaf manifest drop the deleted paths; the four fixtures that name them are regenerated by their own generators.
<!-- /ANCHOR:adr-001-decision -->

**Why this one**: it follows the lead's condition and the verified list.

<!-- ANCHOR:problem -->
## 2. PROBLEM & PURPOSE

### Problem Statement
The doctor retrieval target declared nine staleness signals and computed six: no activity produced a fresh manifest, ran the recipe without `--no-config`, or scanned anchors, and the lookup pass policy named a frozen prompt set no activity read. Its recipe dropped two flags the convention lists. The conventions said the two lanes share no mechanism while their own section 5 names the shared helpers, double-booked one loss across two rows, described the warn-on classes without the judge's numbers or names, and left the judge's seventh class out of the doctor's pollution row. One allowlist reason described an engine tree that no longer exists, the README called the residue sweep a one-shot check, and neither guidance surface for trigger-phrase authors pointed at the judge.

### Purpose
A diagnostic claims only what it runs, a convention carries the numbers the code enforces, and an author who writes a trigger phrase can find the rule that will judge it.
<!-- /ANCHOR:problem -->

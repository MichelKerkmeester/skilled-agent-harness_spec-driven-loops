#### Pi CLI becomes the sixth orchestration executor mode

#### RCAF becomes the data-driven default CLI prompt framework
cli-devin's prompt scaffolding moved from anecdote to evidence. A 42-dispatch optimization pitting five prompt scaffolds against a seven-case failure benchmark on the SWE 1.6 model found the RCAF structure (Role/Context/Action/Format) outscoring the previous STAR default by 33% (0.58 vs 0.44); a rerun and a 70-dispatch check across two other frontier models confirmed the ranking holds everywhere. So v1.0.5.0 makes RCAF the default for SWE-1.6 dispatches, with medium-density pre-planning (three to four steps — dense plans underperformed) and standard bundle-gate wording, since strict wording pushes the model toward defensive output. STAR stays available for narrative-heavy jobs and BUILD for multi-file refactors, but the two should not be combined. The medium-pre-planning guidance also became the shared standard across the master prompt card and the four sibling CLI skills.

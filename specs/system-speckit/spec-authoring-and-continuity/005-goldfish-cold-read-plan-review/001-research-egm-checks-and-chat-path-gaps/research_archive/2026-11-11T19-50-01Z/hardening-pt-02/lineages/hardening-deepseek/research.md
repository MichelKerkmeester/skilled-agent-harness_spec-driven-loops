# Research: Hardening the spec-kit plan, implement and complete workflows

Lineage `hardening-deepseek`, five iterations, fan-out session `fanout-hardening-deepseek-1794422967432-gmxkv6`. Every claim traces to a numbered finding in `iterations/iteration-00N.md`; citations are inline.

## 1. The question

Which existing logic in `speckit-plan.yaml`, `speckit-implement.yaml` and `speckit-complete.yaml` can be hardened — judged from the three prior reads and their unadopted findings — with no new commands, agents, documents or metrics files? Eight leads were supplied; each is confirmed, refuted or narrowed below, and the sweep found eight more defects the leads did not name.

## 2. Verdict

**The family's real enforcement is prose inside steps, and the blocks that look like enforcement are declarations nothing reads.** That single sentence explains most of the findings:

1. `quality_gates` (three definitions), `circuit_breaker` (three), `confidence_framework`, `request_analysis_framework` and `workflow_enforcement` are read by no step. The keys that bind are `on_fail`, `action_if_failed`, `enforcement: HARD_BLOCK` and an explicit `STOP` written inside a step body.
2. Because the dead blocks carry the reassuring words — `threshold: 70`, `blocking: hard` — they hide the absences underneath them. The clearest case: no step in any of the three files stops on a failing test suite.
3. The mode contract is one-directional. Auto skips 32 confirm-only checkpoints (by rule), including the two that hold the only resolution branch out of a failed check; meanwhile eight step-level confidence blocks survive into auto and seven of them wait for a user who is not there.
4. `speckit-complete.yaml` is a second implementation of plan and implement, and the copies have drifted where they decide behaviour — three confidence weight models, three contradictory circuit-breaker failure actions, three names for the pre-change checklist.

## 5. What was checked and did not hold

- **The documentation-level tables have not drifted.** `required_files` are identical per level across all three files; only `loc_guidance` strings differ (`"<100 LOC"` vs `"<100"`). The drift is behavioural.
- **The 32 confirm-only checkpoints are not 32 defects.** Most duplicate a step-level activity that still runs. Two steps have no other exit, and those are rank 4.
- **The dead gate is not a hidden gate.** No `.md` command file, no skill and no runtime script in the repository reads `quality_gates` or `circuit_breaker`; the keys are definition-only, and the same `circuit_breaker` boilerplate appears in more than twenty `create/` assets.

## 6. Level impact of the whole set

- **L1** (<100 LOC): ranks 1, 2, 3, 9, 10, 11, 13, 14 touch it. The quick-fix path is where #10 and #11 bite; the rest are shared blocks.
- **L2** (100-499): adds ranks 4, 5, 6, 7, 12 — this is the level where the acceptance gate and `AC_CLOSURE` are the only closure mechanism, so rank 5 costs most here.
- **L3** (>=500): the same set as L2; no Level 3+ row exists in the structural contract, so nothing scales past L3 except by phase children.

## 7. What not to do

- **Do not wire `quality_gates`.** With 26 undefined check ids, wiring it would gate on a rubric invented at the same moment as the gate — a new metrics surface by another name.
- **Do not reconcile the three `circuit_breaker` semantics.** Three contradictory recovery behaviours with no consumer; reconciling invents a fourth and a consumer for it.
- **Do not fix self-scored confidence with a fresh-reader dispatch.** It changes the plan explorers' conclusion-free contract, already recorded as a separate decision upstream.
- **Do not build a measurement plan on `[DEVIATION:` or debug-delegation counts.** 14 and 1 occurrences respectively across 4,060 packets; the summary section is the populated channel at 5.4%.

## Eliminated Alternatives

- Treating the gate arithmetic as the top defect: it is latent under a dead block and cannot fire today.
- Counting every `applies_to: confirm` block as a defect: the skip is a rule, and only two steps lose their exit with it.
- Reading the `location:` field as an invocation: it names a moment, and no step re-reads the block at that moment.
- Using `[DEVIATION:` counts alone as the emission evidence: the summary section is the second channel and is 15x more populated.
- Reconciling the level tables: they agree.
- Wiring `circuit_breaker`: see §7.
- Adding `mode_overrides.auto` defaults to every confidence checkpoint individually: one block per file is the cheaper move.

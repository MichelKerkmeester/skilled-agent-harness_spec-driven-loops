### C. Q3 — where the gaps iterations 1 and 2 left unowned should live

13. **002's `--apply` mapping is per-machine, and the cheapest mitigation is re-derivation, not new state.** Consumed rows live in gitignored `consumed.jsonl`, so a re-clone forgets that a foreign row already answers a queue record and the record returns to pending. 002 already rejects writing into the foreign record, which is right; the gap is that its repair is not restated as a standing capability.

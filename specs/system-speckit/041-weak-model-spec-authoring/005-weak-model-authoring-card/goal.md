## 1. DURABLE DIRECTIVE

**Objective:** Make one short authoring card the default load for the four authoring intents, and make the card, the router, the inventory and the checklist fail together when one of them drifts.

### Decisions

Frozen choices. Changing one is an amendment.

| ID | Decision |
|----|----------|
| D1 | The card lives at `references/workflows/spec-authoring-card.md`, is 172 lines when copied from `plan.md` section 3.1, and may not exceed 260 lines. |
| D2 | The `PLAN`, `IMPLEMENT`, `INTAKE` and `TEMPLATE_AUTHORING` intents load the card. `references/templates/template-guide.md` is listed in `ON_DEMAND` and in no intent list. |
| D3 | `leaf-manifest.json` and `leaf-aliases.json` are generated by the shipped scripts. No hand edit of either file is allowed. |
| D4 | `check-card-sync.sh` fails on the first disagreement between the four surfaces, and `card-sync.vitest.ts` runs it in the suite. |
| D5 | The authoring checklist sends a reader to a rendered example or a fresh scaffold, never to a `.tmpl` source. |
| D6 | Three Level 1 smoke runs measure the card. They are a smoke test, and phase 009 owns the full before-and-after comparison. |

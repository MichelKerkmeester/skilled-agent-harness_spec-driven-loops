ci(workflows): size every job timeout from its own samples

Thirty-eight jobs carried no timeout, so a hung install or a stuck test
held its runner for GitHub's six-hour default. Every job now declares
one. Each value comes from the job's own start-to-finish timings over
the newest 40 runs of its workflow: the larger of the 10 minute floor,
three times the median and twice the longest run. No successful run
uses more than half of its limit, and the one job with no history yet
takes the floor.

The six timeouts that already existed are re-sized by the same rule.
Four of them fall by more than half (OpenCode battery, both-versions,
plugin and hook tests, rename harness) because those runs finish in
under five minutes.

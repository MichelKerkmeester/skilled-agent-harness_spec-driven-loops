## 1. Verdict

`@spec-kit/shared` is **two packages wearing one directory**. A live core (~60% of the 33.5k lines) — the parsing/save-gates, the advisor's embeddings chain, gate-3-classifier, the compact-merge budget, the retrieval helpers — is production-terminated at every tier and current. Around it: a fully-built, fully-tested, entirely unwired second package (8 of 33 surfaces, ≈2,300+ lines: the embeddings monolith + its barrel + the runtime shim, the F3.1 ranking quartet, the retrieval-trace contracts, the predicate/chunk islands, the retry orphan) that nothing in production imports. Meanwhile the seams — not the modules — carry the defect: a proven-stale dist guarded by an unprovisionable freshness engine, five-plus derivation systems for one database directory whose target doesn't exist, a 24-variable environment read documented at 7, and every coupling enforced by convention. Memory-DB residue: yes — concentrated in six loci, named below, not diffuse. No P0 (nothing broken at the call sites); the P1 ledger = what a maintainer deletes, merges, or unwinds.

## Answers

Q1. F-04 is the consequential false-positive check because step 18 can pass without the backups its rollbacks consume. F-06 is a second instance because step 11 validates the index before, rather than the commit after, the required commit operation.

Q2. F-05 shows that steps 22 and 23 omit reversal of hook links and home configuration. F-03 shows that step 24’s recovery omits ignored state. F-04 shows that the required hook and home backups are not themselves gated.

Q3. F-01 breaks the hook-ordering constraint implied by the dangling-hook probe. “Directly after” the fast-forward is too late because the main-checkout landing is not an atomic replacement.

Q4. F-02 is the contract break. The global Hermes launcher is changed to the new shape before consumer projects that still expose only `.opencode` receive a compatible `.skilled` path.

Q5. F-07 means step 24 is correctly the moved-tree publication boundary, but not the first point requiring a forward fix. Step 5 already crosses the distributed boundary. No earlier step makes physical restoration impossible if the backup amendments land, while F-03 shows that step 24’s current recovery procedure is incomplete.

Final reorganization wave that closes `026-graph-and-context-optimization`. The move contract is `scratch/rename-plan.json` (generated by `scratch/build-rename-plan.js`): 46 disjoint, naming-clean moves. Preflight baselines live in `scratch/baselines/`.

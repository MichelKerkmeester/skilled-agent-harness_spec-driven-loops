# Research Two Caching Libraries

## Objective

Produce a concise comparison of two caching libraries that could serve the same use case in the target language or ecosystem.

## Requirements

- Identify the target language, ecosystem, and caching use case.
- Select two relevant libraries and explain why they are suitable for comparison.
- Compare supported cache types, eviction and expiration behavior, concurrency, persistence, integrations, maintenance, licensing, and operational tradeoffs where applicable.
- Use current, authoritative sources such as official documentation, repositories, and release notes. Link sources and distinguish sourced facts from assessment.
- Recommend one library for the stated use case, or explain why neither is a clear fit.

## Deliverable

A short research report containing:

1. The use case and selection rationale.
2. A side-by-side comparison.
3. Key tradeoffs and limitations.
4. A recommendation with supporting reasons.
5. Links to the sources consulted.

## Acceptance Criteria

- The report covers exactly two libraries aimed at the same use case.
- Comparison criteria are relevant to that use case and applied consistently.
- Material claims are supported by linked sources.
- The recommendation follows from the comparison and states any important assumptions.

## Out of Scope

- Implementing or integrating either library.
- Benchmarking, unless reliable comparable results are readily available and relevant.

## ADR-001: Preserve the seven inherited substrate boundaries

### Metadata

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| **Status** | Accepted |
| **Date** | 2026-07-28 |
| **Deciders** | Deep Improvement Common rollback-gate owner |

---

<!-- ANCHOR:adr-001-context -->
### Context

The golden rollback gate closed the locally enforceable evidence classes and identified seven places where its available substrate handles cannot prove provenance. The common-service clone has the same handle boundaries. Adding local assertions that resemble stronger proof would create a false authority claim and break parity with the accepted golden contract.

### Constraints

- The leaf may use only the landed common siblings and frozen shared substrates.
- The leaf remains additive-dark and cannot add source handles to shared APIs.
- Phase 014 owns cross-store, historical, and authority-transition correlation.
<!-- /ANCHOR:adr-001-context -->

---

<!-- ANCHOR:adr-001-decision -->
### Decision

**We chose**: Mirror all seven boundaries as explicit field-source dispositions and keep their phase-014 obligations visible.

**How it works**: Locally available evidence is re-derived, cross-checked, validated, snapshotted, and digest-bound. Evidence without an authoritative source handle is recorded as a bounded descriptor or assertion and never relabeled as direct observation.

| Boundary | Local enforcement | Required external correlation |
|----------|-------------------|-------------------------------|
| Retained ledger and artifact counts | All four before-and-after assertions are validated, equality-checked, and gateway-bound | Phase 014 reads complete totals from the real ledger and artifact store |
| Shared-contract and write-set descriptors | Both digests are format-checked and signed into the exact certificate | Phase 014 supplies them from its pinned integration contract |
| Lifecycle-kind semantics | Every row uses a distinct authenticated event and receipt identity | Dedicated substrate kinds are required before per-label semantics can be claimed |
| Health aggregate authenticity | Healthy or recovered state is required and the complete aggregate digest is bound | Signed observations or authenticated projector state must establish provenance |
| Resume, window, and risk completeness | Complete resume and window structures are bound, safe-value checked, and deduplicated; passing risks must be empty | Signed resume references, historical window certificates, and the authoritative risk registry establish origin and completeness |
| Complete rollback request binding | Every declared field is externally bound, independently verified, cross-checked, a closed validated value, or a deterministic constant | Phase 014 still supplies authoritative sources for bounded assertions |
| Historical lease identity | The complete tuple is validated and bound; the real coordinator proves canonical resource and strict token supersession | Coordinator or ledger history correlates historical lease, owner, and correlation identities |
<!-- /ANCHOR:adr-001-decision -->

---

<!-- ANCHOR:adr-001-alternatives -->
### Alternatives Considered

| Option | Pros | Cons | Score |
|--------|------|------|-------|
| **Mirror the boundaries and require phase-014 correlation** | Matches the golden contract and avoids false provenance | Leaves explicit integration work | 10/10 |
| Treat bound caller assertions as direct observations | Requires no new integration | Claims evidence the leaf cannot observe | 1/10 |
| Add new source handles to frozen substrate | Could enforce provenance locally | Violates scope and changes shared contracts | 3/10 |

**Why this one**: It closes every enforceable class while preserving an honest account of what only the authority-transition layer can prove.
<!-- /ANCHOR:adr-001-alternatives -->

---

<!-- ANCHOR:adr-001-consequences -->
### Consequences

**What improves**:
- The common gate has the same evidence meaning as the golden gate.
- Phase 014 receives an explicit source map instead of ambiguous green fields.

**What it costs**:
- Readiness remains insufficient for authority transition. Mitigation: phase 014 verifies every bounded source before using the certificate.

**Risks**:

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|--------|------------|
| A consumer treats a bounded descriptor as authoritative provenance | High | Require phase-014 source correlation before any authority decision |
| A downstream lane forks the boundary meanings | High | Reuse the common exported gate, switch, and window identities unchanged |
<!-- /ANCHOR:adr-001-consequences -->

<!-- ANCHOR:adr-001-impl -->
### Implementation

**What changes**:
- The mode gate binds complete health, resume, window, risk, descriptor, and lifecycle evidence under the documented meanings.
- The rollback switch binds every request field and proves strict resource/token supersession without claiming historical grant identity.

**How to roll back**: Remove the new common rollback-gate module, focused test, and leaf completion docs together. No authority state or production data requires reversal because the implementation remains additive-dark.

### Summary

This phase defines the intelligence layer over the durable deep-loop substrate: concept-level semantic communities, typed contradiction and supersession events, stable claim continuity, next-focus semantics, and deterministic transactional projections and gauges that convergence consumes. It answers what is conceptually new, what conflicts with prior knowledge, how a claim persists across iterations, and where investigation should move next, on top of the ledger and fan-in substrate. Per the group parent phase map, this phase is complete; all five children report delivered implementations.

|---|---|
| `001-semantic-communities` | Deterministic semantic-community projection over claim nodes so paraphrases resolve to one concept while legacy graph novelty remains authoritative. |

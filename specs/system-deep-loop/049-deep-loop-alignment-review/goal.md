## 1. DURABLE DIRECTIVE

**Objective:** Every deep-loop surface says the same thing as every other and as the code: routers and their JSON metadata, SKILL.md against its references and assets, catalogs, playbooks, READMEs, commands and agents against the runtime, executor parity across every CLI kind, the architecture read as one system, and all of it held to the repo rules.

### Decisions

Frozen choices. Changing one is an amendment.

| ID | Decision |
|----|----------|
| D1 | Phase 001 ran twenty iterations, four lanes of five in two waves, max-iterations with convergence off, wave-two angles rewritten from wave one, on DeepSeek V4.1 Flash max and GLM 5.3 Flash max via the cli-pi gateway. |
| D2 | Nothing is deferred: every confirmed finding at every tier becomes a phase, fixed by DeepSeek V4.1 Flash max on cli-pi, one dispatch per phase, suite-verified before the next; a refuted finding is recorded with its reason. |
| D3 | Routing artifacts agree exactly per hub: SKILL.md, ROUTER.md, mode-registry.json, hub-router.json, leaf-manifest.json, graph-metadata.json, description.json. |
| D4 | The repo rules bind the fixes: the smallest change that closes a finding, no abstraction without a current requirement, comments carrying the durable why. |

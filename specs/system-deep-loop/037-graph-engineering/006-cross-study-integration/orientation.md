## 1. THE FIVE STUDIES IN ONE LINE EACH

## 2. THE CROSS-CUTTING SPINE

- **Proposal never implies authority — DIRECTLY STATED across all five.** S1's work graph proposes and its compiler seals; S2's beliefs, refusals, claims, and human choices remain non-executable evidence; S3 makes admission explicitly distinct from authorization; S4 keeps every knowledge artifact non-authoritative; S5 separates a valid iteration result from an authorized transition. The common invariant is: **every protected consequence becomes one exact canonical request that only the designated 036 plane may authorize, fence, append, and receipt**. Today this remains target architecture because 036 runs dark and legacy writers remain operationally authoritative.

- **Two orthogonal layer models — INFERENCE from DIRECTLY STATED boundaries.** S1–S4 define the graph/control side: organization policy, per-run work proposal, sealed execution topology, ledger-derived projections, and evidence/knowledge/belief machinery. S5 does not add another authority plane; it defines the harness inside each executable node or typed loop subgraph. Thus "graph versus loop" is composition, not competition: the graph determines eligible work and dependencies; the harness governs how one bounded iteration produces a candidate.

- **One monotonic evidence/gate stack — INFERENCE, with each boundary directly supplied.** The integrated order is: `return-shape admission → artifact/evidence and trajectory acceptance → purpose-bound belief settlement → mode-specific convergence/STOP legality → exact 036 request`. Failure at an earlier layer cannot be repaired by a later score, while success at an earlier layer grants no later authority. S5 supplies shape/evidence separation; S2 supplies belief and checked settlement; S1 supplies convergence as typed subgraph control; S3 supplies admission and authorization separation.

- **Memory, knowledge, and belief form a pipeline, not interchangeable stores — INFERENCE grounded in explicit non-collision rules.** S5 memory optimizes what the loop recalls and may suppress or abstract only derived retrieval state. S4 knowledge production creates provenance-bearing assertions through ontology, extraction, quality, and reversible fusion. S2 belief settlement decides whether those assertions are usable premises for a particular purpose. The composition is therefore `memory locates → knowledge supplies assertions → belief settles usability`; authoritative history is always read through from its owner, and none of the three authorizes STOP or mutation.

- **One promotion-evidence model — INFERENCE from three directly compatible test systems.** S2 compares every causal prefix and identifies the earliest divergence; S3 injects governance faults and requires failure at the earliest responsible owner; S5 adds harness faults covering return shape, evidence, memory, context, fan-in, locking, and capability escalation. S4 adds conjunctive data- and retrieval-quality evidence, while S1 supplies golden cross-adapter traces. Promotion evidence should therefore be one reference-closed bundle containing case and mutant manifests, prefix comparisons, earliest-owner localization, forbidden-event/effect assertions, data/retrieval quality, build and policy digests, and measured baselines. It proposes promotion; 036 alone selects a writer or cuts over.

- **A unified staged sequence — INFERENCE preserving the studies' original axes.** The nine S1 stages remain the delivery spine; S2's eight-stage dependency order constrains safety inside it; S3's ten tranches install governance across it; S5 makes mutants a prerequisite. The resulting sequence is: (1) audit 036 capabilities, freeze contracts, capture legacy baselines, and assemble mutants; (2) shadow typed IR, refusals, replay, traces, and typed returns; (3) execute pure reducers/nodes; (4) add read-only fan-out, bounded context, and advisory memory; (5) introduce claimant-addressed fenced writes, budgets, and effect recovery; (6) add prospective truth admission, belief control, typed gates, and mode subgraphs; (7) compile organization policy and permit bounded generated work graphs; (8) qualify the S4 knowledge pipeline and hybrid serving; (9) progress through dark read, reversible canary, selected writer/effect canary, mode-by-mode cutover, and rehearsed rollback.

## 3. TENSIONS AND CONTRADICTIONS

- **Curated memory versus settled belief.** S5 encourages merge, abstraction, suppression, and decay; S2 requires contradictions, supersession, and premise status to remain truth-maintained. Resolution: memory operations alter retrieval projections only, with complete source handles and never-forget classes; they cannot rewrite assertions, resolve contradiction, or close a question.

- **Programmable LEAF tactics versus typed-subgraph ownership.** S5 permits local helper code and adaptive tactics; S1 requires typed subgraphs, while S3 requires sealed executable meaning and policy admission. Resolution: a LEAF may choose only from a closed local action vocabulary. Parallel work, topology changes, new tools, budgets, gates, or protected effects must be emitted as typed proposals for workflow/compiler handling.

- **"Prefer newer" versus semantic supersession.** S4's recency advice is valid only for retrieval after provenance, valid time, observation time, contradiction, and supersession have been processed. S2's composite ordering and prospective truth admission remain controlling. Resolution is settled: recency ranks candidates; it never determines truth.

- **Many projections versus one source of truth.** Checkpoints, graph state, belief, memory, evidence graphs, parity records, OTel, and budget observations risk becoming competing authorities. Resolution: each projection declares its owner, source cut, reducer/version, freshness, and failure state; unresolved references make it unavailable rather than approximate. Authoritative records stay in their owning ledgers.

- **Target authority versus present runtime reality.** Studies 1–4 often speak as though 036 already controls transitions; S5 establishes that it runs dark and returns legacy outcomes unchanged. Resolution: all subordination statements are target-state contracts until an audited, measured, operator-gated cutover. Open question: which assumed revalidation, fencing, budget, effect, and rollback primitives actually exist in 036, and which must be built in the adapter?

## 4. THE SINGLE INTEGRATED ARCHITECTURE

The target `system-deep-loop` is a **graph-governed, event-derived agent-loop engine**. A stable organization graph defines capability, policy, ownership, and budget ceilings. A per-run work graph proposes bounded work. A deterministic compiler admits and seals that proposal into typed executable topology. Nodes may contain direct actions or mode-specific loop subgraphs; inside each iteration, the S5 harness exposes bounded context, closed local tactics, typed returns, and reducer-owned memory proposals.

Every state-changing graph edge follows the authority spine: proposal → structural admission and sealing → evidence/trajectory evaluation → applicable belief settlement and convergence decision → live policy, budget, gate, claim, head, and fence revalidation → exact 036 request → fenced append → separately authorized effect → receipt and reconciliation. Until 036 leaves dark mode, the same chain operates only as shadow evidence beside legacy authority.

Knowledge, memory, evidence, and belief remain layered. S4 produces versioned, provenance-bearing assertions and reversible identity fusion. S5 memory selects and compresses continuity for recall without deleting history. S2 settles purpose-bound premise usability. S1's evidence graph explains coverage and causality. All are disposable or rebuildable projections; authoritative facts are resolved from their owners.

Evaluation is similarly layered: return admission, artifact integrity, evidence/trajectory acceptance, belief settlement, convergence legality, and authorization each produce scoped results rather than a generic `validated` flag. Promotion requires the unified causal-prefix and mutant bundle, conjunctive data/retrieval/runtime quality, recovery drills, forbidden-effect suppression, and measured baselines. That evidence can nominate a canary or cutover; it cannot perform one.

**Design-settled:** proposal/authority separation; plane ownership; typed compiled IR; ledger-derived replay; claimant fencing; prospective truth admission; durable context-sensitive gates; authority-zero refusal; hierarchical budgets; bounded LEAF behavior; memory/knowledge/belief non-collision; and shadow-first, mutant-gated promotion.

**Still open:** the 036-capability audit; authority-owner disagreement arbitration; multi-host fencing and provider-usage normalization; exact gate/refusal persistence; recursive sealed-subgraph composition; concurrency behavior; threshold calibration; and all quality, latency, cost, storage, contention, repair, recall, and operator-load measurements. The architecture is coherent at design level, but production fitness and authority cutover remain unproven.

Define the exact movement from curated recall through assertions and reversible fusion to purpose-bound belief, including read-through rules, retention, supersession, and invalidation. **Yield:** one layering contract and event/reference map that prevents memory curation from becoming truth settlement.

Decide which adaptations stay inside a programmable LEAF and which require graph patches, fan-out, human gates, or separately sealed subgraphs. **Yield:** one closed action-and-escalation vocabulary plus recursive subgraph admission, budget, identity, gate, and replay rules.

- **Priority 7 — Unified rollout and rollback map.** **Studies:** S1, S2, S3, S4, S5. Convert the nine-stage spine, eight-stage safety dependency, ten governance tranches, knowledge maturity gates, and mutants-first harness order into observable promotion checkpoints. **Yield:** one rollout DAG with prerequisites, parallelizable lanes, retained legacy readers/writers, rollback assets, and measurable exit conditions.

Establish whether governance, knowledge production, and harness indirection improve certified outcomes enough to justify their overhead, and define what happens when authoritative owners disagree. **Yield:** one benchmark plan plus an owner-disagreement protocol covering escalation, timeout, quarantine, and operator resolution.

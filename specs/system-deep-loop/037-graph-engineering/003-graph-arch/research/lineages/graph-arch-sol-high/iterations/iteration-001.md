## Findings

1. **Authority and layering — CONFIRM repo study 1's executive decision; REFINE with a pre-authorization admission boundary.** The controlling anchor remains that a compiled work graph proposes transitions while 036 alone admits and appends them. Graphene strengthens the same boundary by requiring every projection consumer to verify current authority state and epoch. GraphARC admission therefore belongs before 036 as evidence, never as a substitute for it. Assumption: admission output is ordinary, replayable data and cannot mint authority. Gap: define which admission facts become non-forgeable proof and which current facts 036 must independently re-bind. When not to use: do not invoke graph admission for a direct deterministic harness action that has no graph topology or policy decision. 5. **Verdicts, approvals, and human gates — REFINE Decisions 3 and 4; EXTEND Graphene P7 with one unified gate protocol.** Repo study 1 requires a verdict to select a declared consequence edge and makes human gates versioned ledger entities. Graphene adds live dependency-vector revalidation, principal/capability binding, fenced timeouts, and separate effect authorization. GraphARC supplies three useful but separate approval styles: policy ASK routing, planner fingerprint handshake, and session node hold. Assumption: a human decision is not a bearer capability and can become stale before consequence append. Gap: unify open → decide → revalidate → append → timeout so no checkpoint, direct graph invocation, or changed topology/evidence/policy/assignment can bypass or reuse approval. When not to use: do not create a durable human gate for acknowledgement, display preference, or an interaction with no consequence edge, mutation, budget change, or downstream effect.

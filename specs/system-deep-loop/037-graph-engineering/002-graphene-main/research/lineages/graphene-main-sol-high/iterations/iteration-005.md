## Findings

1. **REFINE repo-1: parity is a prefix-indexed operation transcript, not one terminal trace.** Repo-1 correctly requires normalized authorized traces, but a useful comparison row is `(operation request, authorization decision, operation outcome, accepted domain range, projection checkpoint)` at every declared semantic boundary. Graphene's final snapshot plus `apply`-versus-`fold_up_to` check cannot detect a defect shared by both reducer paths; 036 already requires the earliest determinable mismatch and forbids exposing a trusted projection after verification failure. Commit external checkpoint expectations with a closed prefix range, normalized full-state digest, and diagnostic selectors immediately before and after claim/revoke, ask/timeout/resolve, denial, effect, retry, and terminal transitions. [SOURCE: specs/system-deep-loop/037-graph-engineering/context/graphene-main/crates/graphene-core/tests/golden.rs:75-100] [SOURCE: specs/system-deep-loop/036-deep-loop-innovation/006-transition-authorized-ledger-core/003-replay-fingerprints/spec.md:95-116] **When not to use:** do not checkpoint every token, heartbeat, or transport callback; checkpoint declared semantic boundaries, and declare a partial order when independent operations may commute.

2. **EXTEND repo-1: a harness is unproven until a mutation matrix makes every comparator fail at the intended layer.** For each positive case, independently delete, insert, duplicate, reorder, and field-mutate an accepted event; remove a required observation; corrupt one prefix checkpoint; change one replay identity; and alter one refusal or effect outcome. The test must assert the exact blocking class and earliest mismatch, not merely “certificate absent.” This operationalizes the 036 plan's observation-removal, divergence-class, nondeterminism, drift, and authority-snapshot tests. A mutant that passes is evidence of an incomplete observable boundary or over-broad normalizer, not an alternate baseline. [SOURCE: specs/system-deep-loop/036-deep-loop-innovation/008-compatibility-shadow-and-rollback-bridge/003-shadow-parity-harness/plan.md:121-135] [SOURCE: specs/system-deep-loop/036-deep-loop-innovation/008-compatibility-shadow-and-rollback-bridge/003-shadow-parity-harness/spec.md:130-150] **When not to use:** do not generate unconstrained random corruptions whose expected class is unknown; keep a small, manifest-bound mutant set with one intended defect per case.

3. **REFINE repo-1: timing parity needs two explicitly different metamorphic contracts.** Graphene's event `at` is informational and ignored by the fold, so transport/wall-clock jitter may be normalized only when the case's semantic clock inputs and event order are fixed. Deadline behavior is different: Graphene computes `waited = now - asked`, fires at `waited >= after_ms`, and records expire or escalate once. Therefore run every timeout/lease case at `boundary-1`, `boundary`, and `boundary+1`, then repeat the sweep to prove idempotency; separately jitter adapter transport timestamps while requiring identical semantic outcomes. The current `out-of-order` fixture does not exercise late observation because both arrival and `observed_at` increase, so its negative mutant should hold arrival order while setting semantic observed times to `[14:00,13:00,15:00]`. [SOURCE: specs/system-deep-loop/037-graph-engineering/context/graphene-main/crates/graphene-core/src/event.rs:18-28] [SOURCE: specs/system-deep-loop/037-graph-engineering/context/graphene-main/crates/graphene-exec/src/lib.rs:844-887] [SOURCE: specs/system-deep-loop/037-graph-engineering/context/graphene-main/crates/graphene-core/tests/golden/out-of-order.jsonl:2-4] **When not to use:** do not normalize declared deadlines, lease expiry, observed time, event sequence, watermark, or timeout multiplicity; those are semantic inputs or outputs rather than transport noise.

4. **CONFIRM and sharpen repo-1: refusal non-mutation is a two-ledger invariant, not “nothing changed.”** Graphene proves a failed store mutation rolls back its domain records and exposes actionable refusal code/suggestion on the wire. In 036, however, every allow and deny appends an immutable non-domain authorization decision. A correct negative fixture must therefore prove `(domain head, domain event count, projection bytes, budget debit, effect outbox) after == before`, while the authorization-audit stream advances by exactly one decision linked to the request digest and carrying the exact deny reason. This resolves the apparent tension between “refused decision leaves no trace” and auditable deny-by-default authorization. [SOURCE: specs/system-deep-loop/037-graph-engineering/context/graphene-main/crates/graphene-store/tests/store.rs:311-322] [SOURCE: specs/system-deep-loop/037-graph-engineering/context/graphene-main/crates/graphene-exec/tests/exec.rs:977-987] [SOURCE: specs/system-deep-loop/036-deep-loop-innovation/004-architecture-coverage-and-transition-contract/003-transition-versioning-and-rollback-policy/transition-versioning-and-rollback-policy.md:123-150] **When not to use:** do not require byte-identical audit-ledger heads across an attempted refusal; the expected audit append is evidence, while any domain/effect/budget mutation is failure.

5. **EXTEND repo-1: cross-adapter authorization parity needs an allowlist of normalizable fields, never a generic trace scrubber.** Compare exact semantic request digest, mode/stream/expected sequence, event type/version/payload digest, actor and capability, policy/evidence identity, authority state/epoch, correlation/causation/idempotency, decision/reason, accepted event envelope, effect/receipt, budget debit, and prefix checkpoint. Normalize only case-manifest-declared transport IDs, raw wall timestamps, token chunks, and model prose, and retain a reversible mapping from adapter-local identifiers to stable scenario roles. This makes adapter parity auditable and turns missing semantic fields into `missing-observation` instead of silently erasing them. [SOURCE: specs/system-deep-loop/036-deep-loop-innovation/004-architecture-coverage-and-transition-contract/003-transition-versioning-and-rollback-policy/transition-versioning-and-rollback-policy.md:127-150] [SOURCE: specs/system-deep-loop/036-deep-loop-innovation/008-compatibility-shadow-and-rollback-bridge/003-shadow-parity-harness/spec.md:90-100] **When not to use:** do not compare provider-specific raw envelopes byte-for-byte, and do not normalize a field merely because two adapters encode it differently; first map it to the same declared semantic role.

6. **CONTRADICT a literal reading of repo-1's “compare normalized authorized ledger traces”: isolated adapters must not have equal whole-run identities.** The 036 parity contract explicitly says complete replay descriptors bind run- and ledger-specific identity; demanding equal final descriptor digests would reject valid isolated executions. Each path must independently verify its own complete descriptor, after which the harness compares registered replay-contract identity, effective-event components, canonical projection components, and declared semantic transcript fields. Whole-descriptor equality is neither necessary nor safe, because weakening isolation to obtain it would permit shared mutable state. [SOURCE: specs/system-deep-loop/036-deep-loop-innovation/008-compatibility-shadow-and-rollback-bridge/003-shadow-parity-harness/spec.md:60-75] [SOURCE: specs/system-deep-loop/036-deep-loop-innovation/008-compatibility-shadow-and-rollback-bridge/003-shadow-parity-harness/spec.md:194-197] **When not to use:** do not use component parity to excuse an unverifiable path-specific descriptor; both attestations must verify before any cross-path semantic comparison is trusted.

## Reflection

The decisive pattern is asymmetric strictness: isolate and independently verify each adapter run, normalize only declared transport noise, then compare every authorized semantic boundary and prove the comparator with a near-miss mutant. Refusal cases intentionally mutate the audit plane while proving zero domain, projection, budget, or effect mutation.

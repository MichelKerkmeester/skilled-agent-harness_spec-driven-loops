## Findings

1. **REFINE repo-1 Decision 5 (research converges on evidence novelty/coverage): make coverage necessary but not sufficient.** Current `contradictionDensity` is only `CONTRADICTS edges / all edges`, while `claimVerificationRate` accepts any non-`unresolved` metadata value and vacuously passes with no claims. A single critical contested premise can therefore coexist with a low density and a high verification rate. `BeliefProjectionV1` must emit hard `convergence_blockers`; contradiction density remains diagnostic telemetry and must not directly authorize STOP. [SOURCE: .opencode/skills/system-deep-loop/runtime/lib/coverage-graph/coverage-graph-signals.ts:581-630] [INFERENCE: a ratio over all edges cannot encode whether the one contradiction lies on a required answer's premise path]

2. **EXTEND repo-1's `KnowledgeAssertionV1` into an orthogonal, replay-derived claim state.** Each belief row should contain `claimId`, computed `truthState: IN|OUT|BOTH|NEITHER`, `supportMode: all|any`, sorted `supportClaimIds`, `premiseUsable`, `stale`, `fidelity`, provenance/source references, evidence references/snapshot, bitemporal validity, contradiction relation IDs, predecessor/successor IDs, terminal successor ID, and last-applied ledger sequence. `premiseUsable` is true only for `IN && !stale`; fidelity is separate and policy may require a minimum fidelity for critical questions. [SOURCE: specs/system-deep-loop/037-graph-engineering/context/graphene-main/crates/graphene-core/src/belief.rs:136-205] [SOURCE: specs/system-deep-loop/037-graph-engineering/context/graphene-main/crates/graphene-core/src/belief.rs:368-443] [SOURCE: specs/system-deep-loop/037-graph-engineering/001-agent-swarms/research/research.md:85-91]

3. **REFINE repo-1's evidence-graph projection boundary: compose, do not replace, the existing relationship projection.** The current projection correctly preserves append-only relationship history, evidence bindings, active contradiction IDs, supersession chains, and a terminal successor, but its scalar status is only `active|contested|superseded`. It has no support DAG, unresolved state, source staleness, or derived cascade. `BeliefProjectionV1` should consume verified claim-lifecycle/support/source-mutation events plus the verified contradiction/supersession stream; it must not infer four-valued truth from coverage edges or mutate relationship history. [SOURCE: .opencode/skills/system-deep-loop/runtime/lib/contradiction-supersession/types.ts:171-209] [SOURCE: .opencode/skills/system-deep-loop/runtime/lib/contradiction-supersession/projection.ts:332-415] [SOURCE: specs/system-deep-loop/036-deep-loop-innovation/010-novelty-claims-continuity-and-projections/002-contradiction-and-supersession-events/spec.md:60-71]

4. **EXTEND repo-1's deterministic reducer rule with a premise-aware fixed point.** After every accepted ledger event, classify each claim's support as `satisfied|unresolved|contested|withdrawn`, derive truth, and iterate in stable claim-ID order until no state changes. Missing declared support yields `NEITHER`; contested support yields `BOTH`; withdrawn/retracted/superseded support yields `OUT`. When a claim ceases to be usable, all dependent answer paths are recalculated in the same projection transaction. This is a disposable reducer output and never an authority event. [SOURCE: specs/system-deep-loop/037-graph-engineering/context/graphene-main/crates/graphene-core/src/fold.rs:23-41] [SOURCE: specs/system-deep-loop/037-graph-engineering/context/graphene-main/crates/graphene-core/src/fold.rs:693-749] [SOURCE: specs/system-deep-loop/037-graph-engineering/context/graphene-main/crates/graphene-core/src/fold.rs:904-934] [SOURCE: specs/system-deep-loop/037-graph-engineering/001-agent-swarms/research/research.md:3-7]

5. **CONFIRM repo-1's typed terminal verdicts, with a concrete P1 STOP rule.** A required question counts as answered only when its selected answer resolves through the terminal supersession chain to at least one `premiseUsable` claim whose configured support condition is satisfied. STOP is blocked when a required answer path is `BOTH`, `NEITHER`, stale, superseded without a usable terminal successor, or below its explicit minimum-fidelity policy. Non-critical contested claims stay visible but do not block. The projection should expose sorted `answeredQuestionIds`, `blockedQuestionIds`, typed blocker reasons, `usableRequiredAnswerRate`, and a fingerprint bound to ledger head/reducer version; the mode's novelty/coverage thresholds run only after this guard passes. ## Design decision delta

Adopt `BeliefProjectionV1` as a disposable 036-ledger-derived projection composed from claim lifecycle/support/source events and the existing verified contradiction/supersession projection. Its canonical claim state is orthogonal: four-valued truth, staleness, fidelity, validity, support dependencies, relationship history, and terminal supersession are separate fields. Its deterministic reducer settles support to a fixed point after each event. Research convergence remains mode-specific, but STOP now has a first-stage belief eligibility guard; existing coverage, novelty, diversity, and evidence-depth thresholds are evaluated only after all required answer paths are usable. The graph may report or block a proposed STOP, but 036 remains the only transition authority. [SOURCE: specs/system-deep-loop/037-graph-engineering/001-agent-swarms/research/research.md:3-7] [SOURCE: specs/system-deep-loop/037-graph-engineering/001-agent-swarms/research/research.md:61-67]

## When not to use

- Do not require a belief projection for a single deterministic action with no evidence claims or convergence decision; a direct harness action remains simpler. [SOURCE: specs/system-deep-loop/037-graph-engineering/001-agent-swarms/research/research.md:99-108]
- Do not let `BeliefProjectionV1` admit transitions, rewrite claims, or emit contradiction/supersession authority events; it is a rebuildable read model over verified 036 history. [SOURCE: specs/system-deep-loop/037-graph-engineering/001-agent-swarms/research/research.md:3-7]
- Do not block on every contested claim. Only typed required-answer/criticality policy creates a STOP obligation; background disagreement remains visible evidence. [INFERENCE: treating every contradiction as critical would turn healthy divergent research into non-termination]
- Do not impose `Confirmed` fidelity globally: Graphene's premise eligibility depends on truth plus staleness, while fidelity is a separate grounding dimension. Require a fidelity floor only through explicit per-question policy. [SOURCE: specs/system-deep-loop/037-graph-engineering/context/graphene-main/crates/graphene-core/src/belief.rs:85-133] [SOURCE: specs/system-deep-loop/037-graph-engineering/context/graphene-main/crates/graphene-core/src/belief.rs:420-443]

## Ruled out / failed approaches

- **Use contradiction density as the STOP guard.** Ruled out because it measures edge prevalence, not whether contradiction reaches a required answer path. [SOURCE: .opencode/skills/system-deep-loop/runtime/lib/coverage-graph/coverage-graph-signals.ts:613-630]
- **Map `active|contested|superseded` directly to `IN|BOTH|OUT`.** Ruled out because the current relationship projection has no representation for unresolved support (`NEITHER`), source staleness, or dependent cascades. [SOURCE: .opencode/skills/system-deep-loop/runtime/lib/contradiction-supersession/types.ts:19-22] [SOURCE: .opencode/skills/system-deep-loop/runtime/lib/contradiction-supersession/types.ts:171-209]
- **Count repeated same-source evidence as corroboration.** Ruled out because Graphene explicitly treats the same source twice as one witness and refuses the fidelity upgrade. [SOURCE: specs/system-deep-loop/037-graph-engineering/context/graphene-main/crates/graphene-core/src/belief.rs:426-443]
- **Copy Graphene's supersession mutation literally.** Ruled out because current 036 relations are append-only assertions/withdrawals and preserve immutable relationship history; the projection may close effective validity but cannot mutate the prior claim/event. [SOURCE: specs/system-deep-loop/037-graph-engineering/context/graphene-main/crates/graphene-core/src/fold.rs:561-579] [SOURCE: specs/system-deep-loop/036-deep-loop-innovation/010-novelty-claims-continuity-and-projections/002-contradiction-and-supersession-events/spec.md:60-66]

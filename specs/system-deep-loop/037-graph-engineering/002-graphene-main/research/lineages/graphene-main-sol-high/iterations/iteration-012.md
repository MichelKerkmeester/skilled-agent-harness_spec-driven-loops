# Iteration 12: P7 Human Gates over Live Belief Context

## Focus

This iteration resolves P7: compose Graphene's cold-answerable human question, options, consequence map, explicit timeout, and four-valued beliefs with repo 1's durable fenced gate. The focus is the boundary between an immutable explanation snapshot and the live re-evaluation required before a human decision may select a consequence edge.

## Findings

1. **EXTEND repo 1's `HumanGateOpenedV1` with a typed `BeliefContextSnapshotV1`, not a list of belief IDs.** The opened gate records the exact question; stable option IDs plus human labels; the complete option-to-consequence-edge map; the sorted load-bearing belief IDs; each belief's terminal supersession target, `IN|OUT|BOTH|NEITHER`, domain `stale` flag, fidelity, support mode, support/contradiction relation IDs, evidence references and content hash; and explicit missing/redacted markers. This makes the gate answerable cold without treating a summary as proof. Graphene already declares the question, options, belief IDs, consequences, and mandatory timeout, while its premise rule establishes that only `IN && !stale` is usable; the extension materializes those states and their derivation at opening time. [SOURCE: specs/system-deep-loop/037-graph-engineering/context/graphene-main/crates/graphene-core/src/node.rs:228-254] [SOURCE: specs/system-deep-loop/037-graph-engineering/context/graphene-main/crates/graphene-core/src/belief.rs:136-184] [SOURCE: specs/system-deep-loop/037-graph-engineering/context/graphene-main/crates/graphene-core/src/belief.rs:368-424] **When not to use:** do not embed unrestricted belief contents, secrets, or an unbounded graph slice; store policy-filtered display material and durable evidence pointers, with digests over the complete canonical context.

2. **REFINE repo 1: distinguish belief truth from snapshot freshness.** `truthState` and the belief's `stale` bit are domain facts produced by the fold; `contextSnapshotFresh` is a gate-projection fact meaning the current canonical context digest still equals the digest shown to the decision maker. A belief may remain `IN && !stale` while its support lineage, terminal successor, fidelity, evidence, or surrounding consequence topology changed, so snapshot freshness cannot be inferred from truth alone. Conversely, a displayed `BOTH` is not a stale display if the current live projection remains `BOTH`. [SOURCE: specs/system-deep-loop/037-graph-engineering/context/graphene-main/crates/graphene-core/src/belief.rs:167-183] [SOURCE: specs/system-deep-loop/037-graph-engineering/context/graphene-main/crates/graphene-core/src/belief.rs:376-417] [INFERENCE: equality of current canonical context and opened canonical context requires a separate digest because no individual belief field captures topology/evidence/support-set drift] **When not to use:** do not collapse `BOTH`, `NEITHER`, domain staleness, snapshot drift, and projection unavailability into one warning badge or one retryable boolean; they imply different human interpretations and recovery actions.

3. **EXTEND the opened gate with four independently verified anchors.** `HumanGateContextV1` binds `(gateId, gateVersion, suspendedNodeId, graphTopologyDigest, consequenceMapDigest, beliefContextDigest, evidenceBundleDigest, domainLedgerHead, authorizationAuditHead, graphReducerContractDigest, authorityEpoch, policyId/version, fenceToken, openedAt, expiresAt)`. The topology anchor proves what each option would release; the belief/evidence anchors prove what the human saw and why; the two heads locate admitted domain history and authorization history without inventing a cross-ledger sequence; the reducer contract proves how the snapshot was derived. Repo 1 already binds topology/evidence/version/fence, and the live gateway independently rejects stale domain head or authority epoch. [SOURCE: specs/system-deep-loop/037-graph-engineering/001-agent-swarms/research/lineages/agent-swarms-sol-high/iterations/iteration-009.md:9-13] [SOURCE: .opencode/skills/system-deep-loop/runtime/lib/authorized-ledger/transition-authorization-gateway.ts:720-745] [SOURCE: .opencode/skills/system-deep-loop/runtime/lib/authorized-ledger/transition-authorization-gateway.ts:840-875] **When not to use:** do not treat a snapshot digest, browser payload, projection checkpoint, or observed head as authority; each is evidence addressed by the current gate and must be verified against the selected authoritative stores.

4. **REFINE repo 1's decision command into snapshot-bound intent plus mandatory live re-evaluation.** `HumanGateDecisionCommandV1` carries the exact gate/version/fence, decision ID and idempotency digest, authenticated principal/capability, option ID, observed context/topology/evidence/head digests, optional structured rationale/input digest, and expected expiry. In one authoritative decision boundary the gateway re-resolves the current gate, current heads and epoch, current principal assignment, current topology/consequence map, and a newly folded belief-context digest. It accepts only if every load-bearing anchor still matches and the chosen option remains declared. An allow appends one decision and one edge-selection intent; a denial appends only authorization audit evidence and leaves gate/domain/effect state unchanged. [SOURCE: .opencode/skills/system-deep-loop/runtime/lib/authorized-ledger/transition-authorization-gateway.ts:730-745] [SOURCE: .opencode/skills/system-deep-loop/runtime/lib/authorized-ledger/transition-authorization-gateway.ts:878-895] [SOURCE: specs/system-deep-loop/036-deep-loop-innovation/007-shared-evidence-and-control-services/006-locks-and-fencing/spec.md:91-102] [INFERENCE: the live belief digest must be recomputed inside the authoritative decision because a preflight read can race a belief, evidence, topology, assignment, or fence change] **When not to use:** do not let a client-side refresh, a read-then-write check, or an unchanged option label validate a decision; the protected mutation must compare current anchors atomically.

5. **REFINE invalidation into an append-only state transition and reopen into a new decision epoch.** A committed change to any load-bearing belief, terminal successor, evidence bundle, consequence edge, topology, assignment, policy, authority epoch, or fence makes outstanding commands invalid. The reducer may expose this immediately as `contextSnapshotFresh=false`, but authority records `HumanGateInvalidatedV1(reason, oldContextDigest, currentContextDigest, invalidatingEventIds)` before any further decision. Recovery is either explicit cancellation or `HumanGateReopenedV1` with incremented gate version, a new context snapshot and digest set, and a new or higher current fence; it never edits the old ask in place. Duplicate decision IDs replay their original result, while a different decision against a decided, invalidated, expired, or superseded version is denied. [SOURCE: specs/system-deep-loop/037-graph-engineering/001-agent-swarms/research/lineages/agent-swarms-sol-high/iterations/iteration-009.md:10-13] [SOURCE: specs/system-deep-loop/036-deep-loop-innovation/007-shared-evidence-and-control-services/006-locks-and-fencing/spec.md:67-75] [INFERENCE: append-only invalidation preserves what was shown, why it ceased to be actionable, and which replacement context the new decision addressed] **When not to use:** do not automatically reopen for display-only formatting, transport timestamps, or non-load-bearing beliefs; the gate contract must name the semantic dependency set so irrelevant projection churn does not create denial-of-service.

6. **CONFIRM Graphene's consequence semantics but move selection behind authorization.** Graphene correctly makes the chosen option unblock only its declared dependents and skips other dependents, preventing a negative answer from leaving an irreversible node claimable. For 036, this fold behavior is a disposable projection of an authorized `GateEdgeSelectedV1`; the human decision itself is not a capability to dispatch, mutate, or perform an effect. The selected edge still must pass current node/claim state, write-set and resource-fence checks, and any separate effect-authorization/receipt boundary. [SOURCE: specs/system-deep-loop/037-graph-engineering/context/graphene-main/crates/graphene-core/src/fold.rs:439-486] [SOURCE: specs/system-deep-loop/036-deep-loop-innovation/007-shared-evidence-and-control-services/006-locks-and-fencing/spec.md:69-75] [INFERENCE: approving a consequence expresses human intent but cannot prove that downstream mutable resources, claims, budgets, or effect policy remain current] **When not to use:** do not encode an option as a raw node list that bypasses edge predicates or as direct permission to send, publish, delete, grant, spend, or write.

7. **REFINE timeout as a current-context transition, never a synthetic human answer.** Preserve Graphene's explicit `wait|expire|escalate` policy and boundary rule `waited >= after_ms`, but record a typed `HumanGateTimedOutV1` (or escalation event) carrying the gate/version, deadline, observed time, current context/topology/evidence/head digests, and selected timeout edge. `wait` makes no decision; `escalate` keeps the gate pending and changes routing through a new authorized event; `expire/cancel` closes it without approval; a policy-defined retry or manual hold is a new authorized transition. The timeout runner must revalidate the current fence, version, heads, policy, and context just like a human command, so a stale scheduler cannot close a reopened gate. [SOURCE: specs/system-deep-loop/037-graph-engineering/context/graphene-main/crates/graphene-exec/src/lib.rs:828-890] [SOURCE: specs/system-deep-loop/037-graph-engineering/context/graphene-main/crates/graphene-core/src/node.rs:240-254] [SOURCE: specs/system-deep-loop/036-deep-loop-innovation/007-shared-evidence-and-control-services/006-locks-and-fencing/spec.md:94-102] **When not to use:** do not encode silence as approval, reuse a human option as an implicit timeout choice, or let a daemon's local timer mutate the gate without the same durable authorization and fence checks.

8. **CONTRADICT any claim that Graphene's current golden human fixtures prove live-context gate safety.** The fold and timeout sweep prove explicit consequence selection and non-approval on silence, but the gate event/decision shapes carry no gate version, principal assignment, belief/evidence/topology/head digest, authority epoch, idempotency identity, or mutation fence. The expected snapshots therefore demonstrate deterministic local fold behavior, not resistance to belief drift, stale tabs, reassignment, reopen, successor timeout workers, or downstream effect races. P7 parity needs prefix checkpoints for open, belief/evidence mutation, invalidation, stale decision denial, reopen, current decision, timeout boundary-1/boundary/boundary+1, and authorized edge selection, each paired with a plausible stale-context mutant. [SOURCE: specs/system-deep-loop/037-graph-engineering/context/graphene-main/crates/graphene-core/tests/golden/human-gate.jsonl:1-36] [SOURCE: specs/system-deep-loop/037-graph-engineering/context/graphene-main/crates/graphene-core/tests/golden/human-timeout.jsonl:31-34] [SOURCE: specs/system-deep-loop/037-graph-engineering/context/graphene-main/crates/graphene-core/tests/golden.rs:75-100] **When not to use:** do not accept final-state equality or fixture naming as proof of decision-time freshness; assert the earliest mismatch and zero domain/effect mutation on every stale-context refusal.

## Ruled Out

- **A gate context containing only belief IDs or rendered prose.** It cannot prove the exact truth, support, staleness, evidence, and consequence topology shown to the decision maker. [SOURCE: specs/system-deep-loop/037-graph-engineering/context/graphene-main/crates/graphene-core/src/node.rs:228-254]
- **Treating the opening snapshot as permanently valid authority.** Beliefs, evidence, topology, policy, assignment, authority epoch, heads, and fences can change before decision delivery. [SOURCE: .opencode/skills/system-deep-loop/runtime/lib/authorized-ledger/transition-authorization-gateway.ts:720-745]
- **Mutating the pending gate in place after context drift.** That destroys the audit distinction between the question answered and the replacement question. [INFERENCE: replay cannot reconstruct what an old decision observed if its gate row is overwritten]
- **Automatically reopening on every projection change.** Only named semantic dependencies should invalidate; cosmetic and non-load-bearing changes must not create an availability attack. [INFERENCE: an unbounded dependency set lets unrelated graph churn indefinitely prevent decision]
- **Letting approval directly execute the consequence.** Human intent does not prove current claim, resource fence, budget, effect policy, or idempotency state. [SOURCE: specs/system-deep-loop/036-deep-loop-innovation/007-shared-evidence-and-control-services/006-locks-and-fencing/spec.md:91-102]
- **Timeout as approval or as a daemon-local write.** Silence has no human principal, and a stale timer can race reopen/takeover. [SOURCE: specs/system-deep-loop/037-graph-engineering/context/graphene-main/crates/graphene-exec/src/lib.rs:828-890]

## Reflection

The productive move was to separate three concepts that are easy to conflate: the belief's live domain truth, whether the displayed context still matches that truth and its derivation, and whether the decision is authorized to select a consequence now. Graphene establishes the first and useful local consequence/timeout folds; repo 1 and 036 establish the durable gate and mutation boundary. No implementation was attempted. The current Graphene fixtures were useful as positive local semantics but were ruled out as proof of stale-context safety.

## Findings

The reducer orders exclusively by authoritative ledger sequence; `occurred_at` and `recorded_at` remain query metadata. Its dispatch key is the exact `event_type@effective_event_version@graph_reducer_version`; every event in the covered range must resolve either to a typed state transition or an explicitly registered deterministic no-op so projection-relevance filtering cannot silently change the replay input. The graph-specific view retains `event_id`, ledger/stream sequence, stream ID, correlation/causation IDs, authority epoch, authorization decision reference, stored/effective event hashes, and payload-derived graph identities. 3. **CONFIRM repo-1's deterministic-reducer decision and make rebuild fail-closed and atomic.** A full rebuild reads the verified domain stream through one immutable head, verifies its linked authorization audit, resolves every exact reducer/no-op definition, folds from canonical empty state, settles derived graph/belief state after each event, and repeats reduction to require byte-identical canonical output. It publishes the new projection only after the projection digest and replay fingerprint match; partial state is never served. 4. **EXTEND repo-1 Decision 4 with sequence-addressed point-in-time semantics.** `projectionAt(S)` means the fold of the inclusive verified ledger range from genesis through authoritative sequence `S`, identified by ledger ID, terminal record hash, graph reducer/projection versions, and a closed-range replay fingerprint. It may accelerate from the newest fully verified checkpoint `C <= S` and fold the exact suffix `C+1..S`; the result must equal a genesis replay. Event-time queries are a second, explicit bitemporal filter over the sequence-derived projection and never redefine replay order. This preserves the ability to ask both what the system knew at sequence `S` and what claims were valid at a domain time. 5. **CONTRADICT applying Graphene's physical compaction rule to the 036 authority or authorization-audit ledgers.** Graphene deletes events when the current fold remains equivalent, but equal present state does not preserve prior projections, denial/unapplied-allow evidence, authorization provenance, causal history, or the stored-sequence component of a replay fingerprint. The P2 decision is therefore **no destructive compaction of committed 036 domain or audit records**. Safe compaction is limited to disposable projections, indexes, materialized caches, redundant checkpoints, and sealed export packaging that preserves every canonical frame byte plus sequence/hash/authorization linkage in verifiable content-addressed storage. 6. **REFINE repo-1's single-ledger wording into a domain-plus-authorization replay boundary without granting either projection authority.** A trusted graph projection is derived from the verified domain stream, but its replay proof also binds the authorization-audit head and confirms that each applied event has an allow decision, denied requests are absent from domain history, and unapplied allows remain auditable. ## P2 contract decision

Adopt `GraphProjectionReducerV1` as an exact-version, read-only fold over the fully verified 036 domain stream, with a registered typed transition or deterministic no-op for every event in its closed range. `GraphCheckpointV1` is a discardable exact-prefix accelerator bound to the domain head, authorization audit, replay contract, topology, canonical projection, and replay fingerprint. Full rebuild is fail-closed, repeat-reduced, and atomically published. Point-in-time state is an inclusive ledger-sequence projection, optionally accelerated by a verified earlier checkpoint. No committed domain or authorization-audit event may be physically compacted; only projections, indexes, caches, redundant checkpoints, or byte-preserving sealed packaging are compactable.

## How to read it

- **Value and Risk** are H, M or L, with M-H in between.
- **Effort** is S (under a day), M (one to three days) or L (longer).
- **Check** applies to the top ten. The conductor re-read the cited code at `b8003a7df42`:
  - **CONFIRMED** means the code at those lines shows the mechanism, and where noted the run
    showed the effect live.
  - **PLAUSIBLE** means the code is consistent with the claim, but the effect was traced or
    measured by a lineage, not reproduced by the conductor.
  - Items 11 and below carry the lineages' evidence, unchecked by the conductor unless tagged
    `conductor`.
- **Source** tags: `[opus]`, `[deepseek]`, `[conductor]`.
- **Paths are shortened.** A `runtime/` path is under `.skilled/skills/system-deep-loop/`, and
  the two YAMLs are under `.skilled/commands/deep/assets/`.

## How to read it

- **Value and Risk** are H, M or L, with M-H in between.
- **Effort** is S (under a day), M (one to three days) or L (longer).
- **Check** applies to the top ten. The conductor re-read the cited code at `d4420e9510d`:
  - **CONFIRMED** means the code at those lines shows the mechanism, and where noted the run showed
    the effect live.
  - **PLAUSIBLE** means the code is consistent with the claim, but the effect was traced, not
    reproduced.
  - Items 11 and below carry the lineages' evidence, unchecked by the conductor.
- **Rec** is do, defer or drop.
- **Paths are shortened.** A `runtime/` path is under `.skilled/skills/system-deep-loop/`, and the
  two YAMLs are under `.skilled/commands/deep/assets/`.

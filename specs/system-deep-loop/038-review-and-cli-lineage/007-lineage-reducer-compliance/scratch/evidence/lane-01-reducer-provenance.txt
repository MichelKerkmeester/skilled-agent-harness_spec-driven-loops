1. DIAGNOSIS (REQ-002) — why proof-a skipped the reducer
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Mechanism, in one sentence: the reducer runs only as a model-executed step of the
detached lineage workflow, and the runner's "fulfilled" verdict is decided from
artifacts that do not include the reducer's projections, so a lineage that never
invoked it still passes completion.

Step that lets it be skipped, path:line:
  1. .skilled/commands/deep/assets/deep-research-auto.yaml:1841  step_reduce_state
     (command at :1843 runs deep-research/scripts/reduce-state.cjs). It is a step
     in the phase_loop list; the detached CLI leaf orchestrates the YAML itself
     (fanout-run.cjs:1602-1614 hands it "Run phase_init, phase_main_loop ... and
     phase_synthesis"), and nothing machine-verifies that this step ran.
  2. .skilled/skills/system-deep-loop/runtime/scripts/fanout-run.cjs:1043
     findMaxIterationsPolicyViolation. Without a recognised synthesis event it
     falls back at :1073-:1075 to completionFromArtifacts (:1015), which validates
     only research.md (:1023-:1025) and the iteration count (:1027-:1032). The one
     registry-shaped check on this path, hasLineageRegisteredFindings (:950),
     proves only that keyFindings is non-empty, never who wrote it.

Observed on the saved evidence (all paths under
006-research-lineage-reducer-path/research/lineages/):
  - proof-a/deep-research-state.jsonl: iteration row, `phase_main_loop` complete
    event, `synthesis_complete` with stopReason maxIterationsReached.
  - proof-a/research.md exists; 1/1 iteration files on disk -> every check in
    findMaxIterationsPolicyViolation returns null -> lineage reported fulfilled.
  - proof-a/deep-research-dashboard.md: hand-written header, no reducer stamp.
  - proof-a/findings-registry.json: 8 hand keys (schemaVersion, topic, generatedAt,
    iterationsCompleted, openQuestions, resolvedQuestions, keyFindings, metrics),
    none of the reducer's machine-owned fields.
  - proof-b/deep-research-dashboard.md: `description: Auto-generated reducer view
    over the research packet.` (the reducer's own stamp).
  - proof-b/findings-registry.json: reducer-owned shape (sessionId, lineageMode,
    terminalStop, keyQuestions, rejectedPatternIndex, idea backlog, graph rollup,
    corruption/inbox/strategy warnings).

Conclusion: the skip is leaf non-compliance with an unenforced workflow step. The
workflow cannot be fixed by the runner without duplicating the reducer call, so the
gate belongs at the merge, where the operator placed it.

7. CHANGE SUMMARY AND AMENDMENT
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Merge-time check in fanout-merge.cjs main(), for research lineages that carry an
on-disk registry:
  - dashboard present WITHOUT the reducer stamp -> lineage REFUSED (registry
    excluded from the merge, `refused_reducer_provenance` in the payload, and a
    `lineage_reducer_provenance_missing` error line naming the lineage and the
    missing stamp);
  - dashboard absent -> provenance unconfirmed, lineage MERGED with a
    `lineage_reducer_provenance_unverified` warning and
    `reducer_provenance_warnings` in the payload.

Amendment to the "refuse" wording: refusal engages on positive evidence (a
dashboard that proves the projections were hand-written, the proof-a shape).
Refusing dashboard-less registries as well would fail the frozen success criterion
SC-002 ("existing deep-research fan-out tests still pass"): the existing merge
suite's fixtures are hand-built registries with no dashboards, and editing those
tests is outside this lane's allowlist. They are therefore flagged, not refused.

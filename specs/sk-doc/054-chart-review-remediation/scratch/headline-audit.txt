Headline audit: all twenty-seven headlines, checked arithmetically against their own data block.

Twenty-one chart forms under assets/templates and six deliveries under assets/examples. Every
number below was computed from the CHART_DATA block of the file it describes. Eight headlines were
rewritten. Nineteen stand, four of them after a reading that could have gone either way.

REWRITTEN (8)
-------------
bar-rows          "longer than the three stages after it combined"
                  18 vs 9 + 6 + 5 = 20. FALSE by 2 days.
                  -> "the two stages after it combined": 18 vs 9 + 6 = 15. True by 3.

calendar-grid     "the whole of high summer runs at half pace"
                  Weekday mean across the summer weeks (27-35) = 11.04. Year weekday mean = 16.91.
                  11.04 / 16.91 = 65.3%, not 50%. Against the rest of the year (18.14) it is 60.9%.
                  Only weeks 29-33, five of the nine, reach 53.8% of the rest of the year.
                  -> "run at two thirds of the year's pace": 65.3%.
                  The other clause holds: weekend mean 3.74 against weekday 16.91, 22%.

candlestick       "gave back everything the autumn rally had gained"
                  W1 open 100, W10 close 140, W14 close 102. Two of the forty points remain, so
                  under the whole-series reading it is false; under a later rally start it is true
                  and then some. The data carries no dates to settle which.
                  -> "took back thirty-eight of the forty points the rally had added": 140 - 102 = 38,
                     140 - 100 = 40. Exact under either reading.

daily-range       "the ceiling kept climbing"
                  Highs 340 366 352 401 438 402 421 507 548 611 664 702 781 910. Three of the
                  thirteen steps are down (days 3, 6 and 7), so "kept climbing" is falsifiable
                  beside a clause ("the floor held all fortnight") that is strictly true.
                  -> "the ceiling nearly tripled": 910 / 340 = 2.68. Lows stay in 112-126.

stacked-area      "the total stayed inside a fifteen percent band"
                  Period totals run 92 to 106. The band is 14 points, which is 15.22% of the
                  minimum, 14.2% of the mean and 13.2% of the maximum. The claim flips on the
                  denominator, and the largest reading is the one that breaks it.
                  -> "a fifteen point band": 14 points, absolute, true under every reading.
                     The form's own desc already said "between 92 and 106".

treemap           "and no team has claimed it"
                  The data block holds label and value per leaf and no owner field anywhere, so
                  the claim is unsupported rather than false. "Nearly half" is sound: 243 / 543 = 44.8%.
                  -> "its archive line alone costs more than all of Network": 128 vs 63 + 22 = 85.

unit-grid         "two accounts out of forty"
                  The block holds four labelled tiers: two largest 50, next eight 27, everyone
                  else 16, unassigned 7. Nothing in it counts forty accounts. "Half" is sound: 50 of 100.
                  -> "on the two largest accounts", which is the label the block carries.

calls-by-day-     "the quietest weekday mid-morning still carries three times the busiest weekend hour"
and-hour          Busiest weekend slot = 66 (Saturday 14). Quietest weekday 10-slot = 159, which is
                  2.41x. Quietest weekday 08-slot = 208, which is 3.15x. The claim is true only if
                  "mid-morning" means the 08 slot, and 10:00-12:00 is the more natural reading.
                  -> "runs below every weekday slot between eight and six": weekend max 66 against
                     the weekday daytime minimum of 126 (Friday 12).

STANDS AFTER A CLOSE READING (4)
--------------------------------
pick-times-       "spreads almost twice as wide": range 118 vs 65 = 1.82x, which is what the dot
by-depot          cloud's width shows and what the form draws. IQR is 3.42x and sd 2.47x, so the
                  claim understates two measures and matches the one the mark encodes. Kept, and
                  consistent with distribution-strip, which reads spread the same way.
                  Medians 70.5 and 70.0, so "matches on the median" holds.

independent-      "almost none has recovery codes" for 11%. Loose as a quantifier and printed
percentages       beside its own number in the chart. Kept: the argument is the gap against 94%,
                  and 11% is on the bar.

stacked-bars      "the market we entered last year". The block labels it "New market" and carries
                  no date. Read as identifying a labelled column rather than as a second claim.
                  The mix claim is sound: platform runs 61-66% in three markets and 26% in the fourth.

scatter           "bigger squads close more per person" is a trend, not a monotone: 9 people at 24
                  beats 10 at 22. Kept as a trend claim, which is what a scatter draws. The outlier
                  clause is exact: squad 16 at 14 people and 9 tickets.

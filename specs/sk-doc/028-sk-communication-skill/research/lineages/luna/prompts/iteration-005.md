# Iteration 5 Research Prompt

## Focus

Assess the user value, adoption boundary, and operational cost of communication projection compared with deterministic formatting and exact-original output.

## Questions

1. Which user-facing CLI or agent messages plausibly benefit from plain-English projection, and which are already served by deterministic formatting or the original?
2. How do privacy classes, runtime ownership, presentation tiers, latency, provider evidence, evaluation requirements, and rollback affect whether the capability earns its complexity?
3. What concrete rollout rule and use-case decision matrix follow from the package and skill contracts?

## Required evidence

- Cite exact source lines for the skill's intended value and exclusions, privacy/egress modes, support and doctor freshness, runtime/client tiers, evaluation and release gates, and rollback behavior.
- Separate confirmed package constraints from inferred value judgments; do not treat the supplied DeepSeek smoke as a measured benchmark.
- Compare model projection with deterministic formatting on readability scope, privacy surface, operational cost, and fallback safety.
- Recommend a narrow first adoption boundary and identify the evidence needed before claiming broad value.

## Boundaries

Do not modify package or skill sources. Preserve canonical bytes, local restoration, privacy-before-ranking, content-free telemetry, safe-native/full-projection separation, exact-original fallback, and the max-iterations stop policy. Convergence is telemetry only; this is the fifth and final research iteration before synthesis.

# Research angles opened by sk-doc/019

The 019 packet established a much firmer routing substrate: canonical `(workflowMode, leafResourceId)` identity, compiled-policy serving for seven eligible hubs, typed non-route outcomes, and a reversible fleet cutover. It also showed that several apparently structural problems were actually measurement problems: advisor confidence is not probability, typed-leaf recall is not yet the same as successful leaf use, and shadow fixtures do not establish natural-traffic behavior. The largest remaining frontiers are therefore empirical calibration, causal routing observability, recovery economics, and deciding whether the optional adaptive machinery earns its cost. The current fleet reinforces that split: seven multi-mode hubs use compiled routing, while five singleton packages have structurally valid but operationally different routing questions.

Paths below are repository-relative. In shorthand citations, `001-research/...`, `015-router-unification-program/...`, and `016-documentation-quality-program/...` resolve under `.opencode/specs/sk-doc/019-skill-routing-refactor/`. “Extension” means a bounded continuation of an existing 019 research child; “new territory” means 019 exposed the evidence gap but did not dedicate a research child to answering it.

## Extends an existing 019 research child

### 1. Falsify the Threshold–Recovery–Provenance decomposition across the fleet

- **Question.** Are Threshold, Recovery, and Provenance genuinely orthogonal across the routing archetypes now present in all 12 skills? Does authority need to be a fourth coordinate, and can ordered/surface bundles be represented by a single recovery value without losing role or order?
- **Skills involved.** All 12 packages supply the needed counterexamples: multi-mode hubs, transport/judgment compositions, surface bundles, and singleton-mode degeneracies. `system-deep-loop` supplies the research/benchmark machinery; `system-skill-advisor` supplies the fleet-level selector.
- **What 019 established vs left open.** The OOB GLM lineage introduced `(T,R,P)` as a useful explanatory vocabulary, not a migration recommendation (`.opencode/specs/sk-doc/019-skill-routing-refactor/001-research/008-oob-glm-parallel-research/research/research.md` §5–§10). The dedicated child asks the four decisive questions in `.opencode/specs/sk-doc/019-skill-routing-refactor/001-research/009-oob-idea-deep-dives/008-trp-decomposition/spec.md` §7, while the parent `009-oob-idea-deep-dives/spec.md` “PHASE DOCUMENTATION MAP” explicitly records “5-iteration dive NOT run.” This is the cleanest unexecuted research commitment in 019.
- **Research-loop output.** A falsification matrix over at least six archetypes; a minimal coordinate model; counterexamples for bundles, authority, and N=1; and a decision memo choosing “keep TRP,” “add authority,” or “retain TRP only as explanatory vocabulary.”
- **Scope, effort, payoff.** Five to seven iterations, multi-model with one model constructing and another attacking the decomposition. High payoff: it decides whether a fleet-wide conceptual model is sound before more contracts are designed around it.

### 2. Calibrate advisor confidence and the selective controller on operational evidence

- **Question.** Which calibration family, risk slices, sample floors, thresholds, and expiry rules make `estimatedError` and selective auto-routing reliable on privacy-reviewed natural requests? Does the current `0.82` task-intent floor survive that test?
- **Skills involved.** `system-skill-advisor` owns the five-lane RRF ranking and public confidence policy; `system-deep-loop` owns the evaluation loop; `system-spec-kit` owns shared RRF and corpus/governance infrastructure; every routed hub supplies a distinct risk slice.
- **What 019 established vs left open.** The advisor study established that RRF uses `k=8`, zero overlap bonuses, and graduated query-local min-max normalization, while public confidence is a separate quantized policy function. It found `0.82` floor saturation at 31% of the full corpus and 48% of the ambiguity slice, with plateau correctness only 58–65% (`001-research/002-skill-advisor-routing-research/research/research.md` §5). Its §6 limits the result to a frozen filesystem projection, and §12 leaves reliability bins open. The later calibration program implemented the contract but not the evidence: the holdout corpus sampled no live traffic and missed operational sample floors; no live thresholds were fit; the selective controller has no per-hub canary traffic (`015-router-unification-program/008-calibration/001-holdout-corpus/implementation-summary.md`, `002-rank-vs-calibrated-contract/implementation-summary.md`, and `003-selective-controller/implementation-summary.md`, each “Known Limitations”).
- **Research-loop output.** A versioned sampling and privacy protocol; preregistered Brier/ECE/selective-risk/coverage metrics; reliability curves by hub and effect class; threshold and expiry candidates with uncertainty intervals; and a decision table for auto-route versus clarify/defer.
- **Scope, effort, payoff.** Eight to ten iterations, multi-model because labeling and calibration assumptions need independent challenge. Highest operational payoff: the advisor is the front door, and today its confidence should not be read as probability.

### 3. Establish causal, ordered leaf-use telemetry

- **Question.** What is the smallest cross-runtime event contract that can prove a routed leaf was successfully opened, in what order, under which router decision, and whether the read affected the final action?
- **Skills involved.** `sk-code` is the immediate evidence gap; `system-deep-loop` owns benchmark collection; `sk-doc` owns typed leaf identity; `cli-external-orchestration` exercises subprocess boundaries where provenance is easiest to lose.
- **What 019 established vs left open.** The sk-code study proved that `raw.observedReads` is a deduplicated path extraction from tool inputs, not evidence of order, success, canonical identity, or causal router origin (`001-research/011-sk-code-routing-research/research/research.md` §6). Its §12 asks whether Codex can expose exact-read provenance or actual-read scoring must remain OpenCode-only.
- **Research-loop output.** A runtime-neutral event schema; capability matrix for Codex/OpenCode/CLI execution; a minimal prototype trace on representative routes; definitions for “selected,” “opened,” “successful,” and “used”; and a scored recommendation for native, wrapper, or unavailable telemetry per runtime.
- **Scope, effort, payoff.** Five to seven iterations. One primary model can trace and prototype the contract, followed by an independent model audit of causal claims. High payoff: it turns “leaf recall” from a declaration/proxy into an observable outcome and unlocks angle 4.

### 4. Test two-tier required/supplemental leaf selection

- **Question.** Does a two-tier selector—required leaves first, supplemental leaves only when evidence crosses a specificity threshold—improve sealed-holdout Recall@k and successful-read recall without exceeding a preregistered route budget?
- **Skills involved.** `sk-code` supplies the diagnosed monolithic maps; `sk-prompt` supplies lifecycle-versus-selection leaves; `sk-doc` supplies manifest semantics; `system-deep-loop` supplies sealed evaluation.
- **What 019 established vs left open.** The sk-code child established that monolithic map unioning expands candidate sets, D3 can over-penalize minimum-set gold, and two-tier selection is the highest-value untested router candidate (`001-research/011-sk-code-routing-research/research/research.md` §5). Its §12 explicitly asks for preregistered Recall@k/route-budget thresholds and whether the candidate improves both sealed holdout and ordered successful-read recall. No candidate was executed (§6).
- **Research-loop output.** A same-revision baseline; independent train/holdout split; two or three selector variants; preregistered recall, precision, budget, and successful-use metrics; error analysis by surface and leaf class; and a go/no-go design recommendation.
- **Scope, effort, payoff.** Six to eight iterations, multi-model so gold authoring is separate from evaluation. High payoff: this is the most concrete path beyond sk-code’s current leaf-recall ceiling.

### 5. Measure bounded handoff against bounded clarification

- **Question.** For uncertain or wrong initial routes, when does one typed handoff outperform one clarification turn in latency, tokens, operator effort, recovery yield, and failure amplification?
- **Skills involved.** `mcp-tooling` and `sk-design` supply transport-plus-judgment cases; `cli-external-orchestration` supplies executor handoffs; `system-deep-loop` supplies paired trials and budget accounting.
- **What 019 established vs left open.** The OOB GLM research proposed typed handoff but explicitly said the proposal was not implementation-ready and required evidence that one handoff costs less than one clarification (`001-research/008-oob-glm-parallel-research/research/research.md` §4 and §11). The deeper no-wrong-door child leaves acceptable latency, byte cap, recovery yield, failure amplification, fencing, idempotency, and lease policy open (`001-research/009-oob-idea-deep-dives/004-no-wrong-door-handoff/spec.md` §7).
- **Research-loop output.** A paired task corpus spanning read-only, local mutation, and external-effect cases; a cost/risk model; loop-amplification falsifiers; and a policy defining when to clarify, hand off, or stop.
- **Scope, effort, payoff.** Six to eight iterations, multi-model with independent destination evaluation. High payoff: it decides whether recovery can safely replace some front-loaded routing precision and default behavior.

### 7. Measure named-default, null/defer, and clarify behavior at low signal

- **Question.** On naturally low-signal requests, what are the correct-selection base rate, wrong-route cost, clarification burden, and abandonment rate for named defaults versus null/defer versus one-turn clarification?
- **Skills involved.** `sk-prompt` is the one current multi-mode hub with a named `defaultMode` (`prompt-improve`); the other six multi-mode hubs use `null`. `system-skill-advisor` determines whether those hub-local policies are reached.
- **What 019 established vs left open.** The default-mode research settled the architecture—typed no-destination, bounded clarification, destination-local authority—but says the one-turn/three-option/two-attempt/token caps are provisional and advisor confidence lacks a representative held-out corpus (`001-research/007-default-mode-policy-research/research/research.md` §6). Its §12 asks for a privacy-reviewed corpus measuring risk, coverage, latency, option recall, and user friction. The OOB GLM child asks for the observed base rate of correct defaults and frames `defaultMode` as potentially compensating for weak recovery (`001-research/008-oob-glm-parallel-research/research/research.md` §8 and §11).
- **Research-loop output.** A stratified low-signal corpus, counterfactual replay across the three policies, cost-weighted outcomes, and a per-hub recommendation on keeping a named default or preferring bounded non-route behavior.
- **Scope, effort, payoff.** Six to eight iterations, multi-model. High payoff: it replaces policy intuition with evidence while avoiding the already-settled question of whether default-on compiled routing should ship—it already did.

### 8. Decide whether the learning overlay ever earns promotion

- **Question.** How much correction telemetry, from which trusted sources, and with what measured gain is required before a hub should move from `P=static` to a promoted correction overlay? Are any fields beyond vocabulary→destination learnable safely?
- **Skills involved.** `system-skill-advisor` and every multi-mode hub produce corrections; `system-deep-loop` evaluates gains; `system-spec-kit` owns retention/privacy/approval concerns.
- **What 019 established vs left open.** The unified synthesis makes the base complete with `overlay=null`, says the overlay may never be worth its conceptual weight, and states that only real correction volume plus demonstrated gain can settle it (`001-research/010-unified-refactor-research/unified-refactor-synthesis.md` §11–§13). The implementation remains dormant: no production correction corpus, operational gain, live approval, or production privacy program exists (`015-router-unification-program/010-learning-overlay/implementation-summary.md` “Known Limitations”). The spec’s §10 leaves other learnable fields, privacy/retention, and whether any hub accrues enough volume unresolved. The originating GLM lineage says new modes should use static vocabulary as a prior and “graduate” only after enough traffic (`001-research/008-oob-glm-parallel-research/research/lineages/glm-oob/iterations/iteration-004.md` F4).
- **Research-loop output.** A correction-source trust model, poisoning/privacy threat model, power analysis for minimum sample volume, offline pilot on one high-volume hub, gain/concentration/rollback gates, and a “promote, stay static, or abandon overlay” decision.
- **Scope, effort, payoff.** Eight to ten iterations, multi-model. High potential payoff, but lower immediacy than calibration because `P=static` is already safe and complete.

### 10. Classify safe speculative reads and preparation effects

- **Question.** Which nominally read-only or preparation operations are safe before destination verification, and which destinations can provide durable idempotency, compensation, atomic commit, or only weaker delivery semantics?
- **Skills involved.** `mcp-code-mode` and `mcp-tooling` expose external reads/effects; `sk-git` exposes local and remote mutation classes; `sk-design` exposes design transports; `system-spec-kit` supplies authority and audit constraints.
- **What 019 established vs left open.** The default-mode study warns that read-only speculation can still disclose data, cost money, or observe mutable external state, and asks which operations are safe plus how stale proofs/duplicate commits resolve (`001-research/007-default-mode-policy-research/research/research.md` §6 and §12). The proof-carrying child asks which effect classes may run during preparation and which remote operations offer idempotency or compensation (`001-research/009-oob-idea-deep-dives/007-proof-carrying-commit/spec.md` §7). The unified synthesis leaves the idempotency-ledger model and destination PREPARE/COMMIT capability open (§11 question 5).
- **Research-loop output.** An effect taxonomy; adapter capability matrix; privacy/cost/mutability tests; failure-injection scenarios; and a normative boundary for `inspect`, `prepare`, `commit`, and `compensate`.
- **Scope, effort, payoff.** Seven to nine iterations, multi-model with domain-specific adversarial review. Medium-high payoff: it determines where proof-carrying routing can be used beyond document-only or local cases.

### 12. Resolve lifecycle, support, and shared-resource semantics

- **Question.** Can the fleet adopt one semantic model for selection leaves, lifecycle leaves, fallback-only support, navigation indexes, and truly shared preambles without inflating typed precision or inventing route ownership?
- **Skills involved.** `sk-code` has universal preambles; `sk-prompt` has `_index.md`/`pattern_index.md`; `system-code-graph` and `system-spec-kit` explicitly separate selected leaves from support/navigation resources; `system-deep-loop` scores the result.
- **What 019 established vs left open.** The sk-code study recommends but does not implement a non-routable shared-owner contract and asks whether existing owners suffice or a new schema is required (`001-research/011-sk-code-routing-research/research/research.md` §5 and §12). The sk-prompt study identifies index and pattern-index files as lifecycle leaves but leaves whether they belong in expected pairs or telemetry to future evidence (`001-research/006-sk-prompt-routing-research/research/research.md` §5 and §12).
- **Research-loop output.** A cross-package taxonomy; decision table for typed selection versus telemetry/support; schema alternatives; replay experiments showing precision/recall effects; and a recommended fleet contract.
- **Scope, effort, payoff.** Five to seven iterations, multi-model. Medium payoff: it removes recurring ambiguity in typed-resource measurement without reopening already-settled manifest identity.

## Genuinely new territory surfaced by 019

### 6. Test benchmark external validity and overfit

- **Question.** How well do authored route-gold and typed fixtures predict routing on unseen natural prompts, paraphrases, negative requests, and different executor models?
- **Skills involved.** `system-deep-loop` owns the benchmark; `sk-doc` owns fixture authoring rules; `system-skill-advisor` supplies fleet-level recommendations; all hubs provide test strata.
- **What 019 established vs left open.** The advisor numbers came from a frozen filesystem projection and may differ from live daemon behavior (`001-research/002-skill-advisor-routing-research/research/research.md` §6). The sk-code baseline had no sealed holdouts and cannot prove generalization (`001-research/011-sk-code-routing-research/research/research.md` §6). The unified synthesis explicitly claims no production corpus or performance/calibration evidence (§13), and the later calibration corpus says its live summary is synthetic and below operational sample floors (`015-router-unification-program/008-calibration/001-holdout-corpus/implementation-summary.md` “Known Limitations”). No 019 child asks whether the benchmark itself predicts the deployment distribution.
- **Research-loop output.** A blinded external-validation corpus; paraphrase and adversarial generation protocol; inter-rater gold agreement; cross-model and cross-runtime results; overfit diagnostics by hub; and a decision on which benchmark claims remain decision-grade.
- **Scope, effort, payoff.** Eight to ten iterations, multi-model with strict separation between corpus authors and evaluators. High payoff: it validates—or bounds—the evidence foundation used by angles 2 and 4.

### 9. Model cross-hub judgment and transport composition

- **Question.** How should a judgment workflow and a transport workflow represent role, order, authority, and failure when they execute across separate processes or machines?
- **Skills involved.** `sk-design` supplies judgment; `mcp-tooling` supplies Figma/Refero/Mobbin-style transports; `cli-external-orchestration` supplies process boundaries; `system-spec-kit` supplies approval and authority semantics.
- **What 019 established vs left open.** The sk-design research defined WIRE/READ/RUN composition and said implementation must prove judgment-plus-transport bundle behavior (`001-research/003-sk-design-routing-research/research/research.md` §8 and §12). The unified synthesis then made the unresolved step explicit: how cross-hub judgment approvals such as `sk-design → mcp-figma` cross process/machine boundaries (`001-research/010-unified-refactor-research/unified-refactor-synthesis.md` §11 question 8). No child investigated real distributed composition.
- **Research-loop output.** A role-bearing route graph; ordering and authority invariants; paired cross-process scenarios; timeout/retry/partial-result failure matrix; and a contract recommendation distinguishing composition from accidental multi-hub collision.
- **Scope, effort, payoff.** Seven to nine iterations, multi-model. Medium-high payoff: it is the missing bridge between correct local routing and safe composed execution.

### 11. Measure the operational value of compiled serving for singleton packages

- **Question.** For N=1 packages, does authoritative compiled serving produce enough observability, safety, latency, or maintenance benefit to justify promotion from shadow-only operation?
- **Skills involved.** `mcp-code-mode`, `sk-git`, `system-code-graph`, `system-skill-advisor`, and `system-spec-kit` are the five singleton packages in the current fleet survey.
- **What 019 established vs left open.** The unified synthesis proves N=1 structural degeneracy—empty selection/bundle/handoff collections and `overlay=null`—but does not prove operational benefit (`001-research/010-unified-refactor-research/unified-refactor-synthesis.md` §5 and §12). The compiler packet keeps `mcp-code-mode` shadow-only (`015-router-unification-program/004-compiler-n1-shadow/spec.md` executive scope). The other four non-hub implementation summaries explicitly say they grant no live authority or serving-process evidence (`015-router-unification-program/012-non-hub-rollout/001-sk-git/implementation-summary.md`, `002-system-code-graph/implementation-summary.md`, `003-system-skill-advisor/implementation-summary.md`, and `004-system-spec-kit/implementation-summary.md`, “Known Limitations”).
- **Research-loop output.** Paired legacy/compiled measurements for latency, failure modes, observability, policy freshness, and operator burden; a benefit model for candidate cardinality one; and a promote/stay-shadow recommendation per package.
- **Scope, effort, payoff.** Five to seven iterations, one primary model plus an adversarial reviewer. Medium payoff: it prevents structural elegance from becoming an unjustified runtime surface.

### 13. Measure HVR value before authorizing a repo-wide sweep

- **Question.** Does HVR cleanup measurably improve human comprehension, editing accuracy, LLM retrieval/routing, or task success enough to justify per-occurrence review across the existing documentation corpus?
- **Skills involved.** `sk-doc` owns HVR; `sk-prompt` can construct controlled prompt variants; `system-skill-advisor` and `system-spec-kit` provide routing/retrieval outcome surfaces.
- **What 019 established vs left open.** The documentation-quality program deliberately declined a blind sweep. Its reconciled context records roughly 4,601 Markdown files and about 79,000 em-dash occurrences requiring per-occurrence judgment (`.opencode/specs/sk-doc/019-skill-routing-refactor/016-documentation-quality-program/context-index.md` “Deferred at closeout (phase 009), then actioned in phase 010”). Phase 010 leaves whether a dedicated sweep is worth doing to the operator (`016-documentation-quality-program/010-deferred-code-and-checker-fixes/spec.md` §9). That is a cost estimate and deferral, not evidence of benefit.
- **Research-loop output.** A stratified sample of untouched prose; blinded human comprehension/editing study; paired LLM retrieval and task-success evaluation; false-positive/style-damage audit; extrapolated labor and benefit estimates; and a go/no-go or targeted-slice recommendation.
- **Scope, effort, payoff.** Four to six iterations, multi-model or blinded human/model evaluation. Medium decision value at low research cost; it can prevent a very large stylistic project with unproven outcome value.

## Top 3 to run first

1. **TRP fleet falsification.** It is the only explicitly planned 019 deep dive that was never run, is bounded to five-to-seven iterations, and can invalidate or simplify several later designs.
2. **Operational advisor calibration.** The advisor’s `0.82` confidence floor is demonstrably saturated and non-probabilistic; a natural-traffic calibration run has the highest direct routing-risk payoff.
3. **Causal leaf-use telemetry.** Without successful, ordered, attributable reads, leaf-recall optimization remains partly proxy-based; this run unlocks a trustworthy two-tier selector experiment.

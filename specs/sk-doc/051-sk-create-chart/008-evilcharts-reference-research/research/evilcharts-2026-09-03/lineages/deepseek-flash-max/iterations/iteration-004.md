# Iteration 4: What Makes It Beautiful — the physical specifics

## Focus

The concrete drawing decisions that make evilcharts output read as designed rather than plotted: grid and axis weight, tick density, label placement, colour ramps, opacity and layering, corner radius, plot padding, hover/focus, empty state, first-paint animation — each measured against the corpus's `daily-line.html` and the shared primitives.

## Findings

**F4-1. evilcharts draws everything thin; the corpus draws everything thick.** Series strokes are 0.8px (`STROKE_WIDTH = 0.8`); the hover cursor is 0.8px dashed `"3 3"`; the grid is dashed `"3 3"` and horizontal-only; there are no axis lines and no tick lines (`tickLine = false`, `axisLine = false`). The corpus draws a 2px line, 1px solid full-width gridlines, and a 5px emphasis dot. The corpus's own comment says "A hairline is the right weight here" while the stylesheet sets `stroke-width: 2`. **F4-2. The area fill fades to the baseline in evilcharts; the corpus fills flat.** evilcharts area fills are white-overlay vertical fades (`stopOpacity 0.1 → 0` top to bottom), so the fill is strongest at the line and dissolves toward the axis; a "solid" variant holds 0.1 throughout. The corpus's `.area` is a flat `fill-opacity: 0.10` of the series colour. [SOURCE: context/evilcharts/src/registry/charts/recharts-area-chart.tsx:766-768] [SOURCE: context/evilcharts/src/registry/charts/recharts-area-chart.tsx:817-820] [SOURCE: ../../../../../../.opencode/skills/sk-doc/sk-create-chart/assets/templates/daily-line.html:60]

**F4-3. Single-series strokes are gradients, not flat colours.** Every line's stroke is painted from a horizontal `linearGradient`; with one colour it is flat, with two or more the series sweeps through its colour range left-to-right. The gradient comes from the config's slot colours, so it stays inside the theme. The corpus paints every stroke flat. [SOURCE: context/evilcharts/src/registry/charts/recharts-line-chart.tsx:785-809] [SOURCE: context/evilcharts/src/registry/charts/recharts-line-chart.tsx:354]

**F4-4. The intro reveal is a 1-second wipe that moves stroke and dots together.** The default is `left-to-right`, duration 1s, ease `[0, 0.7, 0.5, 1]` (fast start, long soft landing), implemented as an SVG mask whose rect scales 0→1; `right-to-left`, `center-out` and `edges-in` are variants. Recharts' built-in line animation is disabled because it desynced dots from line. The corpus has zero animation; contract rule 13 already anticipates animation with a `prefers-reduced-motion` fallback, and rule 12 bans randomness and clocks, not animation. [SOURCE: context/evilcharts/src/registry/charts/recharts-line-chart.tsx:59-60] [SOURCE: context/evilcharts/src/registry/charts/recharts-line-chart.tsx:726-779] [SOURCE: context/evilcharts/src/registry/charts/recharts-line-chart.tsx:361-364] [SOURCE: ../../../../../../.opencode/skills/sk-doc/sk-create-chart/references/template-contract.md:166]

**F4-5. Selection dims at 0.3 opacity; hover keeps a dashed trace.** With a series selected, every other series drops to `stroke/dot opacity 0.3`; the tooltip cursor is a 0.8px dashed vertical line; the hovered point gets the `ActiveDot`. The corpus has no selection or hover affordance anywhere (iteration 1). [SOURCE: context/evilcharts/src/registry/charts/recharts-line-chart.tsx:542-548] [SOURCE: context/evilcharts/src/registry/charts/recharts-line-chart.tsx:486-500]

**F4-6. The corpus's tick and label decisions are already the designed ones.** The 4-rung ladder with the `1 1.25 1.5 2 2.5 3 4 5 6 8 10` step set, the first/last anchored labels, the every-seventh-day thinning, the one formatter for every number, and the em dash for missing readings are deliberate and are not improved by anything evilcharts does (evilcharts leaves density to Recharts' `minTickGap = 8`). This direction needs no change. [SOURCE: ../../../../../../.opencode/skills/sk-doc/sk-create-chart/assets/templates/daily-line.html:134-144] [SOURCE: ../../../../../../.opencode/skills/sk-doc/sk-create-chart/assets/templates/daily-line.html:197-206] [SOURCE: context/evilcharts/src/registry/charts/recharts-line-chart.tsx:415-417]

**F4-7. The empty state is the corpus's real gap, and its own notice pattern is the mechanism.** evilcharts has no empty state; it has a loading state instead (a state where the figure deliberately shows no data), which is rejected for a static file (iteration 1). The corpus already has the right device for "the figure must say something when it cannot draw": the in-figure notice (`1 day has no reading and is left out of the line`). An empty data block currently draws an empty figure — the manual-testing playbook has a test named "a chart that draws nothing" that exists precisely because this is a live failure mode. [SOURCE: context/evilcharts/src/registry/ui/recharts-chart.tsx:131-144] [SOURCE: ../../../../../../.opencode/skills/sk-doc/sk-create-chart/assets/templates/daily-line.html:191-195] [SOURCE: ../../../../../../.opencode/skills/sk-doc/sk-create-chart/manual-testing-playbook/corpus-integrity/a-chart-that-draws-nothing.md:1]

**F4-8. Glow and endless dash animation are dashboard theatre; the corpus should reject both.** The glow filter (`feGaussianBlur stdDeviation 10` + 2x alpha boost) and the infinitely repeating dashed-stroke march (`animate` on `stroke-dasharray`/`stroke-dashoffset`, 1s loop) only read well on a screen that is being watched. A delivered document is read, printed and screenshotted; an infinite animation makes two screenshots of one file disagree, which is the exact failure rule 12 exists to prevent. [SOURCE: context/evilcharts/src/registry/charts/recharts-line-chart.tsx:812-831] [SOURCE: context/evilcharts/src/registry/charts/recharts-line-chart.tsx:685-704]

**F4-9. Plot padding and aspect are already sane on both sides.** evilcharts container forces `aspect-video` (16:9) with `initialDimension 320x200`; the corpus uses fixed viewBoxes around 720×292 (≈2.47:1) with explicit LEFT/RIGHT/TOP/BASE margins. The corpus's wider aspect suits its caption-style layout; no change recommended. ## Reflection

- **What worked**: Measuring stroke weights, opacities and durations as numbers turned "beautiful" into a diff; the corpus's own comment (hairline) and playbook test (draws nothing) made two recommendations corrections of the corpus's own intent rather than imports.
- **What failed / ruled out**: Glow and endless dash rejected on rule-12 grounds; tick/label machinery confirmed as already-designed; loading state already rejected in iteration 1, its *idea* (figure states) harvested for the empty-state recommendation.
- **Follow-up**: R4-4's settle-time acceptance test needs an operator nod if the review workflow screenshots during the first second; R4-1 and R4-2 are the highest-leverage "why it looks plain" fixes and must lead the synthesis.

    refactor(repo-rules): rename three rules, ban the em dash, brief the next program
    
    Renames three rules so the filename says what the rule is about:
    hub-routing to skill-hub-routing, root-cause to root-cause-and-debugging,
    overengineering to prevent-overengineering. References, symlinks and titles
    follow in the router, AGENTS.md, the authoring mode and the three downstream
    repositories. The new names contain the old ones as substrings, so this was
    checked for double-prefixing rather than assumed.
    
    Historical spec artifacts keep the old names. They record what was true when
    they were written, and rewriting them would falsify that.
    
    Removes every em dash from the rule set and the router, 167 across four
    repositories. A mechanical swap damaged six sentences, so those were repaired
    by hand: five list-introducing dashes became colons or parentheses, and the
    subordination line became two sentences rather than a three-comma splice. That
    line is asserted verbatim by the anatomy contract, so the contract and the
    template were corrected to match rather than left describing a line that no
    longer exists.
    
    communication.md gains a punctuation section carrying the ban, with the
    reasoning that a dash usually hides a decision the writer has not made, and a
    pointer to the full Human Voice Rules standard in sk-doc rather than a copy.
    
    Adds the six-stream brief for the next program under scratch, split into a goal
    and a binding rules file after a fresh review found the single-file version
    would have had four concurrent agents writing the same nine hub-root files and
    running a repo-global git stash on a shared dirty tree.

It is eligible when `score >= 2 * threshold` [SOURCE: specs/system-skill-advisor/host-integrations/003-pi-skill-orchestrator-based-research-refinement/016-pi-context-decisions-based-research-refinement/context/pi-context-decisions-main/src/apps/skill-filter.ts:23]. Eligible skills are ranked and sliced to `maxSkills` [SOURCE: specs/system-skill-advisor/host-integrations/003-pi-skill-orchestrator-based-research-refinement/016-pi-context-decisions-based-research-refinement/context/pi-context-decisions-main/src/apps/skill-filter.ts:25]. The defaults are cap 12 and inventory floor 8 [SOURCE: specs/system-skill-advisor/host-integrations/003-pi-skill-orchestrator-based-research-refinement/016-pi-context-decisions-based-research-refinement/context/pi-context-decisions-main/src/settings.ts:75], and below the floor filtering is skipped as `below-minimum` [SOURCE: specs/system-skill-advisor/host-integrations/003-pi-skill-orchestrator-based-research-refinement/016-pi-context-decisions-based-research-refinement/context/pi-context-decisions-main/src/apps/skill-filter.ts:155]. The decision call defaults to `failMode: "closed"` with an 8,000 ms timeout [SOURCE: specs/system-skill-advisor/host-integrations/003-pi-skill-orchestrator-based-research-refinement/016-pi-context-decisions-based-research-refinement/context/pi-context-decisions-main/src/settings.ts:67]. A configuration error forces fail-closed even when fail-open is set [SOURCE: specs/system-skill-advisor/host-integrations/003-pi-skill-orchestrator-based-research-refinement/016-pi-context-decisions-based-research-refinement/context/pi-context-decisions-main/src/apps/skill-filter.ts:170-171]. The normalized default is 0.375, which is raw 0.75 [SOURCE: specs/system-skill-advisor/host-integrations/003-pi-skill-orchestrator-based-research-refinement/016-pi-context-decisions-based-research-refinement/context/pi-context-decisions-main/docs/benchmarks/relevance-choice-vs-score.md:5].

2. **The advisor's pass rule is a two-bar gate on a floored pseudo-confidence (confirmed from code).** A recommendation passes when `confidence >= confidenceThreshold && uncertainty <= uncertaintyThreshold` [SOURCE: .skilled/skills/system-skill-advisor/runtime/lib/scorer/fusion.ts:1468-1470]. Confidence is a ramp (`0.52 + 0.43 * liveNormalized`), lifted to hard floors of 0.82 for task intent, direct score and read-only routes, pinned to 0.72 when only the derived lane carries the score, and capped at 0.95 [SOURCE: .skilled/skills/system-skill-advisor/references/scoring/advisor-scorer.md:114-130]. Uncertainty is stepped by evidence count (0.42/0.30/0.22/0.18), plus 0.08 when confidence is below 0.8 [SOURCE: .skilled/skills/system-skill-advisor/runtime/lib/scorer/scoring-constants.ts:209] [SOURCE: .skilled/skills/system-skill-advisor/runtime/lib/scorer/fusion.ts:509-522]. The hook bench pins the render thresholds at 0.8/0.35 [SOURCE: .skilled/skills/system-skill-advisor/runtime/bench/hook-brief-signal-noise.bench.ts:28]. Inferred: 0.8/0.35 is also the default of `resolvedConfidenceThreshold()` and `resolvedUncertaintyThreshold()` [SOURCE: .skilled/skills/system-skill-advisor/runtime/lib/scorer/fusion.ts:72-73]. I did not read those resolver bodies, and reading them would confirm it.

3. **Both scores are rubric indices, not probabilities. The advisor's floors hide differences in evidence the way the extension's expectation does (inference).** The benchmark shows argmax labels and thresholded expectations disagreeing on eligibility for 2-15 of 240 verdicts per model, because one number cannot express a three-way distribution [SOURCE: specs/system-skill-advisor/host-integrations/003-pi-skill-orchestrator-based-research-refinement/016-pi-context-decisions-based-research-refinement/context/pi-context-decisions-main/docs/benchmarks/relevance-choice-vs-score.md:32] [SOURCE: specs/system-skill-advisor/host-integrations/003-pi-skill-orchestrator-based-research-refinement/016-pi-context-decisions-based-research-refinement/context/pi-context-decisions-main/docs/benchmarks/relevance-choice-vs-score.md:233]. The advisor's 0.82 floor does the same: many differently evidenced skills land on one value. `ambiguity.ts` already concedes this by dropping floored members from the confidence-margin cluster [SOURCE: .skilled/skills/system-skill-advisor/runtime/lib/scorer/ambiguity.ts:29-32] [SOURCE: .skilled/skills/system-skill-advisor/runtime/lib/scorer/ambiguity.ts:72-75]. [INFERENCE: based on the floor table and the ambiguity exception] The lesson is to read uncertainty, not the confidence value, as the separating signal. A floored count per recommendation in the brief diagnostics would show how often the floor decides the pass. What would confirm it: count `confidenceFloored === true` among passing top-1s over the golden prompts.

4. **A hosted per-skill score as a replacement loses on the advisor's job (confirmed from benchmark data plus inference on fit). REJECT as replacement.** At the default policy, both arms kept about 10 of 30 skills for tasks needing 3-4, with precision 0.39 and recall 1.00 [SOURCE: specs/system-skill-advisor/host-integrations/003-pi-skill-orchestrator-based-research-refinement/016-pi-context-decisions-based-research-refinement/context/pi-context-decisions-main/docs/benchmarks/relevance-choice-vs-score.md:173]. The cap, not the threshold, decided 38 of 80 runs [SOURCE: specs/system-skill-advisor/host-integrations/003-pi-skill-orchestrator-based-research-refinement/016-pi-context-decisions-based-research-refinement/context/pi-context-decisions-main/docs/benchmarks/relevance-choice-vs-score.md:17]. One model scored almost every skill near 1 and kept 12 on the unrelated control [SOURCE: specs/system-skill-advisor/host-integrations/003-pi-skill-orchestrator-based-research-refinement/016-pi-context-decisions-based-research-refinement/context/pi-context-decisions-main/docs/benchmarks/relevance-choice-vs-score.md:200]. The policy is built for high-recall advertising, where a miss is costly and an extra listing is cheap. The advisor brief pushes one route, or an ambiguous pair, into every prompt [SOURCE: .skilled/skills/system-skill-advisor/runtime/lib/scorer/ambiguity.ts:83]. For that job precision at top-1 is what matters, and the measured precision is the policy's weak side. [INFERENCE: based on the benchmark precision and the advisor's top-1 pass contract] Latency also misses our hook budget. Measured picker time ranged 254-5,704 ms [SOURCE: specs/system-skill-advisor/host-integrations/003-pi-skill-orchestrator-based-research-refinement/016-pi-context-decisions-based-research-refinement/context/pi-context-decisions-main/docs/benchmarks/relevance-choice-vs-score.md:124], while the OpenCode hook times out at 3,000 ms [SOURCE: .skilled/skills/system-skill-advisor/runtime/bench/hook-brief-signal-noise.bench.ts:28]. Every call also sends the prompt off the machine [SOURCE: specs/system-skill-advisor/host-integrations/003-pi-skill-orchestrator-based-research-refinement/016-pi-context-decisions-based-research-refinement/context/pi-context-decisions-main/docs/skill-picking.md:33].

5. **Where a model score could win: a bounded shadow or tie-break lane on ambiguous clusters only (inferred). ADAPT.** On the 30-skill demo, four of five models gave needed skills median scores of about 1.85-2.0 and non-required skills about 0.07-0.35, a separation lexical lanes cannot get on paraphrase [SOURCE: specs/system-skill-advisor/host-integrations/003-pi-skill-orchestrator-based-research-refinement/016-pi-context-decisions-based-research-refinement/context/pi-context-decisions-main/docs/benchmarks/relevance-choice-vs-score.md:200]. The advisor already has a shadow seam: a `semantic_shadow` lane whose weight is zeroed when not live [SOURCE: .skilled/skills/system-skill-advisor/runtime/lib/scorer/fusion.ts:363-368], and a shadow sink under `runtime/lib/shadow/` [SOURCE: .skilled/skills/system-skill-advisor/runtime/lib/shadow/shadow-sink.ts:1]. Proposed change: off the hook path only (daemon or async), score just the members of an ambiguity cluster with a decision model through the existing `cli-jev` path, and log the verdict to the shadow sink without changing the brief. Benefit: cluster size is usually 2, so the call is small, and shadow logs measure whether it would have resolved ties correctly. Cost: about $0.0002-0.0018 per call at benchmark prices [SOURCE: specs/system-skill-advisor/host-integrations/003-pi-skill-orchestrator-based-research-refinement/016-pi-context-decisions-based-research-refinement/context/pi-context-decisions-main/docs/benchmarks/relevance-choice-vs-score.md:132-146], plus prompt egress. Risk: calibration is per model (finding 6), so a shadow verdict needs its own threshold before it can ever be promoted. What would confirm it: a shadow-log run over the golden prompts' ambiguous rows, scoring tie-break accuracy against the gold label.

6. **One global threshold is wrong across models, and the extension says so itself (confirmed).** The post-hoc sweep puts the best floor anywhere from about 1.0 to 1.75 depending on the model. For one model no needed skill reaches 1.5 [SOURCE: specs/system-skill-advisor/host-integrations/003-pi-skill-orchestrator-based-research-refinement/016-pi-context-decisions-based-research-refinement/context/pi-context-decisions-main/docs/benchmarks/relevance-choice-vs-score.md:297]. For the same Jev model our `cli-jev` path targets, F1 rises from 0.40 at the default to 0.88 at 1.75 [SOURCE: specs/system-skill-advisor/host-integrations/003-pi-skill-orchestrator-based-research-refinement/016-pi-context-decisions-based-research-refinement/context/pi-context-decisions-main/docs/benchmarks/relevance-choice-vs-score.md:283]. The document closes by asking for held-out labeled tasks per model, rubric and version [SOURCE: specs/system-skill-advisor/host-integrations/003-pi-skill-orchestrator-based-research-refinement/016-pi-context-decisions-based-research-refinement/context/pi-context-decisions-main/docs/benchmarks/relevance-choice-vs-score.md:298]. Any model lane in the advisor would need a per-model threshold keyed to model id and version, never a shared constant. Verdict for copying the extension's default 0.375 into the advisor: REJECT, because it was never calibrated, not even for the extension's own default.

7. **Benchmark method worth copying: negative controls, a required/acceptable split, and policy-pinned resumable raw data (confirmed for the extension, gap on our side inferred). ADAPT.** The extension benchmark fixes labels before any live run. It separates `required` from `acceptable` adjacent skills, so a reasonable adjacent pick is not an error. It includes an unrelated control where any pick is a false positive [SOURCE: specs/system-skill-advisor/host-integrations/003-pi-skill-orchestrator-based-research-refinement/016-pi-context-decisions-based-research-refinement/context/pi-context-decisions-main/docs/benchmarks/relevance-choice-vs-score.md:83-92]. It also refuses to resume a raw file whose recorded policy or inventory hash differs [SOURCE: specs/system-skill-advisor/host-integrations/003-pi-skill-orchestrator-based-research-refinement/016-pi-context-decisions-based-research-refinement/context/pi-context-decisions-main/docs/benchmarks/relevance-choice-vs-score.md:66-68]. The advisor's calibration bench is stronger on calibration itself: reliability buckets, ECE and a Brier/ECE baseline-tolerance gate [SOURCE: .skilled/skills/system-skill-advisor/runtime/bench/scorer-calibration.bench.ts:145-168] [SOURCE: .skilled/skills/system-skill-advisor/runtime/bench/scorer-calibration.bench.ts:270]. The extension has none of that. Proposed change: add an explicit abstain metric to our routing benchmarks (the rate at which a no-skill prompt yields a passing recommendation), plus an `acceptable` set per golden prompt so adjacent hits are scored apart from wrong routes. Benefit: the false-positive surface the extension shows is its weak spot gets measured on ours. Cost: label work on the golden corpus. Risk: labels drift without a pinned inventory hash. UNKNOWN: whether `routing-golden-prompts.vitest.ts` already carries expected-null rows. I did not read its fixtures, and reading them would decide whether this is new work or a reporting change.

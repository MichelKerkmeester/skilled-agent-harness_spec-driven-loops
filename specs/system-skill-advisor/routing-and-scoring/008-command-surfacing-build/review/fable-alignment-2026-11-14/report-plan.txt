**020-command-offer-fit-guards.** Level 2, 16 tasks. It keeps the model's free choice where no command fits. A command the prompt only mentions, refuses or asks about is not offered. **021-brief-head-parity.** Level 2, 13 tasks. One request gets one head on every runtime. The handler gives the first row its command whatever the caller asked for, and the OpenCode plugin leads with the row that passed. A companion command prints its policy word and is not printed twice. It fixes A-2, A-3, A-7, the second half of B-9 and an OpenCode head that showed a raw bridge id, which the probe found. The probe printed 13 of 15 equal heads between the Claude hook and the plugin.

**022-workflow-measure-soundness.** Level 2, 18 tasks (T001 to T019, T010 unused). It makes the workflow measure count what a run did. A shell read and a Pi or Codex prompt mirror count as loads, and a write outside the checkout sets no boundary. A wrong-mode load returns `otherMode` and an ask row that asked returns `asked`. A `followedRate` sits beside the unchanged `loadRate` and `share`, and both stored arms are rescored with a reason for every changed count. It fixes A-9, A-11 in two of three parts, A-12, B-2, B-7 and B-8.

**023-untuned-outcome-evidence.** Level 2, 15 tasks. It states the outcome from committed scripts and tunes nothing. It adds a stage split for the 44 sealed plain rows and 48 new sealed rows that should carry no command. The workflow summary gains a 95 percent range, a split by offer and a `--pair` option. It covers the scorer-level parts of B-1 and the measurement half of B-11, and prints the lines the new goal criterion from B-4 names.

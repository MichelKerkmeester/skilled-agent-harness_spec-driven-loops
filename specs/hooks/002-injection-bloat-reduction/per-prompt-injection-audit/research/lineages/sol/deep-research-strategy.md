## What Worked

- Tracing runtime registries into semantic owners separated adapters from canonical policy.
- Executing exported renderers/constants produced reproducible UTF-8 and UTF-16 measurements.
- Separating configured from observed lanes prevented false Cursor and provider claims.
- The iteration-14 skeptical audit corrected Gate, cache, Pi, and delivery overstatements before ranking.

## What Failed

- Broad repository grep was noisy with generated/history artifacts.
- Exact tokenizer measurement failed because vocabularies were unavailable under restricted networking.
- Warm-daemon sampling failed under sandbox IPC restrictions.
- Static source analysis cannot establish host acceptance, compaction retention, billing, or behavioral equivalence.

## Exhausted Approaches

- Further static arithmetic for provider billing or compliance.
- Inferring runtime receipt from registration or serializer completion.
- Treating provider cache as cross-turn delivery state.

## Ruled-Out Directions

- Unconditional directive drop, no-match/failure silence, or startup-only policy.
- Universal Gate first-open-only suppression.
- Numeric cache-billing savings without receipts.
- Pi 130-byte target as semantically equivalent.
- Cursor configured savings as realized.
- OpenCode `q=1`, prompt-hash-only identity, or finite whole-prompt claims.

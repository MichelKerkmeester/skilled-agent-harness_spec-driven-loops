QUESTION
What should the system inject at an EVENT, rather than at a prompt? An event is something
that just happened: work completed, a goal was met, a turn ended, a session ended, a gate
passed or failed.

WHY THIS IS OPEN, AND WHAT IS ALREADY SETTLED
A prior ten-iteration round asked whether to inject rule content keyed on the USER'S
PROMPT VOCABULARY. It refused eighteen candidates and the refusal stands. Do not
re-propose any prompt-vocabulary trigger; that question is closed.

This round asks a different question the prior round never tested. An event trigger has a
silence condition for free: the event is rare and discrete, so the hook is quiet by
default. That is exactly what every refused candidate lacked.

GROUND TRUTH — verify, then build on it, do not rediscover it
Read `.opencode/hooks/injection-contract.md` section 4 in full. Three facts matter:
- The Completion Evidence Sentinel already DETECTS when a completion claim looks
  unsupported by evidence, and injects nothing. Channel `[LOG]`. Advisory-only was an
  explicit v1 decision, not an oversight.
- Session Stop runs and injects nothing. Channel `[NONE]`.
- The goal hook already reads BACKWARD COMPOSITION at turn end, flattening the ending
  message plus tool results into evidence. That mechanism is proven and running.
So there are live detectors with their output channel switched off. Whether to switch any
on is a policy change, and one prior candidate was refused specifically for upgrading an
advisory log to an injection without consent. Treat that as a real cost, not a formality.

## 2. IDEA ONE — completion presentation
- **The trigger does not exist as an event.** "A goal was met" is emitted by nothing on any runtime. It is a 0.72-confidence heuristic verdict (`goal/README.md:46`) computed only at Pi `turn_end` (`goal-context.ts:229-231`), and it deliberately produces *silence* when met (`:233`). "Work completed" is the sentinel's tail-400-char claim regex (`completion/README.md:31`) — a heuristic, not an event, and its one model-visible instance exists to flag *unsupported* claims, not to carry presentation guidance.
- **The payload is resident and the moment is contract-owned.** `AGENTS.md:405-409` carries the communication/presenting/handoff digests with load commands; `:501` carries the close-out obligation verbatim. The rule defers at its own moment: "Where the run has its own contract for this, that contract wins" (`presenting-decisions.md:129-132`), and the deep modes' completion contracts own their reports (`AGENTS.md:464-465`).

## 3. IDEA TWO — structured question triggering
- **Detection solvable** — the reply exists at turn end and is readable on every runtime that fires one (`completion/README.md:64-69`).
- **Remedy fatal, now bounded from the outside.** Pi exposes the strongest timing surfaces that exist in any runtime: `message_end` handlers can *replace* the finalized assistant message (pi docs `extensions.md:621`), and `ui_prompt_start/end` fire around blocking user prompts (`:583-599`). Neither helps: a role-preserving replacement cannot make the model *ask*, and `ui_prompt_*` observes extension-authored prompts, not model prose. Claude's block-continuation exists in the API but is doubly disabled for these hooks — advisory-only by design (`completion/README.md:64`) and `async: true` in config (`.claude/settings.json:146`, `:152`). No runtime currently contains a surface that can reopen a finished turn for a better question.
- **The judgment is the second killer.** "Should have asked" requires the three-condition bar (`handoff-and-questions.md:101-106`: alternatives nameable; the answer changes what you do next; not resolvable from code/default) — none computable from reply text — and prose is the rule's default (`:108-110`), so a form detector fires on compliant turns.

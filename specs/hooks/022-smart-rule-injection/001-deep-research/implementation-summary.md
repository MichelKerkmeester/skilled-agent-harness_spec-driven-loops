<!-- ANCHOR:what-built -->
## What Was Built

The prompt-keyed question is settled: no rule in the corpus is injected at prompt time, and the reason is stated as a test rather than a judgment. Eighteen candidates were refused, the bar that refused them is written down once, and the one read-side obligation the resident layer does not carry is named with its promotion target.

### The Bar

A prompt-time injection earns its slot by naming a specific prohibition a gate enforces, not by restating a disposition the always-loaded document already carries. The corpus has gates; a candidate that duplicates what already loads on every turn buys nothing and trains the reader to skim.

### The Promotion Target

The delegation self-lens clause is the sole read-side obligation the resident layer does not carry. The remedy is promotion into the resident layer, not injection: a clause that must bind while reading belongs where it always loads. The promotion location and the decision are the operator's.

<!-- /ANCHOR:what-built -->

<!-- ANCHOR:how-delivered -->
## How It Was Delivered

Ten research iterations ran on DeepSeek V4.1 Flash at max thinking through `cli-pi` with no early convergence. Each candidate was tested against the gate that would enforce its prohibition and against the resident-layer text it would duplicate; the deciding test was recorded with the refusal. Claims were accepted only when their citation resolved, and the round ended with the refusal set read back against the iteration records.

<!-- /ANCHOR:how-delivered -->

<!-- ANCHOR:decisions -->
## Key Decisions

| Decision | Why |
|----------|-----|
| Refuse prompt-time injection for the corpus | No candidate named a gate prohibition the corpus did not already carry |
| State the bar once instead of judging candidates case by case | A settled test keeps the question from being re-litigated next round |
| Promote the delegation self-lens rather than inject it | A read-side obligation belongs in the layer that always loads |
| Leave the promotion to the operator | The resident layer is shared across repositories, so the edit is an operator decision |

     Pick by what the badge has to say, because a status badge is read before its label.                                                                                                                
                                                                                                                                                                                                        
     A) Amber — Earns attention, which is what a badge is for, but it reads as caution, so a state that is merely in progress will look like a problem.                                                 
     On a light surface the amber text also needs a tinted fill and dark text to stay legible at small sizes.                                                                                           
                                                                                                                                                                                                        
     B) Teal — Sits outside the red/green warning vocabulary, so it suits neutral-positive states and holds up on both light and dark themes, but readers                                               
     have no built-in meaning for it and must read the label to interpret it.                                                                                                                           
                                                                                                                                                                                                        
     C) Slate — Never competes with the real semantic colours and looks deliberate in a dense UI, but it has the least salience and can read as disabled                                                
     or inactive even when the state is live.

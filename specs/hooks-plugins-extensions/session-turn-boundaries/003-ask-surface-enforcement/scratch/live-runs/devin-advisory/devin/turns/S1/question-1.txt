 Three options, one trade-off each:

 A. Amber signals "attention needed" faster than any other hue, which suits a status badge. The trade-off is that amber also reads as a warning, so a routine state will look like something is wrong.

 B. Teal stays distinct from the green and red pair most UIs use for success and failure, so status never collides with outcome. The trade-off is low urgency, and at badge size teal can be mistaken
 for the green of a healthy state.

 C. Slate is neutral, stays legible on light and dark surfaces, and never competes with semantic colours. The trade-off is low salience, so it reads as chrome rather than status and is easy to miss.

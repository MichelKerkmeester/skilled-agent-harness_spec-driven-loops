## 1. DIAGNOSIS

## 3. THE PROPOSAL

Edit 1 — `AGENTS.md:243`, row 1, opening clause kept verbatim to keep the diff minimal:

> | **Confirmed vs inferred** | A load-bearing claim carries its evidence (file:line, command, artifact), and that evidence could have come out otherwise about the claim: a search that cannot match the target's form, a check narrower than the claim, or a match a look-alike also produces is inference — say what would confirm it. |

Edit 2 — `evidence-and-proof.md:51`, OBSERVED row, so the tier definition matches the row that compresses it:

> | **OBSERVED** | You ran it and read the output and exit status, and the claim's falsifier sits inside what you read | Cite the receipt: the command, the `file:line`, the artifact path |

The counterfactual head is iteration 4's, retained deliberately; what changes is that it becomes a **classification of when OBSERVED applies with its three shapes enumerated** — including "a check narrower than the claim," the level case the head alone leaves the reader to invent.

Against the test set: **#1** — a prefixed pattern cannot match a bare key → inference, remedy named. **#2** — a single-line pattern cannot match a wrapped require, and a one-line read is narrower than "loads" → inference. **#3** — under the amended §1 the total is not OBSERVED, so `:52` requires the derivation step, which was never shown. **#4** — a line-oriented search cannot match a wrapped fix → inference. **#5** — digits identical under both roles are "a match a look-alike also produces" → inference.

## 3. THE PROPOSAL

**Edit 1, replace `AGENTS.md:243` (one row, opening clause kept):**

> | **Confirmed vs inferred** | A load-bearing claim carries its evidence (file:line, command, artifact), and that evidence confirms it only when the claim being false would have changed what it showed. A reading that would come out the same either way is inference, not confirmation: a search that never hit, a read that stopped short, a total never re-derived, a match that never distinguished roles. An inferred one says what would confirm it. |

**Edit 2, insert after `evidence-and-proof.md:56`, closing §1:**

> **A receipt confirms only what could have contradicted it.** If the claim being false would have left the receipt unchanged, it is not an observation of that claim. Whatever was run, it belongs in INFERRED until repaired. Four shapes recur, each with one repair. A search that never hit: demonstrate it on a known instance of what you claim is absent. A read that stopped short: read the span where the failure would print. A total carried from another analysis: derive it here, at the total's own level, since verifying its parts is not deriving the whole. A match that never distinguished roles: compare what the two sides are, not the digits they share. With no known instance at hand, the honest output is INFERRED and the confirm-step, not a confident absence.

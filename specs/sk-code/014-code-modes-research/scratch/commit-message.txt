docs(spec): open the sk-code modes research packet

Open a phased research packet with two children that will study how to
make sk-code-quality and sk-code-review more repo-agnostic, more useful
and better integrated with their sibling modes and with modes a user adds
to the hub.

The children are Level 2 and the whole packet is Planned, with nothing
run. Each child holds ten planned research iterations and a ranked
recommendation synthesis, and no file under the hub changes. The scaffold
also lists the new packet in the sk-code track metadata.

Spec: sk-code/014-code-modes-research

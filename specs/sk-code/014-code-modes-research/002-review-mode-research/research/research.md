# Research: sk-code-review repo-agnostic, useful and integrated

Workflow-owned synthesis of the `/deep:research` run on `specs/sk-code/014-code-modes-research/002-review-mode-research`. The iteration records under `research/iterations/` are the evidence. This file consolidates them.

## 1. Summary

`sk-code-review` keeps a strong, portable core: its resources resolve from the packet's own root, its security and correctness minimums are unconditional, and its output contract is checked by scripts. What ties it to this framework is narrower than it looks. It has one hard-coded positive surface marker (`.skilled/`), one cache key that needs an `origin` remote, one fixed sensitive-path list, and examples that name `.skilled/` paths. What limits its usefulness is evidence: its benchmark scores routing, not finding accuracy. What limits its integration is the absence of named handoffs: it has no reader for the quality gate's advisory envelope, a two-value surface label, and no documented payload contract for a user-authored mode.

The highest-gain change is one repo-owned review profile at a framework-neutral path that may only add or raise, never relax, plus an explicit list of the floors no extension may change. Both are ranked first and second in section 6.

A second model family re-checked the ranked rows in iterations 11 to 15. It confirmed the facts behind 9 of the 12 rows, refuted the named risk in rank 7 and left the decisive claim of ranks 2 and 10 untested. A replay after today's router change shows that a path-only workflow review still routes without the CI surface, so rank 12 is now a confirmed defect rather than a replay request. Section 6a holds the verdicts and section 6b the refreshed order.

Evidence labels: OBSERVED (read in a file and cited), DERIVED (follows from two or more OBSERVED facts), INFERRED (judgment or proposal, with what would confirm it). Source tags were checked against the files at the end of the run. Every `sk-code-review` and `sk-code-quality` path cited here was byte-identical to baseline commit `f8671e07c2a7ae00cda7ba2a2d30b192d5928606` throughout the run. Hub files outside those two folders (`ROUTER.md`, `hub-router.json`, the hub `SKILL.md`, `shared/references/stack-detection.md`) were edited by concurrent CI wiring work during the run, so their tags below carry current line numbers and a quoted fragment.
